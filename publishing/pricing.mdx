Configure base price, market overrides, sale prices, and pre-order discounts in the Partner Center **Pricing & availability** module.

## Pricing concepts

- **Base price tier** — anchors the price across all markets.
- **Market overrides** — per-region price tier exceptions.
- **Scheduled sales** — time-windowed discounts with start / end dates.
- **Pre-order pricing** — separate price for the pre-order window.
- **Bundles** — discount the bundle vs. sum of contents.

## Currency conversion

Microsoft maintains a price-tier table per market. Pick a tier rather than entering raw amounts — the system handles VAT / sales tax computation.
