Every XBOX product ships with a **licensing mode** that governs which sign-in and entitlement flows Microsoft will allow. There are two modes: **Open** and **Restrictive**.

## Modes

| Mode | Behavior | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open licensing** | Any Microsoft account can install and run the product if they have the entitlement (purchase, promo code, Game Pass, family library). | The default for retail titles. |
| **Restrictive licensing** | Only accounts on an allowlist (specific test accounts, specific Xbox Insider audiences, specific accounts you enumerate) can install and run. | Private betas, review copies of unreleased titles, closed pre-launch soft launches, industry demos. |

## Selecting a mode

Licensing mode is set per product in **Game setup → Licensing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Open at product creation for retail titles">
    Almost every commercial retail product should be Open licensed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Restrictive for closed pre-launch">
    If you're distributing a build to a controlled audience (reviewers, closed beta, industry event demos) use Restrictive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move from Restrictive to Open at launch">
    Restrictive titles must transition to Open licensing before the product goes on sale to the general public. This is a metadata change that publishes through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Interaction with visibility and audiences

Licensing mode is orthogonal to [Visibility & audiences](/publishing/how-to-visibility):

- A **hidden token-only** product is Open licensed but only reachable via promo code.
- A **Restrictive** product can be visible or hidden — independent axis.
- A public soft-launched title is Open licensed but audience-scoped to a specific region.

Use Restrictive licensing when you need to enumerate who can play, not just who can see the page.

## Cert implications

Restrictive-licensed titles still pass Certification against the applicable XRs. Some XRs (for example those covering the retail purchase flow) do not apply while the title is Restrictive; Microsoft's test team handles this automatically based on the declared licensing mode. When you flip to Open, a fresh cert pass validates the retail flows.
