The **Store listings** module is where you write per-language store copy, upload screenshots and trailers, and define localized features.

## Required assets per market

- Product title (localized).
- Short description (200 chars).
- Description (4000 chars).
- Hero art, screenshots (at least 3), trailer (recommended).
- Localized feature list.

## Best practices

- Localize for every market you ship in — the system falls back to English but conversion suffers.
- Trailer thumbnails matter — the first frame is what people see in the store.
- Refresh assets ahead of campaigns (sales, expansions, anniversaries).
