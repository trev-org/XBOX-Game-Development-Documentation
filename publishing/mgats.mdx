**Microsoft Gaming Accessibility Testing (MGATS)** is an optional advisory test pass performed by Microsoft's accessibility team. The output is a detailed accessibility report on your build plus, for titles that request it, the public **Accessibility features** callouts on the Store listing.

## What MGATS covers

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Vision** | Colorblind safety, contrast ratios, text scaling, screen reader / narrator support, motion sickness options. |
| **Hearing** | Subtitle presence and quality, closed captions for non-speech audio, visual alternatives for critical audio cues. |
| **Motor** | Fully remappable controls, hold-to-toggle options, aim / turn assist, difficulty and assist modes, Xbox Adaptive Controller support. |
| **Cognitive** | Difficulty options, save frequency, checkpointing, tutorial replay, clear objective communication. |
| **Speech** | Speech-to-text and text-to-speech, alternatives to voice-chat requirements. |

## What you get

- A **detailed report** listing every issue observed, severity, and specific repro steps.
- Guidance on remediation — not just "this failed" but concrete design and implementation suggestions.
- On request, **Accessibility features** entries on your Store listing that are verified by Microsoft (rather than self-declared), which some customers filter on.

## What MGATS is not

- **Not a cert gate.** MGATS results do not block Final Cert. It's advisory.
- **Not a legal accessibility audit.** MGATS is not a substitute for regulatory accessibility compliance (for example the European Accessibility Act) that your legal team is responsible for.
- **Not a substitute for internal accessibility work.** MGATS is most valuable when you've already done a first pass and want expert review.

## When to submit

- **8–12 weeks before launch** if you want time to address findings and re-verify.
- **Ahead of a major content update** that materially changes accessibility surface area (new game mode, new tutorial, new UI system).

## Submission

MGATS is submitted through Partner Center as a submission type distinct from Final Cert or Optional Cert. You provide the build, a description of the accessibility features you've implemented, and any specific areas you want the team to focus on. Standard turnaround is **7–14 business days**.
