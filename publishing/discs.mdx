Physical XBOX discs sit on top of your digital Microsoft Store release. Everything you need for the digital release still applies — packages, certification, ratings, Store listing — plus a separate contractual, packaging, and manufacturing workflow for the disc SKU.

<Warning>
  Discs require an **executed disc addendum** with Microsoft on top of your standard publisher agreement. If your product does not show a **Discs** module in Partner Center, contact your Microsoft representative — the addendum is not yet in place.
</Warning>

## The disc SKU compared to a digital SKU

| Concern | Digital | Disc |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract | Standard publisher agreement | Publisher agreement **\+ disc addendum** |
| Certification | Final Cert on the base package | Final Cert on the base package **\+ Disc Cert on the exact disc image** |
| Age ratings | IARC issuance covers most regions | May also need **physical longform ratings** — see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings) |
| Artwork | Store listing screenshots and hero art | Print-ready front / back / spine per region |
| Manufacturing | None | Replicator lead time typically 4–8 weeks after RTM |
| Update flow | Package update via [Packages](/publishing/packages) | Same digital update flow, plus disc release requires reprint if disc image itself needs to change |
| Royalty | Digital revenue share per your agreement | Per-disc royalty tier submitted in Partner Center |
| Reversibility | Delist any time | Once you RTM, discs are pressed — you cannot recall in-market inventory |

## Disc image essentials

- **Format** — Xbox Optical Disc (BD-XL). Current generation ships on **BD-100** (Series X) or **BD-50** (Xbox One / cross-gen).
- **Locked layout** — the streaming install order and chunk manifest on the disc must exactly match what you certify.
- **Offline boot** — the disc must boot to a playable state with no internet connection. Parts of the game that require the day-one update must fail gracefully with a clear update-required message.
- **Day-one update** — planning a day-one update is normal, but the fully-cert-approved offline experience is what determines whether the disc passes cert.

## End-to-end disc workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm contract">
    Verify the disc addendum is executed and the Discs module is enabled in Partner Center for your product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan disc SKUs and regions">
    In [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup) declare the disc SKU. Confirm the region SKU plan with your Microsoft representative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Produce and validate the disc image">
    Build the disc-layout package using the same toolchain as your digital release. Validate the manifest, install order, and offline boot experience locally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Produce packaging artwork">
    Per-region artwork with correct rating icons, legal marks, and platform lockup. See [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for Disc Certification">
    In parallel with Final Cert on the digital SKU, submit the disc image for Disc Cert. See [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit ratings and royalty tier">
    Confirm physical longform ratings (see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings)) and submit royalty tiers (see [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="RTM">
    Sign off the disc image and artwork. See [Approve discs for manufacture](/publishing/approve-discs-for-manufacture).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order manufacturing">
    Submit per-region manufacturing quantities. Lead time is typically 4–8 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage day-one update">
    Upload and schedule the day-one update in [Packages](/publishing/packages) to publish alongside the disc street date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Street date">
    Digital and disc release publish together. Monitor early telemetry and be ready to publish a first patch via [Packages](/publishing/packages) or [Cert Bypass](/publishing/cert-bypass-program).
  </Step>
</Steps>
