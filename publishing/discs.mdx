Physical XBOX retail discs are produced through a separate **disc fulfillment** process in Partner Center, in addition to the digital Microsoft Store release.

## Disc package

- Built from your standard Game Core for XBOX package.
- Includes mandatory day-one update planning if package size exceeds the disc.
- Must pass the same [certification](/publishing/certification) as the digital build.

## Workflow

1. Plan disc package SKUs in [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup).
2. Upload the disc image alongside the digital package.
3. Coordinate with your publisher for replication and distribution.
4. Ship a day-one update via [Packages](/publishing/packages).
