## What MMOs make hard

MMOs typically have:

- A **publisher account system** the customer signs into on top of their Xbox account.
- **Always-online session state** with a publisher's own matchmaking / world servers.
- **Persistent-world chat channels** (city chat, guild chat, whisper) that span Xbox and non-Xbox players.
- **Third-party services** for account linking, analytics, cash shops.

Each of these is a cross-network concern under [XR-007](/publishing/xr/xr-007). This page is the applied guidance for MMO teams.

## Publisher account linking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Present the account link disclosure once">
    On first launch of the MMO, present a screen that discloses the publisher account system, what data will flow to it (gamertag mapping, minimal profile), and the privacy policy URL. Require an explicit acknowledgment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Support account link without account creation on the Xbox">
    Customers should be able to link an existing publisher account or create a new one, but must not be blocked from playing the game entirely because the publisher account service is down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Persist the link across sign-outs">
    Once the Xbox account is linked to the publisher account, that link should survive sign-out and re-sign-in. Do not require re-linking on every launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Support unlinking">
    Provide an in-game or web-based way for the customer to sever the account link. Follow through on the Xbox side and stop transmitting data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chat channel handling

- **World / zone chat** — typically text, sometimes voice. Respect Xbox mute list on the receiving side even for cross-network players; honor child account communication restrictions.
- **Guild / clan chat** — requires explicit customer consent to join. Honor Xbox privacy privileges at join time.
- **Whisper / DM** — must respect mute / block bidirectionally. If a customer blocks a player on Xbox, whispers from that XUID must be filtered even if the sender is on a non-Xbox platform.
- **Voice chat** — gate behind the cross-network voice privilege and Xbox voice communications privilege. Child accounts must not be able to enter voice chat with strangers.

## Persistent world = always-online

- The title must handle **XBOX network loss** without corrupting local state. See [XR-074](/publishing/xr/xr-074).
- If the publisher's server is unreachable but Xbox network is up, the title must present a **clear disconnect message** rather than freezing on the loading screen.
- If the Xbox account is signed out of Xbox Live but the publisher account is still authenticated, the title must gracefully drop the customer to a state that either re-authenticates or exits cleanly.

## Cash shop / RMT (real-money transactions)

MMOs frequently ship cash shops for cosmetics, currency, or subscription time. These are XStore commerce concerns, not third-party billing:

- Ship your cash shop as XStore add-ons in Partner Center (see [Consumable add-ons](/publishing/consumable-add-ons), [Durable add-ons](/publishing/durable-add-ons)).
- Do not send Xbox players to a third-party web checkout. Xbox platform commerce runs through XStore.
- For subscription products, coordinate with Microsoft on subscription enrollment. Publisher-hosted subscriptions billed outside XStore are typically not allowed on Xbox.
