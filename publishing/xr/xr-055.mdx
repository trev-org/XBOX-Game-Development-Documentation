**XR-055 is tested in Certification.** All games targeting Xbox consoles are required to have Achievements and meet the following requirements. Demos aren’t allowed to have achievements; however, they have the option of supporting Hero Stats.

## Requirement

Titles must ship the required number of achievements and their associated Gamerscore at launch. You can add achievements and Gamerscore at any time after launch — with or without new content — but not beyond the calendar and lifetime limits below.

- **A single achievement can’t exceed 200 Gamerscore.**
- **Every achievement in the title must be achievable.**
- Unlocking achievements in the base game or a content update must represent a thorough exploration of, or engagement with, game content.

| Item | Launch | Semi-annual additions | Lifetime limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum achievements | 10 | 0 | 10 |
| Maximum achievements | 100 | 100 | 500 |
| Gamerscore | 1000 | 1000 | 5000 |

<Note>
  “Semi-annual” means January–June and July–December. Base-game achievements and Gamerscore do not count against the semi-annual limits.
</Note>

## Test cases

### 055-01 Achievements

1. Review the total Gamerscore and total number of achievements supported by the title.
2. Play through the title and attempt to earn every achievement.
3. Repeat step 2 after resuming from connected standby.
4. Repeat step 2 while disconnected from Xbox services.

**Pass:**

- Every achievement can be earned.
- Achievements unlock only when their criteria are met.
- Achievements represent a thorough exploration of, or engagement with, game content.
- The launch build has 1000 Gamerscore spread across 10–100 achievements.

**Fail:**

- An achievement doesn’t unlock when its criteria are met, or unlocks before its criteria are met.
- Achievements can be trivially unlocked without engaging with core gameplay.
- The launch build has more or fewer than 10–100 achievements, or less/more than 1000G.
- A single achievement exceeds 200 Gamerscore.
- Achievements fail to unlock after resuming from connected standby or after reconnecting to Xbox services.
