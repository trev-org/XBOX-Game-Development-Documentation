## Intent

Xbox network services are shared infrastructure. A title that hits a service faster than it should degrades the service for every other title. XR-132 makes rate-limit respect and backoff a cert-blocking requirement.

## Applies to

Every title that uses Xbox network services (MPSD, achievements, presence, stats, social, XStore).

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Respect the **rate limits documented for each Xbox network service**.
- On `429 Too Many Requests`, **honor the `Retry-After` header** exactly. Do not retry sooner.
- Use **exponential backoff** for transient `5xx` errors.
- **Batch requests** where the service supports it.
- Cache **read-heavy responses** with an appropriate TTL.
- Avoid **hot request loops** on any service.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Retry a rate-limited request immediately.
- Fan out large numbers of parallel requests as a workaround for a batch call.
- Poll a service at a rate faster than the service's documented refresh interval.
- Use undocumented or higher-limit endpoints to bypass rate limits.

## Tested scenarios

- Force a `429` response and verify the title honors `Retry-After`.
- Verify batched calls are used where the service supports batching.
- Verify no hot loops on presence, achievements, or MPSD updates.

## Best practices

- **Instrument service call rates** in your telemetry so you can catch a runaway loop before it hits cert.
- **Use platform helper libraries** — the GDK's Xbox network helpers include rate-limit handling.
- **Coordinate across systems** — avoid two independent subsystems both polling presence at once.
