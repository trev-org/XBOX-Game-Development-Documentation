## Intent

Microsoft product names change (Xbox Live → Xbox network, Games with Gold sunset, Xbox Series X|S naming conventions). Customers rely on consistent naming to know what they're getting; Microsoft brand teams enforce consistent naming so customers aren't misled. XR-022 makes correct naming a cert-blocking requirement.

## Applies to

Every product's Store listing, in-game UI, achievements, add-on copy, and shipping marketing materials that pass through cert.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST** use current official Microsoft product names, including:

- **Xbox Series X|S** with the pipe between X and S. Not "Xbox Series X and S", not "Series X\\S", not "Xbox Series X/S".
- **Xbox One** with no space around "One". Not "Xbox 1", not "XboxOne".
- **Xbox network** (or "Xbox" as a service where appropriate). Not "Xbox Live" going forward except in historical references.
- **Xbox Game Pass** (proper case). Not "XBox Game Pass", not "Xbox GamePass".
- **Play Anywhere** where the product is enrolled. Not "cross-play", not "Xbox anywhere".
- **Microsoft Store** for the store name. Not "Windows Store", not "MS Store".
- **Windows 10** / **Windows 11** with the correct version.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Refer to competitor platforms in ways that violate Microsoft brand guidelines.
- Use deprecated Microsoft product names in shipping copy (except in a historical context and clearly labeled).
- Modify Microsoft logos or platform lockups. See [Branding assets](/publishing/branding-assets).
- Refer to the Xbox console without a generation qualifier where the generation matters.
- Claim compatibility or features ("Optimized for Series X|S", "HDR", "120 FPS") the title doesn't actually ship.

## Tested scenarios

- Walk every menu and every Store listing string. Look for old naming, wrong casing, wrong spacing.
- Verify achievement names and descriptions (localized per language) use current naming.
- Verify feature callouts on Store listing (HDR, 4K, 120 FPS, Smart Delivery) match what the title actually ships.
- Verify the title's own name matches the Partner Center reservation (see [Game name](/publishing/game-name)).

## Best practices

- **Automate a lint pass** — grep for known-bad naming across localizations and shipping strings.
- **Coordinate marketing copy** through the same lint before it goes on the Store listing or trailer.
- **When Microsoft renames a product**, update pending submissions before they hit cert if the change is announced with a grace period.
