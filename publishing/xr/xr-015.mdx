## Intent

Customer safety in multiplayer titles depends on consistent enforcement of the platform's communications controls. If a customer mutes or blocks another player on Xbox, that must extend into the title. If a customer reports a message, the report must reach Xbox network moderation. XR-015 makes these behaviors mandatory.

## Applies to

Titles with any form of customer-to-customer communication: voice chat, text chat, in-game whispers, quick-chat / voice-line systems, in-game emotes, party chat integration, cross-platform communications.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Honor the **communications privilege** (`XPrivilegeCommunications`, `XPrivilegeCommunicationsCrossNetwork*`) on the signed-in account. If the privilege is denied, communications features must be disabled.
- Honor the **Xbox mute list** — muted XUIDs must not have their voice or text reach the muting customer.
- Honor the **Xbox block list** — blocked customers cannot be added to sessions, cannot invite, and cannot communicate.
- Provide **in-game mute controls** for real-time voice / chat, in addition to platform-level mute.
- Provide a **reporting flow** so a customer can report offensive communication. The report must include enough context (timestamp, sender XUID, message) to be actionable.
- Route reports through Xbox network reporting where possible, or via a moderated publisher pipeline. Reports must not be silently dropped.
- Enforce **child account restrictions** — no unmoderated voice or text communications with strangers for accounts flagged as child.
- Enforce **communications privileges cross-network** where technically feasible (see [XR-007](/publishing/xr/xr-007)).

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Bypass mute or block via a title-owned chat system.
- Present a "mute" toggle that only mutes on the current device without persisting to Xbox network.
- Ship an unmoderated open chat channel to child accounts.
- Include chat-content data in customer-visible analytics or leaderboards.

## Tested scenarios

- Mute a player on Xbox; verify they cannot be heard in the title.
- Block a player; verify they cannot enter the same lobby / invite the customer.
- Send an offensive message from account A to account B; verify account B can report it and the report is recorded.
- Sign in a child account with restrictive family settings; verify voice / text chat is disabled or moderated.
- Verify communications privilege gates the entire feature, not just the microphone.

## Best practices

- Query and cache the mute / block list at session start; refresh it on session events.
- Consider **profanity filtering** in text chat channels as a defense in depth, especially for younger audiences.
- For **voice**, moderation typically requires transcription or clip-based reporting. Xbox network reporting supports voice clips.
- Log **only aggregated** communications data for analytics — never raw chat content.
- Provide **automated moderation** for common infractions and a **human moderation queue** for reports.
