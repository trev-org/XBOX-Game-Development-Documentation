## Intent

When a title launches from cold boot, from suspend, or from Quick Resume, it must correctly identify **who** is playing and **which controller** they're using. XR-112 defines the correct behavior across these entry points so customers don't end up controlling the wrong profile.

## Applies to

Every title.

## Requirement

On **cold boot** or **launch from home**, your title **MUST**:

- Establish the **active user** from the launching controller's associated account.
- Handle the case of **no signed-in account** — either sign in a guest, prompt sign-in, or exit cleanly.
- Associate the launching **controller** with the active user.

On **resume from suspend**, your title **MUST**:

- Verify the previously-active user is still signed in. If not, handle the account change (see [XR-115](/publishing/xr/xr-115)).
- Verify the previously-associated controller is still connected. If not, prompt for controller reconnect or accept a new controller.
- Restore in-progress gameplay state, respecting any account or controller changes.

On **Quick Resume swap-in**, your title **MUST**:

- Behave the same as "resume" — verify the active user and controller are unchanged, or handle the change.
- Not assume the network state is the same as before the swap.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Silently continue with the wrong user's profile.
- Assume a specific controller port maps to a specific user.
- Fail to detect an account change and continue writing to the previous account's save.

## Tested scenarios

- Cold boot with a single signed-in account. Verify correct activation.
- Cold boot with no signed-in account. Verify graceful sign-in prompt or guest activation.
- Suspend, sign out, resume. Verify title handles the account change.
- Suspend, disconnect controller, resume with a new controller. Verify correct reassociation.
- Quick Resume swap out; sign out; swap back in. Verify handling.

## Best practices

- **Query the active user on every activation event** rather than caching across suspends.
- **Use `XUserGetLastError`** consistently to detect sign-in state changes.
- **Provide graceful sign-in / controller-select UI** rather than crashing on missing state.
