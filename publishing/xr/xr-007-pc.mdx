## Intent

The PC variant of [XR-007](/publishing/xr/xr-007) covers cross-network data usage from the Windows and mobile surface. On PC, additional considerations apply because:

- The **Microsoft Store account** and the **Xbox network sign-in** can be different users.
- **Third-party PC services** are more common (anti-cheat SDKs, ad networks, publisher launchers).
- The **Windows privacy model** is broader than console — titles can access more of the OS than they can on Xbox.

## Applies to

PC and mobile titles that use Xbox Live / Xbox network services and exchange data with non-Xbox networks. This includes Play Anywhere titles on their PC surface.

## Requirement

On PC, in addition to the base [XR-007](/publishing/xr/xr-007) requirements, your title **MUST**:

- Honor the **Xbox Network Services data-processing addendum** — see [PC certification](/publishing/pc-certification). Reference the addendum in your privacy policy.
- Detect **mismatched Store account** scenarios and handle them without silently sending data under the wrong identity. See [XStore on PC](/publishing/xstore-pc).
- Not use **Windows telemetry APIs** to circumvent Xbox network privacy privileges.
- Not use **anti-cheat SDKs** that transmit data classified as personal information under the addendum without disclosure.
- Respect the **Microsoft Store license token** as the primary identity for entitlement checks. Do not fall through to a third-party identity when the customer expects to be signed in as their Xbox account.
- Isolate **third-party PC launcher integrations** — publisher launchers must not intercept or modify Xbox network state.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Transmit gamertag, XUID, or Xbox friend graph to a third-party service on the PC surface without disclosure in the privacy policy.
- Enable persistent third-party account creation from within an Xbox-network session on PC without customer consent.
- Use PC clipboard, screen capture, or accessibility APIs to sample Xbox network data.

## Tested scenarios

- Sign the Microsoft Store in as user A, run the game as user B. Verify the title does not silently transmit data under user A's identity.
- Verify the privacy policy referenced in Properties: Basic info covers third-party services active on the PC surface.
- Verify anti-cheat and third-party SDKs behave when the customer denies their permissions.
- Verify no OS-level telemetry API is used to bypass an in-game privacy toggle.
