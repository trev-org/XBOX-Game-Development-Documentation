## Title Ratings for Xbox

This document focuses on two essential concepts pertaining to content classifications and restrictions on Xbox consoles:

- **Rating:** Specifies the age level for which different types of content are deemed appropriate by different regions' ratings boards.
- **Parental controls:** A profile setting on Xbox consoles that defines the highest rating level of content that a user can play or view within an application.

There are 2 different means of obtaining ratings:

- **IARC** (International Age Rating Coalition) covers many markets when distribution is digital-only. Participation is free at the point of use and publishers complete a questionnaire in Partner Center to generate a rating.
- **Rating certificates** are required for many markets when distribution is via physical disc. Also referred to as obtaining 'long-form' or 'traditional' ratings.

### What are ratings?

Ratings are subjective classifications applied to titles or applications, based on:

- **Content:** violence, drug use, coarse language, nudity, or sexual activity.
- **Functionality:** social features, privacy, modes of interacting with social networks, loot boxes, reward systems, or other pressures to play.
- **User-generated content:** the nature of content accessible through the application created by other users and any restrictions applying to it.

### Content policies

Microsoft has store policies that prohibit offensive, pornographic, illegal, and other types of inappropriate content. Review both the current [content policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/store-policies#content-policies) and any specific exclusions in your licensing agreement with Microsoft.

### Multigeneration releases

When releasing your game simultaneously on multiple console generations (Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S) or adding a generation later, process may vary by ratings board and region. Contact the appropriate age rating authority directly for specific questions.

- Xbox Series X|S is considered a new platform by most ratings authorities, requiring a separate ratings classification.
  - Unchanged content: most authorities classify via their additional platform process (fees may apply).
  - Materially changed content: most authorities require a full submission (fees may apply).
- Partners are responsible for ensuring all required age rating certificates are provided via Partner Center.

### About IARC

Administered by many of the world's game rating authorities, IARC provides a globally streamlined age classification process for digital games and mobile apps. See the [Age Ratings](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/uwp/publish/age-ratings) page for implementation details or the [IARC website](https://www.globalratings.com/about/) for more info. Challenge IARC rating decisions via the link provided within Partner Center when the IARC rating is generated.

### Setting your rating in your product

All ratings are set during ingestion in Microsoft Partner Center. Ratings elements should **not** be declared in the AppXManifest; as of May 11, 2020, doing so is cause for certification rejection. Content updates for existing titles that already have ratings declared in the AppXManifest can remain unchanged, or you may remove the ratings element completely.

### How ratings are pulled

- **Xbox digital store:** pulled from the age ratings module in Partner Center (IARC or manually overridden with uploaded certificates).
- **Physical disc:** overridden long-form ratings are pulled from Partner Center at time of submitting a disc request. Only overridden ratings are added to the disc.
- **AppXManifest:** declaring ratings within the AppXManifest is no longer permissible.

### Console ratings logic

- If the console is **online**, store ratings take priority over any ratings on the disc.
- If the console is **offline** and the AppxManifest has no ratings element, the console uses ratings on the disc image (from catalog.js pulled from Partner Center).
- If the console is **offline** and the AppxManifest has the ratings element but some/all ratings are missing, disc image ratings are ignored and the game is treated as unrated in those regions.
- If the console is **offline** but was previously online when the game was installed, cached ratings from the digital store are used (refreshed every 10 days).

### Ratings terminology

**Rating:** the high-level rating for the game. Xbox consoles map parental controls to these ratings and prevent users from accessing higher-rated content.

**Rating descriptors:** specific details about game substance of particular interest to parents/guardians.

**Parental controls:** a setting on Xbox consoles that defines the rating level of games a user can play or view.

**Trailers/videos:** videos playable within the Xbox console UI.

**Game add-on:** content built by the publisher (DLC) downloadable through the Xbox network. Downloading the add-on alone does not allow access; the underlying game is required.

**Catalog listing (store listing, GDP/PDP):** the "wrapper" for a game on the Xbox Store — ratings, art assets, localized titles/descriptions, features.

**Demos, betas, and trial experiences:** limited-functionality releases of a portion of a game, distributed digitally to drive purchase intent.

## Ratings setup guidance

### Pre-orders

Pre-orders for digital-only products display IARC-generated ratings. Pre-orders for products intended for physical release must display the rating from the respective board's certificate. If unavailable, the approach varies by rating authority — e.g., ESRB requires an RP rating (at the correct tier); PEGI allows an IARC-generated PEGI rating with a "Provisional" designation.

### Betas/Game previews

Betas and Game Preview program titles are digital-only. IARC should be used in supported markets. For non-IARC regions, certificates are required.

### Demos

Digital-only regions display IARC-assigned ratings. Physical release regions must display the rating from the respective board's certificate; if not obtained, select rating pending.

<Warning>
  Any **free product** (including betas or demos) rated as CERO Z or IARC 18\+ is **not allowed to release on Xbox in Japan** regardless of the distribution method.
</Warning>

Demos should follow the rating of the main title. If the main game is RP, the demo should be as well, and match the final rating once assigned.

### Game trailers

Only UNRATED trailer needs to be uploaded to Partner Center. Microsoft adds the necessary age ratings 'pre-roll' to the trailer.

<Note>
  This exception is only for Partner Center/Microsoft Store PDP. Any trailer that is not destined for the Microsoft Store product page must still display embedded rating information, where required, per the appropriate authorities' guidelines.
</Note>

Any game trailer showcasing CERO Z or IARC 18\+ rated gameplay is **not allowed to release on Xbox in Japan** regardless of distribution method.

#### Requirements for downloadable content

If a **game add-on** (Xbox console DLC) required for base gameplay has a different rating than the core game, the publisher must obtain an updated rating from ESRB and submit it with the content. If the DLC rating is higher than the core game, the core game rating must be increased to match. If the game add-on is optional with different rating/content descriptors, the new rating information must be communicated to consumers where it will be accurately associated with the downloadable content.

#### Ratings process for code to content (C2C) cards

If the game will be sold at brick-and-mortar stores, the publisher must obtain a ratings certificate from the ESRB.

#### Requirements for music game add-ons

Game add-ons for music games must contain the following text:

- **English:** (Online Music Not Rated by the ESRB)
- **French:** (Musique en ligne non classée par l'ESRB)
- **Spanish:** (Música en línea sin clasificación por parte de la ESRB)

#### Restrictions for game trailers

The ESRB considers trailers to be advertising material:

> **No advertisement should contain any content that is likely to cause serious or widespread offense to the average consumer.**

Trailers with excessive violence, blood/gore, headshots, or chainsaw kills may be rejected regardless of consistency with the overall title.

#### Rating requirements for demos and betas

Demo/betas must display the appropriate ESRB Rating Pending logo and/or Rating Pending Notice at 50% vertical height for four seconds prior to demo start.

<Note>
  Demos/betas with the ESRB logo can only be released in the US and Canada. Rating information is not required on-screen if it's clearly displayed on the webpage/digital storefront and visible before the user accesses or downloads the demo.
</Note>

#### Rating certificate expirations

ESRB may require re-rating 10 years after original certificate for continued updates (major update, new platform, new bundle). Applies only to games rated after April 1, 2018.

#### PEGI Provisional

Used for unrated games that will acquire a long-form PEGI rating for full release. Must be replaced by the final long-form rating once acquired.

#### New PEGI Rating Criteria (2026)

As of June 2026, PEGI introduced new criteria for online interactive risks:

- **Purchases of in-game content:** time/quantity-limited offers → PEGI 12. NFTs or blockchain → PEGI 18.
- **Paid random items:** default PEGI 16 (some cases PEGI 18).
- **Play-by-appointment:** reward mechanisms (daily quests) → PEGI 7. Punishment mechanisms → PEGI 12.
- **Safe online gameplay:** entirely unrestricted communication (no blocking/reporting) → PEGI 18.

More info: [PEGI news](https://pegi.info/news/pegi-expands-age-rating-criteria-interactive-risk-categories).

#### Certificate Requirements

Rating certificate required at submission for disc distribution. Digital-only content may obtain a rating via IARC unless rated 19, which then requires GRAC.

When releasing on multiple Xbox platforms (Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Windows), one certificate suffices provided content is the same.

#### Age Validation and I-PIN

Since December 2012, all Xbox users in Korea must validate their age using I-PIN or equivalent as part of profile setup.

#### 19\+ IARC-generated GRAC ratings

If the IARC-generated GRAC rating is 19\+ the title is **automatically blocked from the Xbox Store in Korea** until a traditional GRAC certificate is obtained and uploaded.

#### Enforcement and fines

Fines up to KRW 20,000,000 or penal servitude of two years per violation.

#### Guideline on the disclosure of probability information

Games offering "probabilistic items" must disclose the probability of obtaining items and adhere to the [Guideline on the Disclosure of Probability Information](https://www.grac.or.kr/board/Inform.aspx?searchtype=004&type=view&bno=823&searchtext=).

## Certification Test Cases

### 017-01 Age Rating Validation

<Note>
  For digital only titles or for testing without green discs, refer to test case BVT 000-13.
</Note>

**Configuration:**

1. Title must be a disc submission and test discs must be available.
2. The Submission Materials (Certificates)
3. Title's age ratings as declared in Microsoft Partner Center

**Test steps**

1. Compare ratings values in Partner Center against ratings certificates in submission materials. Each ratings value should have a corresponding certificate.
2. TEST DISCS REQUIRED — Insert disc into a dedicated offline console set to RETAIL and install the title.
3. For each ratings body supported: set the console Location within Settings, sign into an Xbox profile with the corresponding age restriction, and launch the title.

**Expected result**

With a console Location matching each supported age-rating region:

- The title must challenge the user when the console age rating is set lower than the title's certificate value.
- The title must boot without challenging when the age rating is equal to the certificate value.

### BVT-13 Age Rating Validation

**Configuration:**

1. Title must be Final (Base or Content Update).
2. Submission Materials available for review.
3. Access to product age ratings in Microsoft Partner Center.

**Test steps**

1. Verify these documents/information are available: Submission Validator log for the submission's XVC, age rating declarations in Partner Center, age rating certificates.
2. Open the Submission Validator log and ensure no warnings/failures for the `Manifest-Ratings` component test.
3. Review the product's age rating information in Partner Center. For each value, verify a corresponding rating certificate has been provided.

**Expected result**

1. All files must be supplied and relevant to this submission type.
2. All ratings must have a corresponding certificate.
3. Base final submissions after May 11, 2020 must not have an age ratings element in AppxManifest.
4. Content updates for titles certified before May 11, 2020 must not add or remove ratings from AppxManifest except to completely remove the element.
