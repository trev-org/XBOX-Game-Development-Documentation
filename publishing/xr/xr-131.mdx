## Intent

Xbox platform capture (Game DVR, screenshots) is a customer-controlled surface every title must support. XR-131 ensures capture works across the title's declared display modes (HDR, 4K, 120 FPS, VRR) without corrupting the game or producing unusable captures.

## Applies to

Every title.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Support **Xbox platform capture** during gameplay in every declared display mode.
- Support **HDR capture** for HDR titles — captured HDR frames must be usable.
- Support **120 FPS capture** where declared — the capture surface must handle the higher frame rate.
- Support **screenshots** at the title's rendered resolution.
- Not disable capture during customer-visible gameplay unless the title has a specific reason (competitive integrity in a ranked match may be an exception with disclosure).

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Crash or corrupt frame output during platform capture.
- Disable capture entirely without customer disclosure.
- Fail to update HDR metadata during capture on HDR titles.

## Tested scenarios

- Capture a Game DVR clip during gameplay in each declared display mode. Verify the clip is playable.
- Capture a screenshot in each mode. Verify the image is correct.
- Attempt to capture during a mode you disable (e.g. cutscene). Verify graceful handling.
