## Intent

Every Xbox account has a set of **privileges** that gate what network features the customer is allowed to use: multiplayer, voice, text, cross-network, UGC, sharing, video capture. Privileges reflect the customer's Xbox subscription, their family settings, and their region. XR-045 makes privilege checking a mandatory pre-condition for the corresponding feature.

## Applies to

Every title that uses Xbox network features that map to a privilege. This includes almost all multiplayer titles, titles with UGC, titles with communications, and titles with sharing / capture integrations.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Check the **applicable privilege** on the signed-in account before enabling the corresponding feature. Common privileges include:
  - `XPrivilegeMultiplayerSessions` — basic multiplayer.
  - `XPrivilegeCommunications` — text and voice chat.
  - `XPrivilegeCommunicationsCrossNetworkVoice` — cross-network voice.
  - `XPrivilegeCrossNetworkMultiplayer` — cross-platform multiplayer.
  - `XPrivilegeUserCreatedContent` — access to or creation of UGC.
  - `XPrivilegeSharing` — share via Xbox social.
  - `XPrivilegeVideoCommunications` — video chat.
- **Re-check privileges** when the signed-in account changes or when the platform signals a privilege change.
- **Gracefully degrade** when a privilege is denied — present a clear message that the feature is unavailable due to Xbox account settings, not silently no-op.
- Route privilege-denial recovery through Xbox's own privilege-request UI (`XPrivilegeGetPrivilegeCheckResultOrDenyReason` and follow-through) so parents / family managers can resolve.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Enable a feature and only then check the privilege.
- Cache privilege state across sign-in changes.
- Bypass a denied privilege by routing through a title-owned network path.
- Present a denied privilege as an application error — it's an account state, not an error.

## Tested scenarios

- Sign in an account with `XPrivilegeCommunications` denied. Verify chat is disabled.
- Sign in a **child account** with restrictive family settings. Verify multiplayer, chat, and cross-network are all correctly gated.
- Deny cross-network privilege mid-session. Verify the title responds appropriately.
- Attempt to bypass a privilege via a publisher account layer. Verify the title still respects Xbox privileges.

## Best practices

- Check privileges **at the gate** before showing the feature UI, not deep in the code path.
- Use `XPrivilegeCheckPrivilegeWithUI` where the customer can be prompted to resolve the denial (e.g. request family-manager approval).
- Document your **privilege dependency matrix** — which features require which privileges, so QA can plan test accounts.
- Consider child accounts a **primary test case**, not an edge case.
