## Intent

Customers have three ways their identity can appear in a title: **gamertag** (public), **real display name** (subject to privacy privilege), and **gamerpic** (image). All three come from Xbox network services and must be handled per the customer's privacy settings. XR-046 defines the rules.

## Applies to

Every title that displays the customer's Xbox identity or the identity of other players.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Retrieve **gamertag** via the Xbox social APIs. Gamertags include modern gamertag suffixes (e.g. `PlayerName#1234`) — render the full modern gamertag correctly.
- Retrieve **real display name** only if the customer's privacy privilege permits it. If not permitted, fall back to gamertag.
- Retrieve **gamerpic** via the profile service. Cache with a short TTL and refresh appropriately.
- Honor privacy privileges on **other players' identities**. If Player B has restricted their real name from Player A, Player A must not see Player B's real name.
- **Refresh identity data** when the customer changes gamertag, gamerpic, or privacy settings mid-session.
- Render gamertag with the **correct character set** — modern gamertags support Unicode characters in some locales.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Persist a customer's real name locally in a way that bypasses privilege changes.
- Show real names when the privilege has been denied.
- Cache gamerpic beyond the platform-recommended TTL.
- Replace gamerpic with a title-specific default without customer consent.
- Truncate the modern gamertag suffix in a way that could create identity collisions.

## Tested scenarios

- Change gamertag mid-session. Verify the title updates.
- Deny real-name privilege for Player B. Sign in Player A. Verify Player A sees only Player B's gamertag, not real name.
- Change gamerpic mid-session. Verify refresh.
- Sign in a customer with a Unicode gamertag. Verify correct rendering.

## Best practices

- **Query gamertag on every login**, cache with a short TTL, refresh on account-change events.
- **Use the platform gamerpic** rather than a title-specific avatar unless the title is explicitly avatar-based.
- **Handle Unicode-aware rendering** — truncation must not break mid-codepoint.
- **Store XUID as the durable identity**, not gamertag. Gamertags can change.
