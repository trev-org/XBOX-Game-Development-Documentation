After an achievement is published to users, it can’t be removed, nor can its unlock rules or rewards be changed.

**Permitted modifications:**

- Achievement text strings — name and description.
- Achievement art — icons and background.

**Not permitted:**

- Removing a published achievement.
- Changing the unlock criteria for a published achievement.
- Changing the Gamerscore or any other reward tied to a published achievement.
