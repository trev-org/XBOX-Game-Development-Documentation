## Intent

Every XBOX title must remain **stable and responsive** during normal gameplay and during every platform-driven state transition. Customers rely on titles to survive suspend / resume, controller changes, sign-in changes, network transitions, and Quick Resume swaps without crashing, hanging, or losing data. Stability is the foundational XR — a title that trips XR-001 is almost always blocked from publish regardless of how well it satisfies other XRs.

## Applies to

- Every retail submission on every device family.
- Every recert and content update.
- Every disc submission.
- Also enforced (with slightly narrower scope) in [Beta and Game Preview](/publishing/cert-beta-game-preview) submissions.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Not crash to the dashboard, hang, or produce a black / frozen screen under any tested scenario in the cert suite.
- Recover cleanly from suspend and resume, including after long idle windows (multiple hours).
- Handle Quick Resume swap-in and swap-out without corrupting game state, save data, or platform-visible state.
- Survive sign-in state changes: account sign-out mid-session, account switch, guest account activation and removal.
- Survive controller state changes: controller disconnect, secondary controller join, Xbox Adaptive Controller connect.
- Handle network state transitions: full network loss, partial packet loss, DNS failure, and reconnection without user-visible corruption.
- Handle storage transitions: install / uninstall of the shipping title's own updates, add-ons, or DLC while the title is running (see [XR-123](/publishing/xr/xr-123)).
- Complete a full title lifecycle (launch → gameplay → exit) without leaked memory that would trigger platform-level OOM.
- Not produce a state where the customer must hard-reset the console to recover.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Ship with unhandled asserts, debug break-in-debugger paths, or heap corruption present in retail builds.
- Leave the console in a state that requires a factory reset to recover from a title crash.
- Continue running with corrupted save data or corrupted XBOX services state.

## Tested scenarios

The cert suite exercises XR-001 through a broad set of scenarios. Common test cases:

- **Cold boot** — launch from a cold system, from a warm system, from suspend, and from Quick Resume.
- **Suspend / resume** — Home button, dashboard switch, sign-in change, background download, sleep.
- **Extended suspend** — leave the title suspended for a multi-hour window; resume and continue.
- **Quick Resume swap** — swap the title out via another title's Quick Resume; swap back in.
- **Sign-out mid-session** — sign out the active account during gameplay; verify no crash and no data loss for the account's progress.
- **Controller disconnect** — disconnect the primary controller during gameplay; reconnect a different controller.
- **Network loss** — pull the network mid-session in three modes: online multiplayer, downloading content, and idle in a menu.
- **Storage full** — fill available storage; verify the title fails gracefully rather than crashing.
- **DLC install during play** — install an add-on while the title is running (see also [XR-123](/publishing/xr/xr-123)).
- **Rapid state transitions** — alternate suspend / resume repeatedly for stress.
- **Localization sweeps** — launch the title in every declared language / locale; each launch must be crash-free.
- **Long soak** — leave the title running for the cert-specified soak window (multiple hours) with no crashes.

## Best practices

- **Instrument crash reporting** — collect stack traces from your test builds so intermittent crashes can be root-caused.
- **Test on real hardware, not just devkits** — retail hardware behaves differently than devkits under thermal load.
- **Cover Series S memory constraints** — Series S has substantially less memory than Series X; many XR-001 failures come from Series S OOMs that Series X hides.
- **Handle every callback path** — XBOX platform callbacks (services, controllers, users) can fire at any time; every path must be safe.
- **Timebound network calls** — unbounded waits on Xbox network calls are a common source of hangs.
- **Free resources on suspend** — the platform may reclaim GPU or memory resources while your title is suspended; hold no assumptions across suspend.
- **Validate save writes** — corrupted saves that crash on load are XR-001 failures.
