## Intent

Customers buy DLC, redeem promo codes, join a family group, or start a Game Pass subscription while a title is running. Their new entitlement should apply to the running session without requiring the customer to close and reopen the title. XR-123 requires titles to detect and honor entitlement changes mid-session.

## Applies to

Every title with DLC, add-ons, consumables, or Game Pass-only content.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Subscribe to platform events that signal **entitlement changes**: `XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost`, `XStoreQueryAddOnLicensesAsync` result changes.
- Re-query entitlements at **natural break points**: opening the shop UI, returning to the main menu, before starting a mode that requires an entitlement.
- Grant access to content on **entitlement acquisition** without requiring the customer to restart.
- **Install DLC packages during gameplay** (for `durable with package` add-ons). The platform downloads the package; the title mounts and consumes it when install completes.
- Handle **entitlement loss** gracefully — refund, family sharing removal, subscription end. Lock the affected content on next reference; do not corrupt current session state.
- Refresh **consumable balances** when a purchase completes so the customer sees their new balance without restart.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Cache entitlements across a session so a mid-session purchase requires restart.
- Ship the customer a locked-down UI that says "restart to unlock" when the platform doesn't require it.
- Continue granting access to content after an entitlement is revoked.
- Crash on an entitlement change.

## Tested scenarios

- Start the title without a specific add-on. Purchase the add-on. Verify access appears without restart.
- Redeem a promo code mid-session. Verify entitlement.
- Simulate a family-sharing change that revokes an entitlement mid-session. Verify graceful behavior.
- Subscribe to Game Pass mid-session. Verify Game Pass-only features unlock.
- Purchase a consumable. Verify the balance refreshes in the in-game shop.

## Best practices

- **Query entitlements on shop-open, mode-select, and title-resume** — the three natural refresh points.
- **Handle the license-lost event immediately** — lock content, log the event, present UI at the next appropriate break.
- **For durable-with-package** DLC, listen for the install-complete event and mount the package when ready.
