## Intent

Store listing assets are what customers see before they buy. They must **accurately represent** the shipping product (no bullshots passed off as gameplay, no CG-only screenshots claimed as in-engine), meet the Microsoft Store's technical specifications, and comply with regional legal marks. XR-006 is where visual claims become legally binding for consumer protection.

## Applies to

Every product with a Store listing: base games, add-ons, bundles, and pre-order products.

## Requirement

All Store listing assets **MUST**:

- **Accurately represent** the shipping product. Trailers marketed as "gameplay" must show gameplay footage. CG must be labeled.
- Meet the current **hero art** specification (aspect ratio, resolution, safe zones, no burned-in age-rating icons or Store branding).
- Provide the required **minimum screenshot count** (typically 3, target 8\+). Every screenshot must be in-game content captured at supported resolution.
- Meet **trailer** requirements (codec, resolution, frame rate, audio levels). Trailers must not include unlicensed music or third-party IP.
- Include correct **age rating icons** on the Store listing for every market. For disc SKUs, include physical **longform ratings** where required (see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings)).
- Include **required legal marks** for licensed content (©, ™, ®) where applicable.
- Match the **product name** reserved in Partner Center (see [Game name](/publishing/game-name)). No renaming between Store listing and shipping title.
- Match the **Store listing copy** to the shipping product's genre, features, and modes (see [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing)).

Store assets **MUST NOT**:

- Include competitor platform lockups (PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam) without approval.
- Include Xbox logos or marks used incorrectly. See [Branding assets](/publishing/branding-assets).
- Include content above the declared age rating (an M-rated screenshot on an E-rated title fails XR-006 and [XR-017](/publishing/xr/xr-017)).
- Include unlicensed likenesses, unlicensed music, or unlicensed IP.
- Include placeholder text (`Coming Soon`, `TBD`) in customer-facing copy.

## Tested scenarios

- Verify hero art meets current spec sheet: dimensions, safe zone, no logo burn-in.
- Verify minimum screenshot count. Verify each screenshot is captured at the target platform resolution.
- Verify trailers play cleanly on retail Xbox and PC. Verify audio isn't clipping.
- Verify age rating icons for every market. For disc, verify physical rating icons on packaging (see [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging)).
- Verify Store listing copy is complete for every declared language.
- Cross-reference Store screenshots against shipping build — no material differences.

## Best practices

- **Export screenshots losslessly** from the game engine — don't re-encode from a compressed capture.
- **Capture on retail hardware** at the target-platform resolution. Series X 4K captures don't automatically look right on Series S surfaces.
- **Have a copy editor review localized listings** — machine-translated Store copy is a common XR-006 failure.
- **Verify legal clearances** for any music, likenesses, or IP shown in trailers before submission.
- **Update Store listing screenshots** when a content update materially changes the game (new mode, new UI); customers rely on the PDP being current.
