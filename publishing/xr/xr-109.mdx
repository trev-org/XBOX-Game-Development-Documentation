## Intent

Titles occasionally launch other apps — open the Microsoft Store PDP for the base game to prompt a DLC purchase, launch the Xbox app for a friend view, hand off to a companion app on a phone. These transitions must use the platform's supported app-linking mechanisms so the customer arrives at the intended destination and can return.

## Applies to

Titles that launch external apps, deep-link into the Store, or coordinate with companion experiences.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Use the **platform-supported app-linking APIs** (protocol activation, `LaunchUri`, XStore-provided links) rather than opening web URLs that require the customer to navigate.
- Handle the case where the target app is **not installed** — present a clear message rather than failing silently.
- Return the customer to a **sensible state** when they navigate back from the launched app.
- Not link out to **third-party stores or apps** in a way that competes with Microsoft's commerce surface.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Force a browser to open for what should be a native platform link.
- Deep-link into a Store product other than one associated with the title / publisher.
- Fail without a graceful fallback if the target app is missing.

## Tested scenarios

- Launch the Store PDP for a DLC. Verify the customer arrives at the PDP for the correct product.
- Simulate the Xbox app not being installed. Verify graceful fallback.
- Return to the title after opening the Store. Verify the title resumes correctly.

## Best practices

- Prefer **`XStoreShowProductPageUIAsync`** to open a product PDP rather than a generic URL.
- Cache and use **XStore product IDs** for deep-links; don't hard-code Store URLs.
