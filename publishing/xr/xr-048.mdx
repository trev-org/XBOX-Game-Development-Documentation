## Intent

Customers set Xbox profile preferences — preferred language, region, content filters, display preferences — and expect titles to honor them. XR-048 makes those settings authoritative at title runtime.

## Applies to

Every title.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- Read the customer's **preferred language** from the profile and default the title to that language if it's a declared supported language (see [Supported languages](/publishing/supported-languages)).
- Read the customer's **region** and use it to drive market-specific behavior (currency in in-game store, region-restricted content, network routing).
- Read **content filters** (e.g. family content settings) and gate mature content accordingly. Child accounts with strict content filters must not receive mature content.
- Refresh settings when the customer changes them mid-session.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Override profile-declared language with a title-only preference without customer consent.
- Ignore content filters on child accounts.
- Use a different region for pricing than the customer's declared region.

## Tested scenarios

- Sign in with a preferred language different from the console UI language. Verify the title defaults to the profile language.
- Sign in a child account with restrictive content filters. Verify mature content is not shown.
- Change region mid-session. Verify pricing and market-specific content refresh.

## Best practices

- Load the preferred language at title start, but allow the customer to override in-game.
- Respect content filters at the earliest gate; do not load or download mature content the customer has filtered out.
