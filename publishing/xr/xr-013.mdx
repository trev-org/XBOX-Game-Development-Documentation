## Intent

Many titles link the Xbox / Microsoft account (MSA) to a publisher-owned account so the customer can carry progression across platforms. Linking creates a durable identity relationship and, done badly, can leak PII, prevent sign-in, or trap customers into an unwanted account association. XR-013 defines the contract for how MSA ↔ publisher account linking works safely.

## Applies to

Any title that maintains a publisher-owned account, subscription, or identity outside of Xbox Live and links it to the signed-in Xbox account.

## Requirement

Your title **MUST**:

- **Disclose the link** at the point of linking. The customer must see, in plain language: what publisher account this is, what data will flow, and where to find the privacy policy.
- Require **explicit customer consent** for the link. Passive linking based on which account happens to be signed in is not allowed.
- Allow **unlinking** — from within the game or via a web endpoint the customer can reach. Unlinking must revoke ongoing data flow and remove the association.
- Handle **MSA sign-out gracefully** — if the customer signs out of Xbox mid-session, the linked publisher session must either safely re-authenticate or exit; it must not continue running with mismatched identity.
- Handle **MSA switch** — if the customer switches accounts, the publisher session tied to the previous account must be closed cleanly. Do not silently rebind the publisher account to the new MSA.
- Handle **publisher account issues gracefully** — if the publisher account service is down, the customer must still be able to launch and play the offline portion of the title (unless the title is exclusively online).
- Not require the customer to create a publisher account to play the offline / single-player parts of the title, unless that account is explicitly the primary account model.

Your title **MUST NOT**:

- Silently link the currently signed-in MSA to a publisher account created earlier without asking.
- Sell or share the MSA / publisher account link with third parties outside your privacy policy.
- Use the MSA gamertag or XUID as a public identifier in the publisher account UI without disclosure.
- Prevent the customer from unlinking or from using the title with a fresh account.

## Tested scenarios

- Launch the title with an account that has never been linked. Verify the link flow is disclosed and explicit.
- Sign out of Xbox mid-session with a linked publisher account. Verify graceful behavior.
- Switch to a different Xbox account. Verify the previous publisher session ends cleanly.
- Attempt to unlink from in-game or via web. Verify data flow stops.
- Play the offline portion without linking (where applicable). Verify no forced link.

## Best practices

- Make the link flow **skippable** for as long as possible. The customer should be able to play offline / trial / demo without linking.
- Use a **QR code / short link** flow for web-based linking rather than in-game credential entry — credential-entry-in-game is a common phishing vector.
- Persist the link so **customers don't have to re-link on every launch**. Store the mapping server-side, not just client-side.
- **Log unlink events** so customer support can validate an unlink actually took effect.
