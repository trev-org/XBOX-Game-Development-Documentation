Bundles are not immutable — you can add, remove, or swap constituent products after a bundle is published. But there are rules, especially for bundles with existing customers.

For the concept of bundles see [Bundles](/publishing/bundles). For creating a new bundle see [Create products](/publishing/how-to-create-products).

## What you can change

| Change | Allowed? | Effect on existing owners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add a product** to the bundle | Yes | Existing owners **automatically receive** the entitlement to the added product. |
| **Remove a product** from the bundle | Yes, with caveats | Existing owners **retain** the entitlement to the removed product. New buyers don't get it. |
| **Swap a product** | Yes | Removed product's entitlement stays with existing owners; new product's entitlement is granted to existing owners. |
| **Change the price** | Yes | Existing owners unaffected. See [Sale pricing](/publishing/sale-pricing) for time-boxed price changes. |
| **Delist a constituent product** | Yes, but bundle becomes unpurchasable | Existing owners retain entitlement to the delisted product. Bundle can't be purchased until you either remove the delisted product or delist the bundle. |

## Add or remove

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bundle in Partner Center">
    Navigate to the bundle product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Product relationships">
    In the left nav, select **Product relationships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove">
    Add a product by selecting it from your catalog. Remove a product by clicking the remove icon next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm entitlement propagation">
    Partner Center shows a preview of what changes for existing owners. Verify this matches your intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). Entitlement changes propagate within minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common patterns

### Add a new DLC to an existing Season Pass bundle

As each DLC in your roadmap ships, add it to the Season Pass bundle. Pass owners automatically get it in their library. See [Add-on bundles and Season Pass](/publishing/add-on-bundles).

### Swap an old title for a remaster in a franchise bundle

When you ship a remastered version of a title in a franchise bundle:

1. Add the remaster to the bundle.
2. Existing bundle owners get the remaster in their library.
3. Optionally remove the original older title from the bundle. Existing owners keep both.
4. New buyers get the remaster (and, if you kept the original, both).

### Retire a bundle

When a bundle is no longer commercially useful:

1. Delist the bundle (see [Pricing changes & delist](/publishing/how-to-pricing-changes)).
2. Existing owners retain entitlements to all constituent products.

Do not delete the bundle product — that breaks customer library entries.

## Regulatory considerations

In some markets (EU, Australia), materially changing the contents of a bundle after sale can trigger consumer-protection review. Adding value is fine; removing value from a bundle customers have already purchased is not. Adding a new DLC to a Season Pass is safe; removing a promised DLC is not.
