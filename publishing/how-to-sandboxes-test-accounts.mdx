## Create a sandbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account settings → Sandboxes">
    Sandbox management is at the account level, not per-product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new sandbox">
    Provide a short name (letters and digits, `PUBLISHER.NN` style). This name is what devkits will target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant access to team members">
    Only Partner Center users you explicitly add to the sandbox can publish services state to it or provision test accounts within it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish XBOX services">
    Publish achievements, stats, session templates, and Rich Presence strings to the new sandbox from your services configuration. See [XBOX services](/publishing/xbox-services).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Provision test accounts

Test accounts are Xbox Live accounts scoped to a sandbox. Purchases made by a test account are simulated — no real money changes hands.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account settings → Test accounts">
    Choose the target sandbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Provide a gamertag prefix, region, and age (adult, teen, child — age gates trigger different privacy defaults).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant entitlements">
    From the account's detail view, grant the base game and any add-ons you want the account to own for testing. This is the equivalent of a promo code redemption in the sandbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in on a devkit">
    Sign the test account in on a devkit pointed at the same sandbox to validate behavior.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multiplayer test account sets

For multiplayer titles, create a **friend cluster** of 4–8 accounts pre-friended to each other. Include these in [Supplemental Info](/publishing/supplemental-info) when submitting to cert so the tester can run multiplayer scenarios without spending 30 minutes setting up friend graphs.
