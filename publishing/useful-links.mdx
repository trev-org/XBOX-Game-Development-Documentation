A curated list of external resources you'll refer to across the publishing lifecycle.

## Partner Center

- [Partner Center dashboard](https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard) — primary work surface.
- [Partner Center support](https://partner.microsoft.com/support) — open a support ticket.
- [Redeem a Microsoft Store code](https://redeem.microsoft.com) — promo code redemption URL for reviewers and end users.

## Store Policies

- [Microsoft Store Policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/store-policies) — the master policy document your product must comply with.
- [Microsoft Store Policies change history](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/store-policies-change-history) — track policy changes.
- [Microsoft Store Developer Code of Conduct](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/store-developer-code-of-conduct).
- [Microsoft Store Product Ranking, Placement, and Marketing Disclosure Statement](https://aka.ms/disclosurepolicypdf).

## Age ratings

- [IARC](https://www.globalratings.com/) — International Age Rating Coalition.
- [ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/).
- [PEGI](https://pegi.info/).
- [USK](https://usk.de/) — Germany. Longform required for disc.
- [CERO](https://www.cero.gr.jp/) — Japan. Requires direct submission.
- [GRAC](https://www.grac.or.kr/) — Korea. Longform required for disc.
- [ACB](https://www.classification.gov.au/) — Australia. Longform required for disc.
- [ClassInd](https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/sua-protecao/classificacao) — Brazil.
