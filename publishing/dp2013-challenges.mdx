**Challenges** in DP 2013 are time-boxed player objectives with progression, rewards, and community aggregation. Common uses: daily quests, weekly events, seasonal goals, and community-wide targets.

## Challenge types

| Type | Scope | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily challenge** | Per-player | 24 hours |
| **Weekly challenge** | Per-player | 7 days |
| **Seasonal challenge** | Per-player | Full season / event window |
| **Community challenge** | Aggregated across all players | Days to weeks |
| **Friend-group challenge** | Aggregated across a friend group | Days |

## Anatomy

- **Challenge ID** — identifier queried at runtime.
- **Objective** — backed by one or more [Player stats](/publishing/dp2013-stats) with a threshold (`TotalKills` reaches 50).
- **Time window** — start and end UTC.
- **Reward** — stat grant, in-game currency (via consumables), cosmetic unlock (via durables), or gamerscore-adjacent recognition.
- **Localization** — challenge name and description per supported language.

## Authoring a challenge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Author the backing stat">
    Every challenge needs a stat that measures progress. See [Player stats](/publishing/dp2013-stats).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Challenges editor">
    Under Xbox services (DP 2013), open Challenges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the challenge">
    Provide ID, name, description, backing stat, target value, time window, and reward mechanism.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to sandbox">
    Publish and run test accounts through the challenge to confirm progression updates and reward grants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For live-ops: schedule ahead">
    Daily and weekly challenges are typically authored in bulk ahead of the event, then activated on schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Community challenges

Community challenges aggregate progress across all players. Example: "The community must complete 1 million missions this week to unlock a global reward."

- Community challenges use the same event pipeline as per-player stats, aggregated server-side.
- Latency is subject to the DP 2013 pipeline (hours), so avoid using them for real-time competitive countdowns.
- Reward grants at completion require your game to poll the community-challenge state and grant on next launch.
