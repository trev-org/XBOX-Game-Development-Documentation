For conceptual background on rating tracks see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings). This page covers the click-by-click Partner Center flow.

## Run the IARC questionnaire

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Age ratings">
    Navigate to your product's Age ratings module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new IARC questionnaire">
    Click **Start questionnaire**. Partner Center opens the IARC form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer every question">
    Answer for the **finished shipping content**, not the current build. Categories cover violence, sex, controlled substances, gambling-like mechanics, communication features, UGC, in-game purchases, and location data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    IARC returns provisional ratings for participating bodies within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the certificate ID">
    IARC issues a certificate ID. Keep it — you reference it when submitting to physical rating bodies later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload a region-specific rating certificate

For regions that require direct submission (CERO Japan, USK longform for discs, GRAC longform, ACB longform):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the body's own submission">
    Submit build, materials, and fees directly to CERO, USK, GRAC, or ACB. Turnaround varies from 2–8 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the certificate">
    The rating body issues a physical or digital certificate with an official rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload in Partner Center">
    In Age ratings, open **Physical rating certificates**. Upload the certificate file and enter the rating category and content descriptors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). The rating unlocks that region on the product's availability.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Re-run after content changes

Any content update that changes the answers to the IARC questionnaire requires you to re-run:

- New violent content, sexual content, or gambling mechanics.
- Added communication features (voice chat, direct messages).
- Added UGC.
- Removed features that had been declared.

Re-running does not always change the resulting rating, but it must be up-to-date to remain compliant.
