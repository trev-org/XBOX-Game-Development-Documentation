Before you can create a product in Partner Center you must **reserve a game name**. The reserved name becomes the primary display name on the Microsoft Store and in every downstream system (achievements, receipts, family libraries).

## Rules

- Names must be **unique** across the Microsoft Store on XBOX.
- Names must comply with Microsoft Store Policies (no trademark infringement, no offensive content, no impersonation of first-party or well-known franchises).
- Names cannot be pure single characters or reserved words ("Xbox", "Microsoft", "Game Pass", etc.).
- A reservation lasts until the product is either published or explicitly released back to the pool.

## How to reserve

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for your intended name">
    In Partner Center, open **Games → Reserve name** and search. Partner Center shows whether the exact name and close variants are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reserve">
    Click **Reserve** on the available name. This ties the name to your Partner Center account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the product">
    From the reserved name, create the product. See [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Localized names

The reserved name is your **base display name**. You can override the display name per locale in [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing) to accommodate translation and localization. Some markets (Japan, China) also allow region-specific display names distinct from the base.

## Renaming a game

Renaming a published product is possible but requires:

- Updated [Store listings](/publishing/store-listing) in every locale.
- A recert if the name change affects any achievement, in-game text, or Store artwork that includes the title.
- Coordination with your Microsoft representative if the title is on Game Pass or in any curated program.

Avoid renames after launch unless commercially essential — they confuse discovery and search.
