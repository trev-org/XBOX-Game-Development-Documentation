For initial pricing configuration see [Pricing](/publishing/pricing). This page covers ongoing pricing changes and delisting.

## Change base pricing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability">
    In the product, open the Pricing section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update per market">
    Change the base price per market. Price changes propagate to the Store within a few hours but retain a customer-visible history for regulatory purposes in some regions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). No cert required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Frequent price changes damage customer trust and, in some markets (EU, Australia), trigger consumer-protection review of your "was / now" pricing. Coordinate marketing before repricing.
</Warning>

## Run a sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability → Sales">
    Create a new sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sale window">
    Choose start and end UTC dates. Sales default to global; you can scope to specific markets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sale price or discount percentage">
    Either a hard price or a percentage off the base. Percentage sales apply per market automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Sales publish through Review and publish. The Store surfaces the sale price and "was / now" pricing during the sale window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Sale pricing](/publishing/sale-pricing) for eligibility rules and coordination with launches.

## Delist a product

Delisting removes a product from sale. Existing owners retain the entitlement, can re-download, and continue to receive updates you push.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability">
    Change the product's visibility to **Hidden** (see [Visibility](/publishing/how-to-visibility)) and set availability to end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify customers if needed">
    For live-service titles, communicate the sunset plan clearly ahead of delist. See [Content updates](/publishing/content-updates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Delist propagates within a few hours. The product PDP becomes unreachable via search but existing owners retain access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Full removal

Removing a product entirely — including revoking re-download for existing owners — is **rare**. Requires business justification (legal takedown, license expiry) coordinated with your Microsoft representative. Do not attempt to force this by deleting the product in Partner Center.
