The **Xbox Network Services Data Processing Addendum** (the "Addendum") is part of the App Developer Agreement (**ADA**) between you and Microsoft. It governs the Processing of Personal Data made available to your **PC and mobile** apps through Xbox Network Services.

If the ADA and the Addendum ever conflict on the treatment of Personal Data, **the Addendum controls**.

This page mirrors the operative text from the current Microsoft source. Reference it from your privacy policy where required. See [PC certification](/publishing/pc-certification) for how the DPA fits into PC cert obligations.

<Info>
  The DPA applies automatically to every publisher agreement that covers PC and mobile releases using Xbox Network Services. You don't sign it separately for each title. The DPA does **not** apply to Xbox console titles; console data handling is covered by other sections of the publisher agreement.
</Info>

## Key definitions

**Data Exporter** — The party that (1) has a corporate presence or other stable arrangement in a jurisdiction that requires an International Data Transfer Mechanism, and (2) transfers Personal Data, or makes Personal Data available to, the Data Importer.

**Data Importer** — The party that (1) is located in a jurisdiction different from the Data Exporter's, and (2) receives Personal Data from the Data Exporter or is able to access Personal Data made available by the Data Exporter.

**Data Protection Law** — Any law, rule, regulation, decree, or statute applicable to you or Microsoft relating to data security, protection, Processing, and privacy, including implementing legislation and regulatory guidance, as amended or replaced.

**Personal Data** — Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (**Data Subject**), and any other data that constitutes personal data or personal information under any applicable Data Protection Law.

**Controller** — The entity that determines the purpose and means of Processing.

**Process / Processing** — Any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data — collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, alignment, blocking, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

## Controller status

Both you and Microsoft are **independent data Controllers** — not joint Controllers — of the Personal Data each independently Processes under the ADA and this Addendum.

Each party is independently responsible for its own compliance with Data Protection Law, including:

- Providing notice to Data Subjects (for example, GDPR Articles 13 and 14).
- Responding to Data Subject requests to exercise their rights (for example, GDPR Chapter III).
- Identifying a lawful basis for Processing (consent, legitimate interest, etc.).

## Cooperation on Inquiries

If either party receives an **Inquiry** — a request, inquiry, claim, or complaint from a governmental, legislative, judicial, law-enforcement, or regulatory authority (for example, the FTC, a US state Attorney General, or a European data protection authority) related to Personal Data shared under the ADA and this Addendum — the receiving party will:

- Notify the other party without undue delay, unless notification is prohibited by applicable law.
- Promptly share information relevant to the Inquiry, including any material relevant to defending a claim.
- On request, provide information the other party needs to conduct data protection impact assessments or prior consultations with data protection authorities.

## Data security

You must ensure your network, operating system, software, databases, and other systems are properly built, configured, and operated to store, manage, and protect any Personal Data received from Microsoft in a secure manner and in compliance with the [XBOX Requirements](/publishing/xbox-requirements).

Each party must take all measures required by good industry practice and by Data Protection Law relating to data security (including GDPR Article 32).

## Confidentiality

Each party will ensure that persons authorized to Process the Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in the NDA or under an appropriate statutory confidentiality obligation.

## Prohibition on sale of Personal Data

Subject to a customer's authorization or contrary instructions, you will:

- Only use Personal Data in connection with the provision of games and game-related services.
- Not transfer, **Share**, or **Sell** Personal Data to any third party, except to contractually bound Processors or sub-processors operating on your behalf (where "Share" and "Sell" are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act or other applicable Data Protection Law).

Where Microsoft transfers data that is considered deidentified data under applicable Data Protection Law, you (and your sub-processors) will not attempt to reidentify the data such that it would become Personal Data.

To the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to any Personal Data, you certify that you understand these restrictions and will comply with them. Notify Microsoft if you determine you can no longer meet your obligations under applicable Data Protection Law.

## Compliance review

You will comply with Microsoft's request to conduct a compliance review of your adherence to the privacy obligations under this Addendum and applicable law. Microsoft may request such review **once per calendar year** or upon customer complaint. Except in the case of a customer complaint, you may submit an **attestation of compliance** in lieu of participating in a compliance review.

## Deletion on termination

On termination of the ADA for cause, an adverse compliance-review finding of mishandling, or the commencement of an Inquiry, you will, at Microsoft's request, **immediately delete or return** to Microsoft all copies of Personal Data shared under the ADA and this Addendum — except to the extent you have a right or obligation under applicable Data Protection Law to retain Personal Data after termination.

If requested by Microsoft, you must confirm deletion in writing within **30 days**.

## International data transfers

Some jurisdictions require an **International Data Transfer Mechanism** when Personal Data is sent to a recipient in another jurisdiction whose law doesn't provide equivalent protection. The parties will comply with any International Data Transfer Mechanism required by applicable Data Protection Law, including the **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**.

**Standard Contractual Clauses** means the European Union standard contractual clauses for international transfers from the EEA to third countries — Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.

- If the International Data Transfer Mechanism is invalidated or superseded, the parties will work together in good faith to find a suitable alternative.
- For Data Subjects in a jurisdiction that requires an International Data Transfer Mechanism (for example, EEA, Switzerland, UK), by executing this Addendum the parties also execute the SCCs. The SCCs are incorporated by reference. Schedule 1 provides the information the SCCs require in their Annexes.

### Localization

- **Switzerland** — The parties adopt the GDPR standard for all data transfers. The competent authorities under SCC Clause 13 and Annex I(C) are the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner and, concurrently, the applicable EEA member-state authority. Governing jurisdiction and forum: Ireland. Data Subjects in Switzerland may sue for their rights in Switzerland (SCC 18(c)). "Data Subjects" is interpreted to include Swiss legal entities until the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection becomes operative.
- **United Kingdom** — The SCCs are deemed amended as necessary to operate for UK-to-third-country transfers and to provide appropriate safeguards under UK GDPR Article 46. References to GDPR become UK GDPR; references to EU Member States become the United Kingdom. Governing jurisdiction: UK. Forum: the courts of England and Wales. Data Subjects may bring legal proceedings against either party in the courts of any UK country. Transfers from the UK are governed by the IDTA (the International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner's Office under S119A(1) of the UK Data Protection Act 2018) implemented by Microsoft.

## Schedule 1 — Description of the Processing

### Processing Activity

You collect or receive Personal Data as a **Controller**. Microsoft is a Controller. You are a Controller. **Module 1** of the SCCs applies.

### Categories of Personal Data that may be Processed

The categories below are descriptive; not every category is Processed in every scenario.

- Xbox User Identification (**XUID**)
- Location data
- IP address
- Device preferences and personalization
- Service usage — website and webpage click-tracking
- Social media data and social-graph relationships
- Activity data from connected devices (for example, fitness monitors)
- Contact data — name, address, phone number, email address, date of birth, dependent and emergency contacts
- Fraud and risk assessment, background checks
- Metadata and telemetry
- Xbox Live, OneDrive Consumer
- Customer-originated support tickets
- Billing data
- E-commerce data
- Event registration
- Training
- Globally Unique Identifier (**GUID**)
- Passport User ID or Unique Identifier (**PUID**)
- Hashed End-User Identifiable Information (**EUII**) — session IDs
- Device IDs
- Diagnostic data
- Log data
- Crash dump data

### Categories of Sensitive Data that may be Processed

- Data related to the age of the customer.
- Data related to children.

### Frequency and retention

- **Frequency of transfer:** Continuous for all Personal Data.
- **Retention periods:** As Controllers, each party retains Personal Data for as long as it has a business purpose or for the longest time allowable by applicable law.

### Standard Contractual Clauses — selections

- **Clause 7 (Docking):** The parties do not adopt the optional docking clause.
- **Clause 11(a):** The parties do not select the independent dispute-resolution option.
- **Clause 17 (Governing law):** Option 1 — Ireland.
- **Clause 18 (Forum):** Ireland.
- **Annex I(A) (Parties):** The data exporter and data importer are as defined in "Key definitions" above.
- **Annex I(B) (Description of the transfer):** As described in this Schedule 1.
- **Annex I(C) (Competent supervisory authority):** The Irish Data Protection Commission.
- **Annex II (Technical and organizational measures):** As set out in the Data Security section above.

## Your compliance checklist

- Reference the DPA by name in your privacy policy for any PC or mobile title that uses Xbox Network Services (see [Properties: Basic info](/publishing/properties-basic-info)).
- Keep your support contact monitored so it can respond to data-subject requests forwarded from Microsoft.
- Don't export Xbox Network Personal Data to third-party analytics or ad networks without explicit customer opt-in.
- Don't attempt to re-identify Xbox users by cross-referencing gamertag with other data sets.
- Keep your sub-processor list contractually bound to no-less-protective obligations.
- Be prepared to attest to compliance annually, or participate in a compliance review, at Microsoft's request.
