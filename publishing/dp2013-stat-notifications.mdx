**Stat change notifications** fire when a player's stat crosses a defined threshold. Common uses: "You've reached 100 wins!" milestone toasts, achievement-adjacent nudges, and social sharing prompts.

## How they work

- Each notification declares a **stat** to watch and a **threshold expression** (`value >= 100`, `delta > 1000`).
- When the stat updates and crosses the threshold, the DP 2013 pipeline triggers the notification.
- Notifications can be surfaced as **toasts**, **push notifications** (to the Xbox app), or **in-game hooks** (the game queries pending notifications on next launch and displays its own UI).

## Best practices

- **Don't spam.** Reserve notifications for genuine milestones (10, 100, 1000).
- **Respect user preferences.** Xbox family settings and per-title notification opt-outs disable notifications for opted-out accounts.
- **Consider timing.** For in-game hooks, buffer and display at natural break points.
- **Provide localization** for every supported language.
