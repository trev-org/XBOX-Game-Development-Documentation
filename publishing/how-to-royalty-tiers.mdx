A **royalty tier** is the wholesale price band you declare for a disc SKU in a specific region. Microsoft uses the tier to:

- Calculate the platform royalty per unit sold.
- Position the SKU with retail partners at the correct wholesale price band.
- Feed into your commercial reporting and payout reconciliation.

See [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs) for the broader disc order flow.

## Before you start

- Confirm you have an executed **disc addendum** with the current tier table. If you don't have the tier table, request it from your Microsoft representative.
- Confirm the target retail price band per region so you can pick the appropriate tier.
- Confirm you're the correct approver — tier submissions typically require a signer on your Partner Center account with Manager or Publisher role.

## Submit a tier per region

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Discs → Royalty configuration">
    In your product's Discs module, open Royalty configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select regions">
    You'll set a tier per region you plan to ship discs into. Common groupings: North America, EU, UK, Australia/NZ, Japan, Korea, LATAM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tier per region">
    Choose from the tier options in your addendum. Tiers typically map to standard retail wholesale bands (e.g. tier `A` = $59.99 SRP wholesale band, tier `B` = $49.99, etc.). Names and values are specific to your contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach justification (if required)">
    Some addendums require justification text for premium tier selection. Provide the rationale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Submit. Microsoft reviews within a few business days for consistency with your addendum and market norms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address feedback if needed">
    If Microsoft requests a change (typical: adjust tier to match a specific market's floor), update and resubmit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier changes after approval

After initial approval, tier changes are possible but require re-submission:

- **Region price adjustment** (e.g. moving a market between tier bands) requires a fresh submission and can take 5–10 business days.
- **Cross-region uniform change** is faster (single submission covering multiple regions at once).
- Changes typically **cannot be applied retroactively** to already-shipped inventory; they take effect on the next manufacturing order.

## Impact on payout

Royalty tier feeds into per-unit publisher payout:

- **Wholesale price** × quantity sold at retail = gross publisher revenue.
- Microsoft's per-unit royalty (per your addendum) is deducted.
- Chargebacks and returns adjust downward.
- Net publisher payout appears in the monthly payout report. See [Pricing models](/publishing/pricing-models).

## Common issues

| Issue | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Tier selection rejected as too high for market | Adjust to the tier Microsoft indicates; check your addendum's market-tier bindings. |
| Tier submission blocked | Usually a prerequisite is missing (disc addendum not on file, product not yet configured for discs). Contact your Microsoft representative. |
| Different tier needed for a specific retailer | Retailer-specific tiers are not supported in Partner Center; work with the retailer on end-consumer pricing under the region tier you've selected. |
