Common questions from teams running their first (or second, or tenth) XBOX Insider Program flight. For the concept overview see [XBOX Insider Program](/publishing/xbox-insider-program).

## Audience and rings

### How many Insiders are in each ring?

Microsoft doesn't publish exact ring populations. Rough guidance: **Alpha Skip-Ahead** is the smallest (thousands), **Beta** is mid-sized, and **Omega** is the largest (millions). Ask your Microsoft representative for current sizing when planning a flight.

### Can I target a specific country?

Yes. Flights can be scoped by region on top of ring membership. Common pattern: Beta ring, EU-only, for a soft-launch multiplayer test.

### Can I target specific accounts?

Yes — the **Private Preview** flight type lets you enumerate specific Microsoft accounts. Used for reviewer builds, closed alpha with named participants, and industry demos.

### Can I gate my flight by hardware?

Partially. Scope to a device family (Series X, Series S, Xbox One), but not to specific hardware SKUs beyond that.

## Content and cert

### Does the flight build have to pass Final Cert?

No. Flights ship without Final Cert. Flights do pass a lighter automated compliance check.

### What XRs still apply?

Stability and safety XRs (crashes, data loss, offensive content protection) still apply. Multiplayer, matchmaking, and store XRs are relaxed for flight builds.

### Can I flight content that isn't rated yet?

Only behind an NDA and to a Private Preview audience. Public Insider ring flights need age ratings issued.

## Telemetry and iteration

### What data do I get back from a flight?

- **Crash rate** and health telemetry.
- **Engagement metrics** (session count, session length, MAU on the flight).
- **Insider feedback** — submitted through Xbox Insider Hub, surfaced in Partner Center.
- **Custom telemetry** if you've instrumented your own.

### How fast can I iterate?

A new flight package can be uploaded, processed, and published in hours. Multi-daily iteration is possible.

### Should I do a public flight before Final Cert?

Often yes — a public flight catches issues internal QA and Optional Cert don't (network scale, hardware variety, real customer usage). Budget 2–4 weeks of flight time before Final Cert for major titles.

## Graduating to retail

### How do I promote a flight to retail?

1. Take the flight package that's proven stable.
2. Submit it for [Final Certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification).
3. On pass, publish through the standard retail flow.

There is no "one-click promote" — the Final Cert step is required.

### Do Insider-flight players carry saves to retail?

Cloud saves carry across because they're tied to the Microsoft account and your product ID. Handle save-format migration in code if the format changed.

### Are Insider testers paid?

No. Insider Program membership is free and voluntary.

## Operational

### Can I have multiple concurrent flights?

Yes — different rings can receive different packages. Use this for A/B testing.

### Can I stop a flight mid-way?

Yes. Revoke the flight package in the Packages module. Flight-audience customers stop receiving the update.

### What if the flight breaks retail?

Flight packages ship only to the flight audience. They do **not** touch retail owners.
