The **Certification Bypass Program** lets qualifying titles ship updates without going through the full XR test pass on every release.

## Eligibility

- Titles with a strong track record of clean cert passes.
- Live-service titles that ship frequent server-side updates.
- Approved by your Microsoft account manager.

## What still gets tested

Even on bypass, packages must pass:

- SubmissionValidator (local).
- Automated package scans (security, manifest, signing).
- A subset of high-risk XRs (privacy, crash, ratings).
