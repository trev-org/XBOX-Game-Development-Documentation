**XBOX services** are the platform-level online capabilities Xbox Live provides to your title: identity, presence, social, achievements, stats, leaderboards, multiplayer sessions, matchmaking, and privileges. Configuration happens in Partner Center; runtime integration happens in your game code via the GDK.

## What you configure in Partner Center

### Achievements

- Declare each achievement with ID, name, description, image, and gamerscore value.
- Total gamerscore for a base title is capped (typically 1000G at launch, with 250–1000G additions per DLC).
- Localize every achievement's name and description per supported language.

### Stats and leaderboards

- **Stats** are per-player values you write from game code (kills, distance, high score).
- **Featured stats** appear on the player's Xbox profile.
- **Leaderboards** are stat-backed and can be global, social (friends), or scoped by segment.

### Multiplayer

- **Session templates** describe the shape of your multiplayer sessions (max players, join rules, arbitration).
- **Matchmaking hoppers** define how players are matched into sessions.
- Both must be published to the target sandbox before the game can create sessions there.

### Rich Presence

- Localized strings that describe what a player is currently doing ("Racing on Silverstone", "Fighting the Ender Dragon").
- Referenced from game code by string ID; edits in Partner Center flow to clients without a game update.

### Privileges and privacy

- Declare which Xbox Live privileges the game requires (multiplayer, communications, UGC, cross-network play).
- Runtime checks against these privileges are gated by [XR-113 Privilege checks](/publishing/xbox-requirements) at cert.

## Publish to a sandbox

All XBOX services configuration lives in a **sandbox** and must be published to that sandbox before it becomes active.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit in your dev sandbox">
    Author achievements, stats, session templates, and Rich Presence strings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to dev sandbox">
    Publish services to your dev sandbox and test end-to-end with test accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote to RETAIL for Final Cert">
    Before Final Cert, promote the identical services config to the **RETAIL** sandbox. See [Sandboxes](/publishing/sandboxes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate on RETAIL post-publish">
    After the product publishes, verify achievements unlock, stats write, and multiplayer sessions form on RETAIL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Platform 2013 vs 2017

Older titles use **Data Platform 2013** (the classic Xbox 360 / Xbox One services model). New titles use **Data Platform 2017** with the modern GDK services. The two are configured through different Partner Center modules and use different runtime APIs. If you're building a new title, use Data Platform 2017; the Xbox services documentation in the [GDK](/gdk/what-is-gdk) tab covers the runtime side.
