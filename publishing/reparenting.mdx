**Reparenting** is the operation of moving a published product from one Partner Center account (the source) to another (the destination). It preserves customer entitlements, purchase history, achievements, and Store listing continuity.

<Warning>
  Reparenting is **not self-service**. Contact your Microsoft representative to initiate it. Attempts to sidestep this by delisting and republishing under a new account will break customer entitlements and are contractually prohibited.
</Warning>

## When reparenting is used

- **Studio acquisition** — a studio's catalog moves to the acquirer's Partner Center account.
- **Publisher change** — a title transitions from one publisher to another at contract renewal.
- **Sub-publishing dissolution** — a title previously published under a [sub-publishing](/publishing/sub-publishing) arrangement returns to the developer's account.
- **Corporate restructuring** — a publisher consolidates multiple Partner Center accounts into one.

## What transfers

| Item | Transfers with reparenting? |
| --- | --- |
| Customer entitlements and purchase history | ✅ |
| Achievements earned by customers | ✅ |
| Store listing (with new publisher line applied) | ✅ |
| XBOX services state (achievements, stats, multiplayer templates) | ✅ |
| Package history and cert history | ✅ |
| Add-ons and bundles owned by the same product | ✅ (must be reparented together) |
| Analytics data | Reported under the new account going forward |
| Payouts | Payouts land in the destination account from the reparenting effective date |
| Existing agreements (Game Pass, etc.) | Renegotiated — not automatically transferred |

## Reparenting workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the business change">
    Have the underlying contract, acquisition, or publisher-change agreement in place with all parties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact both Microsoft representatives">
    Notify the source and destination Microsoft representatives. Microsoft verifies that both parties consent to the transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Microsoft executes reparenting">
    Microsoft's account team performs the transfer server-side. The product simply appears in the destination account once complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Store presence">
    Refresh the [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing) publisher line, developer credit, and any assets that reference the previous entity. Publish these updates through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payouts and tax">
    Verify the destination account's tax and payout details. See [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account).
  </Step>
</Steps>
