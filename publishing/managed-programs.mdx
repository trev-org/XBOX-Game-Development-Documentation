Beyond standard self-service publishing, Microsoft runs several **curated** programs that offer additional distribution, marketing, or revenue opportunities. Participation is opt-in and typically requires a formal agreement.

## Common programs

### ID@Xbox

The indie publishing program. ID@Xbox members get access to XBOX devkits, XBOX Live features, marketing support, and Microsoft's cert team on standard commercial terms with no upfront fees beyond agreed royalties.

- **Eligibility**: Indie or mid-tier developers with previously shipped games or a demonstrable production pipeline.
- **How to join**: Apply through the [ID@Xbox portal](https://www.xbox.com/developers/id) and complete the [onboarding process](/onboarding).
- **What's included**: Devkits, XBOX Live services access, cert support, marketing consideration.

### Xbox Game Pass

A subscription service where Microsoft licenses your title for inclusion in the Game Pass catalog for a defined term. Titles are typically curated for portfolio fit, projected engagement, and strategic value.

- **How it works**: Microsoft pays a negotiated licensing fee for inclusion. During the Game Pass term, subscribers can install and play the title without a separate purchase; you retain the right to sell the title outside Game Pass.
- **Getting in**: Not self-service. Discussed with your Microsoft representative or ID@Xbox contact.
- **Technical requirements**: Passing Final Cert, no known blocker XRs, and integration with any Game Pass-specific requirements (for example graceful handling of Game Pass entitlement revocation — see [XStore S2S APIs](/publishing/xstore-server-to-service)).

### PC Game Pass

The PC-specific arm of Game Pass. Same commercial model, different technical requirements — titles must run on Windows PC and meet PC-specific XRs. See [XStore on PC](/publishing/xstore-pc).

### Play Anywhere

A title-level program where a customer's purchase entitles them to play on both Xbox and PC with shared saves and progression. Requires cross-play, cross-save, and cross-entitlement engineering work but positions the title as a premium cross-platform offering.

## Other opportunities

- **Xbox Live Gold / Games with Gold** — legacy free-monthly-game promotions; less common than they used to be.
- **Xbox Store curated sales** — seasonal sales, publisher spotlights, and platform-level events. Discussed with your Microsoft representative.
- **Regional launch programs** — co-marketing programs for launches in specific regions.

## How to explore

All curated programs are relationship-driven. Talk to your Microsoft representative or ID@Xbox contact to discuss which programs fit your title, roadmap, and business goals.
