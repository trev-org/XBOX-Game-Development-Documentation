**Promotional codes** (also called **tokens**) are Microsoft Store redemption codes that grant a specific product to whoever redeems them. Use them for review distribution, giveaways, influencer partnerships, contest prizes, and pre-configured hidden products.

## Types of promo codes

| Type | Grants | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product codes** | The specific product (base game, edition, or add-on) | Reviewer copies, influencer distribution |
| **Bundle codes** | Every product in the referenced bundle | Season pass giveaways |
| **Hidden token-only product codes** | A product that is not visible on the Store; only redeemable via code | Retailer pre-order bonuses, contest prizes with content that shouldn't be publicly listed |

## Ordering codes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the product is published">
    Codes can only be minted for products that are **published** (`In the Store`), including hidden token-only products. See [Publish to RETAIL](/publishing/publish-to-retail).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Promotions → Promotional codes">
    In Partner Center, navigate to your product's **Promotions → Promotional codes** module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a batch">
    Choose the product/bundle, quantity, and target markets. Standard batch sizes are up to 500 codes per request; larger batches require approval from your Microsoft representative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and distribute">
    Approved batches are delivered as a CSV file to the requestor. Codes are one-time use and redeemable at [redeem.microsoft.com](https://redeem.microsoft.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Regional considerations

- Codes are **market-scoped**. A code minted for the US market can only be redeemed by a Microsoft account whose region is set to the US.
- For global influencer distribution, mint separate batches per major market.
- Some markets (mainland China) do not support promo codes — confirm with your Microsoft representative before promising codes for those regions.

## Best practices

- Order review codes at least **2 weeks before** review embargoes lift. Approval and delivery can take a few business days.
- Track distributed codes so you know who has what — codes are not revocable once redeemed.
- For pre-launch review builds, pair codes with a private sandbox flight if the retail SKU isn't yet public. See [XBOX Insider Program](/publishing/xbox-insider-program).
