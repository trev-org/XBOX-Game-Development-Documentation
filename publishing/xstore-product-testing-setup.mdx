XStore is not usable from a dev kit or PC until your product is visible in the sandbox that machine is switched to. This page walks through the exact steps.

## 1. Choose (or create) a development sandbox

A **sandbox** is an isolated Store/services environment. Your team typically has one or more dev sandboxes with names like `PUBLISHER.1`, `PUBLISHER.2`. RETAIL is the sandbox real customers see.

Create additional sandboxes under **Xbox Services → Sandboxes** in Partner Center.

## 2. Publish the product to that sandbox

In Partner Center for the product:

1. Complete a **Store submission** with at least Product Setup, Pricing and Availability, Properties, Age Ratings, and Packages.
2. Under **Pricing and Availability → Sandboxes**, add your dev sandbox.
3. Submit the certification-free path (dev-sandbox submissions do not require full cert).
4. Wait until the sandbox status shows **In the Store**.

Repeat for every add-on you want to see at runtime.

## 3. Switch the target machine to the sandbox

On an Xbox devkit (via XbConnect / Windows Device Portal or `xbconnect.exe`):

```text
xbconnect /X:<devkit-name> sandbox PUBLISHER.1
```

On PC, use the **Gaming Services** sandbox switcher tool provided with the GDK, or set the sandbox via `wdapp.exe`.

See [Switching sandboxes properly for store operations](/publishing/xstore-switching-sandbox) for PC-specific caveats.

## 4. Add the test account to the sandbox

Under **Xbox Services → Access**, add the Microsoft accounts you will sign in with to the sandbox's allow list. Sign that account out and back in on the device so the token refreshes.

## 5. Sign in on the device and validate

1. On the target device, sign in with an allow-listed test account.
2. Launch your title (or the **Store** app in the sandbox) and confirm your product is discoverable.
3. From your title, call `XStoreQueryAssociatedProductsAsync` and log the returned products.

If nothing shows up, see the sandbox/access diagnostics in [Troubleshooting XStore development](/publishing/xstore-troubleshooting).

## Recommended dev habits

- Keep at least two test accounts: one that **owns** the product, one that does not.
- Keep a **trial-owner** account for testing trial flows.
- Keep a family group of accounts for family-sharing and home-Xbox scenarios.
- After a Partner Center change, wait 5–15 minutes and re-launch before assuming your code is wrong — sandbox propagation is not instantaneous.
