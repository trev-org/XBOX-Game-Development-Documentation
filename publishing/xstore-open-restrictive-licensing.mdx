When configuring a PC title in Partner Center, you choose one of two license enforcement models:

- **Open licensing** — the title can launch even if the license check fails to reach the Store server. Best for single-player content, offline scenarios, and titles that treat DRM lightly.
- **Restrictive licensing** — the title requires a successful license check on every launch. Best for online-first titles or titles that need strong entitlement enforcement.

Set this in **Partner Center → Product Setup → Licensing**.

## Behavior differences

| Aspect | Open | Restrictive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First launch, license present | Launch | Launch |
| First launch, Store client offline | Launch (open license grace) | Refuse launch |
| Subsequent launch, license present, offline | Launch (cached license) | Grace period (configurable), then refuse |
| License revoked mid-session | `PackageLicenseLost` fires | `PackageLicenseLost` fires |
| Sandbox switch causing missing license | Launch may still succeed | Refuse launch |

## When to choose open

- Story-driven single-player games where an offline experience is important.
- Titles that already ship additional DRM layers.
- Free-to-play titles where you're relying on service-side entitlement checks anyway.

## When to choose restrictive

- Multiplayer-only titles.
- Titles with heavy paid DLC where restricting play mirrors real-money value.
- Games in markets where piracy pressure warrants stricter enforcement.

## Trade-offs to weigh

- Restrictive licensing eliminates certain "pirate can run the game offline" scenarios but produces launch failures during real-world outages (customer's ISP, Windows Update disrupting the Store client, corporate firewall).
- Open licensing is friendlier to customers but means your title can run for extended periods against a stale license. Your service-side entitlement checks become the real backstop.

## Testing licensing modes

Simulate offline by:

1. Ensuring the title has a fresh license (launch once online).
2. Disconnecting the machine from the network.
3. Launching the title. Behavior should match your chosen mode.

Repeat with the machine offline for extended periods (1 day, 7 days, 30 days) to catch stale-license expiry cases specific to restrictive mode.
