A **franchise hub** is a discoverability and merchandising surface in the XBOX Store that groups related products (base game, expansions, DLC, bundles) into a single experience.

## Configure a franchise hub

- Define a franchise grouping in Partner Center.
- Associate your product family (base game, [add-ons](/publishing/add-ons), bundles, season passes).
- Provide hero art, trailers, and editorial copy.

## Benefits

- Better cross-sell of DLC and follow-up titles.
- Centralized storefront for live-service titles with multiple SKUs.
- Eligible for editorial promotion on the XBOX Store front page.
