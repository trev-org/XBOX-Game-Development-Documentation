**Featured stats** are the small set of player stats that appear on a player's Xbox profile card for your game. They're the customer-facing summary of what a player does in your game.

## What players see

For titles with featured stats, the Xbox profile card shows:

- Up to a small handful (typically 3–5) of stats you chose to feature.
- The stat name and current value per player.
- Comparative context where available.

Featured stats power some social discovery flows — friends can compare progress in your game.

## Choosing featured stats

Good featured stats are:

- **Understandable at a glance** — `TotalKills`, `HighestScore`, `HoursPlayed`.
- **Cross-mode meaningful**.
- **Slow-changing on the profile side** — DP 2013 latency for featured stat updates is typically hours.

Avoid featuring stats that only make sense to hardcore players or that change too frequently to be a useful profile signal.
