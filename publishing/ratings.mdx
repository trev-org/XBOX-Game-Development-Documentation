Age ratings (ESRB, PEGI, USK, CERO, ACB, IARC) are required before your title can publish to retail in each region.

## How it works

1. Complete the **IARC questionnaire** in Partner Center.
2. IARC issues provisional ratings for most regions (PEGI, ESRB, ClassInd, USK, ACB, GRAC).
3. Some regions (CERO Japan) still require a separate submission.
4. Update the questionnaire when adding new content (UGC, gambling-like mechanics, etc.).

## Tips

- Be accurate — misrepresenting content causes resubmission and possible delisting.
- DLC must align with the base-game rating or get its own rating.
- UGC titles default to the most permissive rating in many regions.
