After your disc image is on track for certification, the two remaining commercial steps are (1) declaring the **royalty tier** for the SKU and (2) submitting the actual **manufacturing order** for each region.

## Submit a royalty tier

The royalty tier declares the anticipated wholesale price of the disc SKU. Microsoft uses it to calculate platform royalty and to place the SKU in the correct pricing band with retailers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Discs → Royalty configuration">
    In the product's Discs module, open **Royalty configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the tier per region">
    Choose the tier for each region you plan to ship into. Tiers are defined in your disc addendum with Microsoft — reach out to your representative if you need the current tier table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Microsoft reviews the tier selection against your addendum and either approves or requests a change. Approval is required before you can submit a manufacturing order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submit a manufacturing order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm RTM is complete">
    Orders cannot be placed against a SKU that has not been RTM'd. See [Approve discs for manufacture](/publishing/approve-discs-for-manufacture).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quantities per region">
    In **Discs → Manufacturing order**, enter quantities per region. Meet or exceed the minimum order quantity (MOQ) defined in your disc addendum for each region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm ship-to and destination warehouse">
    Provide the destination warehouse address for each region. Microsoft's replicator ships to your named distributor — Microsoft does not warehouse finished discs on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and track">
    Submit the order. Track status in the Discs module: `Order received → Scheduled → In production → Shipped`. Standard lead time is 4–8 weeks depending on quantity and region.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reprints

Reprints reuse the same RTM'd image and artwork. Open the Discs module and submit an additional manufacturing order — no re-cert or re-approval required as long as the image and artwork versions are unchanged.

<Note>
  If you need to update the disc image (for example to include content beyond the day-one update), that is a **new SKU**, not a reprint — it requires a fresh cert, RTM, and royalty submission.
</Note>
