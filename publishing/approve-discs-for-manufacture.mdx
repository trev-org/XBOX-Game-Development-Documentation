**Release To Manufacture (RTM)** is the point of no return for a physical XBOX product. When you RTM, you are contractually approving the exact disc image and packaging artwork that will be pressed at the replicator. Any change after RTM requires a full re-cert and re-manufacture at your cost.

## Prerequisites

RTM is only enabled once **all** of the following are true for the product:

- Disc image has passed **Disc Certification** — see [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification).
- Digital base package has passed **Final Certification** and matches the disc image manifest.
- Age ratings are issued (including physical longform ratings) for every region on the disc SKU plan — see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings).
- Disc artwork is approved in the Discs module — see [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging).
- Royalty tier is submitted and approved — see [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs).
- Day-one update package (if any) is uploaded and staged in [Packages](/publishing/packages).

## RTM sign-off in Partner Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discs module for your product">
    Navigate to **Product → Discs → RTM approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the manifest">
    Verify the disc image build number, package hash, and the linked cert submission ID. Verify the artwork version listed matches the approved artwork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the RTM attestation">
    An authorized signer on your Partner Center account (a role with **Manager** or **Publisher** rights, not just Developer) must attest that this build is the intended shipping build. This is a legal sign-off — treat it accordingly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit RTM">
    Once submitted, the disc image and artwork are released to Microsoft's replication partner. Manufacturing begins on their schedule; typical lead time from RTM to first-copies-off-the-line is 4–8 weeks depending on order size and season.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common RTM blockers

| Blocker | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Disc cert not passed | Complete disc cert first. See [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification). |
| Artwork not approved | Address feedback in the Discs module. See [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging). |
| Missing physical longform ratings | Submit longform ratings to the physical rating body for that region. See [Ratings](/publishing/ratings). |
| Mismatched manifest between digital and disc | Re-align packages so day-one update applies cleanly on top of the disc image. |
| Royalty tier not filed | Submit royalty tiers before RTM. See [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs). |

## After RTM

- Track manufacturing progress and expected in-market dates in the Discs module.
- Coordinate the **street date** with your Microsoft representative — the digital release and disc release usually go live together.
- Prepare a day-one update in [Packages](/publishing/packages) and schedule its publish to align with the disc street date.
