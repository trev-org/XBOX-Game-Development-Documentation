**Achievements** in DP 2013 are the legacy achievement model. New titles use the modern DP 2017 achievements API (documented in the GDK). Legacy titles maintained on DP 2013 continue to use this editor.

## Achievement anatomy

- **Achievement ID** — numeric identifier written from game code.
- **Name** — short customer-facing title.
- **Description (locked)** — what the player sees before earning. Often a hint.
- **Description (unlocked)** — what the player sees after earning. Reveals the accomplishment.
- **Gamerscore value** — 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, 75, or 100 G. Sum across a title must not exceed the cap.
- **Achievement image** — unlocked-state artwork. Displayed in the Achievements panel.
- **Secret** — hidden until unlocked (locked name and description are blurred).
- **Unlock condition** (optional) — bind to a stat threshold for auto-unlock; alternatively unlock explicitly from game code.

## Gamerscore rules

| Title type | Total gamerscore cap |
| --- | --- |
| Base game | 1000 G |
| Small DLC pack | 100–250 G |
| Large DLC pack | Up to 500 G |
| Season Pass (cumulative across DLC) | Typically 750–1000 G |
| Free-to-play title | Up to 1000 G, subject to review |

Exceeding the cap requires business approval from your Microsoft representative.

## Authoring an achievement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Achievements editor">
    Under Xbox services (DP 2013), open Achievements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new achievement">
    Provide ID, name, locked/unlocked descriptions, gamerscore, and image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set unlock condition">
    Bind to a stat threshold ("unlock when stat `TotalKills` \>= 100") or leave for explicit game-code unlock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Localize">
    Provide name and descriptions per supported language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to sandbox and validate">
    Publish to dev sandbox. Trigger the unlock condition (either by writing the stat or calling the unlock API) and confirm the achievement fires on a test account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote to RETAIL before Final Cert">
    Achievements are validated by cert against the RETAIL sandbox schema — promote before submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Achievement guidelines

- **Cover the game's play surface** — story completions, mode variety, difficulty tiers, collectibles. Avoid every achievement being "kill N enemies."
- **Not every achievement should be secret.** Secret achievements should be actual surprises — story endings, hidden bosses — not obvious progression.
- **Reachable, but not trivial.** Achievements that unlock on the first launch feel worthless.
- **Localize carefully.** "Achievement unlocked" is a customer-facing moment; typos and awkward translations get called out on social media.
- **Consider DLC ahead of time.** Reserve some of your 1000 G cap if you plan post-launch achievements via DLC.
