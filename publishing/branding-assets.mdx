Microsoft supplies approved XBOX brand assets for use in your Store listing, box art, marketing, and trailers. Using these assets correctly is contractually required and enforced at cert and media validation.

## What Microsoft provides

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Xbox platform lockup** | The "Xbox Series X | S", "Optimized for Xbox Series X | S", "Xbox One" lockups used on box art and marketing. Placement rules are strict. |
| **Xbox controller renders** | Approved product photography and 3D renders of Xbox Wireless Controller, Elite Controller, and Xbox Adaptive Controller. |
| **Feature badges** | Smart Delivery, HDR, 4K UHD, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, VRR, 120 FPS, Play Anywhere. Use only when the shipping product actually supports the feature. |
| **Xbox Game Pass logo** | Only for titles in the Game Pass catalog. Do not use if you are not currently in Game Pass. |
| **Xbox Live / Xbox network callouts** | Marks required or permitted for titles with online features. |
| **Marketing templates** | Photoshop / InDesign templates for box art, key art, and Store hero art. |
| **Style guide** | The current XBOX Retail Packaging Specification and the Xbox marketing style guide. |

## How to access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask your Microsoft contact">
    Branding assets are distributed through your Microsoft representative or ID@Xbox contact. They are not self-service in Partner Center for most publishers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the brand usage agreement">
    Microsoft requires a brand usage acknowledgment before releasing the current asset pack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the asset kit">
    You'll receive a versioned asset kit (usually a downloadable ZIP) with all approved marks, renders, and templates. Kits are versioned — always use the current version referenced by your representative.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usage rules

- **Never modify** logos, lockups, or renders. Do not recolor, skew, or crop into safe zones.
- **Never combine** an Xbox lockup with competitor platform lockups without approval.
- **Never claim** features you don't ship (Smart Delivery, HDR, Play Anywhere). Feature-badge misuse fails media validation and, in some markets, is grounds for consumer-protection review.
- **Retire outdated marks** promptly. When a mark version updates, remove the old version from active marketing within the transition window Microsoft specifies.
