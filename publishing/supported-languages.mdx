Supported languages are declared in [Gaming metadata](/publishing/gaming-metadata) and surface on the Store PDP's supported-languages callout. This page is the reference for **which languages Microsoft supports** and **how they are presented**.

## Support levels

For each declared language you choose one or more of:

| Level | Meaning | Customer sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Interface** | Menus, HUD, system text translated. | "UI" mark. |
| **Full audio** | Voice content dubbed in this language. | "Full audio" mark. |
| **Subtitles** | Voice and cutscene content subtitled. | "Subtitles" mark. |

A language declared with all three shows the highest support level. A language declared as Subtitles only shows Subtitles.

## Do not over-declare

<Warning>
  Declaring a language you don't actually support is a media-validation failure and a Certification issue. Customers who buy expecting Japanese and get English-only file support tickets and negative reviews.
</Warning>

Declare only what ships in your day-one build. If localization arrives via post-launch update, add the language declaration when the update ships — not before.

## Regional store presence

Supported languages are separate from **market availability** (which regions can buy). You can ship a title that:

- Supports English \+ Japanese \+ French but is available for purchase in every market.
- Supports 20 languages but is only available in EU markets.
- Is Japanese-only but available globally (rare, but allowed).

Market availability is configured in [Pricing](/publishing/pricing) and [Availability schedule](/publishing/availability-schedule).
