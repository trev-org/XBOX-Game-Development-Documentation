The GDK exposes Microsoft Store commerce to your title through two complementary surfaces:

- **XStore (client-side, in your title)** — `XStore*` and `XPackage*` APIs for showing purchase UI, enumerating add-ons, checking licenses and entitlements, mounting DLC packages, and reporting consumable fulfillment.
- **Microsoft Store S2S REST APIs (service-side, in your backend)** — HTTPS endpoints for authoritatively querying entitlements, fulfilling consumables, granting products, detecting Xbox Game Pass, and processing refunds and chargebacks.

Both surfaces share the same product catalog you configure in **Partner Center** and the same identity model rooted in the player's Microsoft account.

## What Store commerce covers

- **Base games** — the primary product players buy or receive via Xbox Game Pass.
- **Add-ons** — durables (permanent unlocks), durables with packages (DLC that ships its own MSIXVC), consumables, and store-managed subscriptions.
- **Bundles and season passes** — collections that grant multiple child entitlements from a single purchase.
- **Trials** — time-limited or usage-limited trials that grant a temporary license.
- **Cross-device entitlements** — Xbox Play Anywhere, family sharing, home Xbox, and Xbox Game Pass.

## Product model at a glance

Every product in Partner Center has a set of identifiers that show up throughout the XStore APIs and Store REST:

| Identifier | Format | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **StoreId** (Product ID) | 12-character alphanumeric (for example, `9NBLGGH42THS`) | Deep links, `XStoreProduct::storeId`, S2S queries |
| **BigId** | 12-character alphanumeric | Legacy product identifier still surfaced by some APIs |
| **SKU ID** | GUID | Identifies a specific SKU within a product |
| **TitleId** / **ServiceConfigurationId (SCID)** | Hex / GUID | Ties the title to XBOX services and `MicrosoftGame.config` |
| **MSAAppId** | GUID | Used for Microsoft account auth from the title |

## How the pieces connect at runtime

1. Player launches your title. The Store activates the license and mounts the base package.
2. Title calls `XStoreCreateContext` for the signed-in user.
3. Title queries the catalog with `XStoreQueryAssociatedProductsAsync` or `XStoreQueryProductsAsync`.
4. Title checks entitlements with `XStoreQueryEntitledProductsAsync`, `XStoreQueryAddOnLicensesAsync`, or `XStoreQueryGameLicenseAsync`.
5. Title shows purchase UI with `XStoreShowPurchaseUIAsync` when the player wants to buy something.
6. For consumables, title reads the balance with `XStoreQueryConsumableBalanceRemainingAsync` and reports spend with `XStoreReportConsumableFulfillmentAsync`.
7. Optional: title requests a User Store ID and hands it to your service for authoritative S2S calls.

## Client vs. service responsibility

<Info>
  **Rule of thumb:** show it on the client, prove it on the server. Any decision that must survive reinstall, sign-out, or client tampering (durable currency, granted rewards, subscription state) should be reconciled with the S2S REST APIs from your backend of record.
</Info>
