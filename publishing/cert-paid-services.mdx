Beyond standard [Certification](/publishing/certification), Microsoft offers **paid certification services** that expand the standard cert offering. These are optional and priced separately from your publisher agreement.

## Available paid services

### Expedited certification

Moves your submission to the front of the cert queue. Typical turnaround compresses to **2–4 business days** instead of the standard 5–14 business days.

- **When it's worth it**: launch windows where a slipped cert cycle would miss a fixed street date (Q4 release, tent-pole moment, disc RTM lead time constraint).
- **When it's not**: routine content updates, small metadata changes, or when a [Cert Bypass](/publishing/cert-bypass-program) would meet the need.
- **Cost**: quoted per submission by your Microsoft representative.

### Dedicated test resource

Assigns a specific Microsoft test engineer to your title for the duration of a cert cycle. They act as a single point of contact for FMAs, deviation requests, and clarifying repro steps.

- **When it's worth it**: complex live-service titles with many cert cycles per quarter, or titles with a highly custom multiplayer / matchmaking setup that a generalist tester needs onboarding time to understand.
- **Cost**: quoted per week or per quarter.

### Pre-cert consulting

A scheduled consult with Microsoft's cert team to walk your build in-development, identify likely XR issues, and plan the cert submission. Not a formal cert pass — the output is guidance, not a FMA.

- **When it's worth it**: first-title teams, cross-platform titles targeting Console \+ PC for the first time, or teams that had a rough previous cert cycle and want to reset.

### Third-party authorized test vendors

Microsoft-authorized independent test vendors that run pre-cert validation on your behalf. They cannot issue an official cert pass but they can produce a report identifying likely XR failures against a build you provide.

- **When it's worth it**: teams without a large internal QA function.
- See [Third-party test vendors](/publishing/test-vendors) for the current authorized list.

## How to purchase

All paid cert services are arranged through your Microsoft representative. There is no self-service purchase flow in Partner Center for these services.
