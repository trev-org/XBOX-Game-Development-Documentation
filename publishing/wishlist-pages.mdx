A **wishlist page** (also called a **coming soon page** or **visible non-purchasable page**) is a live product page on the Microsoft Store for a title that isn't yet purchasable. Customers can add it to their wishlist, follow it, and receive a notification when it becomes available or opens for pre-order.

## When to use a wishlist page

- The title has been publicly announced and you want a Store presence to point marketing at.
- Pre-orders are not yet open, but you want to start collecting demand signal.
- You want to test messaging (title description, hero art, feature callouts) before a purchase moment goes live.

## What's live vs what isn't

| Element | Wishlist page | Purchasable page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store listing (title, art, description) | ✅ Live | ✅ Live |
| Trailers, screenshots | ✅ Live | ✅ Live |
| Add to wishlist / follow | ✅ Enabled | ✅ Enabled |
| Price displayed | ❌ "Coming soon" | ✅ Displayed |
| Buy button | ❌ Hidden | ✅ Live |
| Pre-order button | ❌ Hidden | ✅ If configured |

## Set up a wishlist page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete Store listing">
    Finish enough of your [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing) that the page presents well: title, hero art, at least three screenshots, short description, and a trailer if you have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Age ratings">
    Provisional IARC ratings are required for the page to publish. See [Ratings](/publishing/ratings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set availability to Visible, non-purchasable">
    In [Pricing](/publishing/pricing) and availability, set visibility to **Visible** but leave the purchase state as **Not available for purchase**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and publish">
    Submit through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). Because there's no package yet, this is a metadata-only publish and does not go through the full cert queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transitioning to pre-order or purchase

When you're ready to open pre-orders or launch, edit the same product's availability to enable purchase or pre-order. See [Pre-release](/publishing/pre-release). The wishlist and follower base carries over to the purchasable page automatically — do not create a new product.
