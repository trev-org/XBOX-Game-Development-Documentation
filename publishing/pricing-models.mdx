Partner Center supports several **pricing models** that govern how your product is priced, sold, and paid out. The default is the standard retail model; the others cover specific commercial arrangements.

## Standard retail model

The default for almost every self-published title.

- **Publisher sets the retail price** per market via [Pricing](/publishing/pricing).
- **Microsoft sells** the product on the Microsoft Store at that retail price.
- **Publisher receives a revenue share** per your commercial agreement with Microsoft, net of applicable taxes, chargebacks, and refunds.
- Standard **monthly payout** at agreed thresholds via the payout method configured in [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account).

## Reseller / wholesale model

Used for **physical disc** channel distribution and some large-publisher digital arrangements.

- **Publisher sets a wholesale price** — the price Microsoft pays the publisher per unit.
- **Microsoft (or a retailer) sets the retail price** independently.
- Retail-price fluctuations don't affect publisher payout — you're paid at the fixed wholesale price per unit sold.
- Common for **disc SKUs** and some Game Pass licensing arrangements.

See [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs) for the disc-specific royalty tier submission that feeds into this model.

## Free-to-use / free-to-play

- Base game priced at **\$0**.
- Publisher earns revenue only from [Add-ons](/publishing/add-ons), advertising (where allowed by regional law and platform policy), or subscription tie-ins.
- Ad revenue and third-party monetization must comply with Microsoft Store policies.

## Game Pass licensing

For titles included in Xbox Game Pass or PC Game Pass:

- **Microsoft licenses** the title from the publisher for a defined term.
- **Licensing fee** is negotiated and paid up-front, in tranches, or on a per-engagement basis depending on the deal.
- Publisher **retains the right** to sell the title outside Game Pass at the standard retail model.
- Standalone retail sales continue to pay out at the standard retail revenue share.

See [Managed programs](/publishing/managed-programs).

## Sub-publisher model

When a title is sub-published (see [Sub-publishing](/publishing/sub-publishing)):

- **Publisher's account** receives Microsoft's payout at the standard retail model.
- **Publisher then settles** with the developer per their private contract.
- Microsoft is not party to the developer-publisher split.

## Payout mechanics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure payout account">
    In [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account), configure a bank account or PayPal (where available) for payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure tax profile">
    Provide tax residency, VAT number (where applicable), and any withholding forms Microsoft requires for your jurisdiction. Missing tax info blocks payouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the payout threshold">
    Payouts execute monthly once your accrued balance passes the minimum threshold (typically \$50 USD equivalent; varies by payout method).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor payouts">
    Payout history and reconciliation reports are under **Payouts** in Partner Center.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refunds, chargebacks, and clawback

- **Refunds** in the standard 14-day / 2-hour Store refund window: revenue is reversed on the day of refund.
- **Chargebacks** (customer disputes a transaction with their card issuer): revenue is reversed plus a small chargeback fee, per your agreement.
- **Clawback** (customer's card declines after entitlement was granted): if the customer consumed in-game consumables between purchase and clawback, the S2S clawback API lets your backend reconcile. See [XStore S2S APIs](/publishing/xstore-server-to-service).
