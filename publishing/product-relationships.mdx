**Product relationships** are the explicit links between products that Partner Center uses to determine ownership, upgrade eligibility, and Store presentation. They sit on top of [Product groups](/publishing/product-groups) with more granular semantics.

## Types of relationships

| Relationship | From | To | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add-on parent** | Add-on | Base game | The add-on is only usable/purchasable in the context of the base game. Every add-on has exactly one parent. |
| **Edition upgrade** | Upgrade add-on | Base game \+ Deluxe edition | Grants a Standard owner the entitlements of the Deluxe edition. |
| **Bundle contents** | Bundle | Multiple products | Purchasing the bundle grants entitlements to every referenced product. |
| **Cross-gen included product** | Xbox One product | Xbox Series X | S product | Purchasing one grants entitlement to the other (rarely used post-Smart Delivery). |
| **Season Pass entitlement** | Season Pass add-on | Individual DLC add-ons | Owning the season pass grants entitlement to every included DLC as it releases. |
| **Trial parent** | Trial product | Full product | The trial redirects to the full product for upsell. |

## Configuring relationships

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the child product">
    Navigate to the add-on, bundle, or upgrade product in Partner Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Product relationships">
    In the product's left nav, select **Product relationships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the parent(s)">
    Select the base game (and any additional referenced products) and choose the relationship type. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the relationship change through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). Relationship changes propagate to the Store within minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common mistakes

- **Missing add-on parent** — the add-on will publish but customers cannot purchase it because it has no context.
- **Season Pass without individual DLC entitlements** — the pass sells but doesn't grant anything until each DLC add-on is linked to it.
- **Bundle with a delisted product inside** — the bundle becomes unpurchasable. Delist the bundle first, or swap the contents.
- **Circular relationships** — Partner Center prevents most of these, but a hand-crafted product-group setup can occasionally hit this. Contact your Microsoft representative if you get stuck.
