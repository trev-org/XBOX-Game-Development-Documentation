Microsoft Store entitlements are not always one-license-per-account. Understanding the sharing model helps you predict what `XStoreQueryGameLicense` and `XStoreQueryAddOnLicensesAsync` will return in real households.

## Home Xbox

Every Microsoft account can designate exactly one console as its **Home Xbox**. On that console:

- Anyone signed in can play the digital games the Home-Xbox account owns.
- The Home-Xbox account itself can play those games on other consoles when signed in.

At runtime, this presents as an active license without the current user being the license owner. `licenseXuid` (on `XStoreGameLicense` and `XStoreAddonLicense`) tells you which account granted the entitlement.

## Family sharing (Microsoft family group)

Adults and children in a Microsoft family group can share purchases when the family setting is enabled. This works across accounts, not devices — the shared license appears wherever the family member signs in.

Family membership can change dynamically. Register for `XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost` and be ready to re-gate content.

## Xbox Play Anywhere

A Play Anywhere title purchased on Xbox is entitled on PC (and vice versa) for the same Microsoft account. `XStoreQueryGameLicense` returns an active license on either device.

Requirements:

- Product configured as Play Anywhere in Partner Center.
- `MicrosoftGame.config` on both console and PC targets uses the same StoreId.
- The account is the same across devices.

## Xbox Game Pass

Game Pass is a subscription that grants entitlements for the catalog. At runtime the game license is active while the subscription is; when the subscription lapses or the title leaves the catalog, `XStoreRegisterGameLicenseChanged` fires with an inactive license.

From services, detect Game Pass with the [Detecting Xbox Game Pass subscription access](/publishing/xstore-detecting-game-pass) flow.

## Disc licenses

On console, `XStoreGameLicense::isDiscLicense` is `true` when the entitlement is granted by an inserted retail disc. If the disc is ejected mid-session, the license flips to inactive.

## Trials

A trial license is granted to the individual account that acquired the trial — it is **not shareable** through Home Xbox or family. `isTrialOwnedByThisUser` distinguishes trial owners from other users on the device.

## Refunds and clawback

When a customer refunds a purchase, the entitlement is revoked. On the client this surfaces as license-lost; on the service side, use the [clawback endpoints](/publishing/xstore-rest-clawback-v1) to reconcile against granted in-game rewards.

## Design implications

- Do not tie irrevocable in-game grants to a **single observation** of a client-side license. Reconcile server-side.
- Do not assume `XUserGetId(user) == licenseXuid`. Home Xbox and family sharing routinely produce mismatches.
- Do not lock content permanently on license-lost — re-query on next sign-in; the license may return.
