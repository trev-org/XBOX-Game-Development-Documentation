A **game concept** is a formal proposal you submit to Microsoft describing a title you intend to publish on XBOX. Concept approval is a **prerequisite** to creating a product in Partner Center for that title — you cannot skip it.

<Note>
  Every new title needs its own concept, including sequels, remasters, and platform ports of a previously approved title. Add-ons and DLC for an already-approved title do not need a new concept.
</Note>

## What to include

A concept submission covers:

- **Working title** and any sub-title / edition names.
- **Genre and elevator pitch** — a short description of core gameplay, mood, and target audience.
- **Target device families** — Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PC.
- **Content overview** — violence, sexual content, controlled substances, UGC, communication features, gambling-like mechanics. This feeds age rating planning downstream.
- **Monetization model** — premium, free-to-play, subscription, ad-supported.
- **Launch window** and target markets.
- **Team background** — studio, prior shipped titles, engine, third-party middleware.
- **Reference materials** — pitch deck, screenshots, trailer if available.

## Submission workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Concepts in Partner Center">
    Navigate to **Games → Concepts** in Partner Center and start a new concept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the concept form">
    Provide every field listed above. Attach a pitch deck if you have one — richer submissions get faster and more useful feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Microsoft's team reviews concepts for policy fit, business viability, and pipeline capacity. Typical review time is **2–4 weeks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval or follow-up">
    On approval you can create the product in Partner Center. If Microsoft has questions or requests changes, you'll be contacted directly — iterate and resubmit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After concept approval

- Create the product in Partner Center via [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup).
- Plan your commercial structure in [SKU planning](/publishing/sku-planning).
- If you're an early-in-development title, evaluate [XBOX Game Preview](/publishing/xbox-game-preview) as a pre-full-release option.
