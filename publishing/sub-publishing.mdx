**Sub-publishing** is a formal arrangement where one Microsoft partner (the **publisher**) ships a title on behalf of another partner (the **developer**). The publisher takes on Store-facing responsibility while the developer retains creative ownership.

## When sub-publishing applies

- A developer without their own Partner Center account or Microsoft agreement contracts an established publisher.
- A studio wants a publisher partner for marketing, localization, or global reach.
- A title changes publisher between concept and release, or between one release and the next.

## What it changes

| Concern | Standard (self-publishing) | Sub-publishing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner Center product owner | Developer | **Publisher** — the product lives in the publisher's Partner Center account |
| Store listing publisher line | Developer | Publisher (with developer named as "Developed by") |
| Payouts | To the developer's account | To the publisher; the publisher settles with the developer per their contract |
| Concept approval | Developer submits | Publisher submits on the developer's behalf |
| Support relationship | Developer's Microsoft representative | Publisher's Microsoft representative |

## Setting up a sub-publishing arrangement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contract with your counterparty">
    Sign a commercial contract between developer and publisher covering revenue split, IP ownership, support obligations, and exit terms. Microsoft is not party to this contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify Microsoft">
    Contact the publisher's Microsoft representative to declare the sub-publishing arrangement. Microsoft may request the contract for verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the concept under the publisher's account">
    The publisher submits the [game concept](/publishing/game-concepts) referencing the developer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Partner Center access">
    In the publisher's Partner Center, grant the developer team appropriate roles on the product so they can upload packages, configure XBOX services, and manage packages. See [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing publishers on an existing title

If a title needs to move from one publisher to another (acquisition, publisher change, contract expiry) that is a **reparenting** operation. See [Reparenting](/publishing/reparenting). Reparenting is not self-service and requires Microsoft involvement.
