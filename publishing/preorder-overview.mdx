A **pre-order** is a purchase transaction executed before a product's release date. The customer pays now (or has the transaction authorized) and receives the entitlement on release.

For the click-by-click of configuring pre-orders see [Pre-orders](/publishing/how-to-pre-orders). This page covers the lifecycle and rules.

## Pre-order lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-order opens">
    On the pre-order start date, the buy button flips to "Pre-order" on the PDP. The pre-order price is charged (or authorized, depending on payment method).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-order window">
    Customers can pre-order at any point in the window. Late pre-orderers get the same content as early pre-orderers unless a pre-order bonus was time-limited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-load window (optional)">
    24–48 hours before release, pre-orderers can download the package so it's ready to play at unlock. See [Package management](/publishing/how-to-package-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release / unlock">
    At the release UTC time, the entitlement unlocks. Pre-loaded customers can play immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-release">
    Pre-order becomes a standard purchase. The pre-order price becomes the base price unless you configured a launch-week sale.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules and guarantees

### Price guarantee

Microsoft guarantees the pre-order customer the **lowest price** the product carries between pre-order and release. If you drop the price at any point before release, existing pre-orderers automatically get the lower price (and are refunded the difference for already-charged purchases).

### Refund window

Pre-orders are refundable **any time before release** without needing to justify. After release, the standard Microsoft Store refund policy applies (usually 14 days and under 2 hours of playtime).

### Payment timing

- **Credit / debit card** — charged at pre-order time.
- **Microsoft account balance / gift card** — deducted at pre-order time.
- **Xbox Game Pass Ultimate members** — pre-order still requires payment; Game Pass membership does not include pre-order titles.

### Communication

Partner Center sends automated emails to pre-orderers at:

- Pre-order confirmation.
- Pre-load available (if you enabled a pre-load window).
- Release / unlock.
- Refund processed (if refunded).

You cannot customize these emails, but pre-orderers can be reached via your XBOX Live notification pipeline for in-game announcements after release.

## Pre-order bonuses

A **pre-order bonus** is content granted only to customers who purchased during the pre-order window.

- Configure the bonus as a hidden token-only add-on (see [Visibility](/publishing/how-to-visibility)).
- Bundle it with the pre-order (see [Pre-order unlock bundle](/publishing/how-to-pre-orders)).
- Grant the entitlement on release. Customers who buy post-release do not receive the bonus.

### Time-limited bonuses

Some campaigns offer a bonus for the first N days of pre-order. This is high-friction to configure — typically requires a separate pre-order SKU for the bonus window that stops accepting orders and transitions to the standard pre-order SKU. Coordinate with your Microsoft representative for large-scale time-limited campaigns.

## Cancellation and delays

### You delay the release

If you slip the release date, existing pre-orderers keep their pre-order. Send communication (via your marketing channels) and update the Store listing's release date. Pre-orderers get an updated confirmation email.

### You cancel the release

If a product is cancelled outright, refund all pre-orders through your Microsoft representative. Do not simply delist — that leaves pre-orders in an ambiguous state.
