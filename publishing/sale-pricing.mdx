A **sale** is a time-boxed price override on a product. Sales appear on the Store PDP with the base price struck through and the sale price displayed, plus a countdown to the sale end.

## Sale types

| Type | How it's priced | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Percentage sale** | "25% off" applied to the base price per market. | Portfolio-wide sales, seasonal events. |
| **Fixed price sale** | Hard price per market (e.g. `$29.99` regardless of base price). | Anchoring a specific price point. |
| **Publisher sale** | A group of products sold together as a publisher event. | Publisher spotlights, franchise sales. |
| **Platform sale** | Microsoft-run cross-catalog sales (Winter Sale, Summer Sale). Publishers opt in per product. | Tent-pole platform moments. |

## Eligibility rules

- Base game must have been at its **base price** for a **minimum period** before a sale can display a "was / now" price. This varies by market and typically ranges 30–90 days.
- Consecutive sales without a base-price gap between them fail eligibility in some markets — particularly the EU and Australia.
- Some markets (Germany, France, Australia) enforce **anti-doubling** rules: you cannot advertise a discount off a price that was itself a recent discount.
- Sale pricing must not go below Microsoft's minimum price for the market (varies; typically \$0.99 USD equivalent).

<Warning>
  Repeated flash sales trigger consumer-protection reviews in some markets. If you plan aggressive discounting, coordinate with your Microsoft representative on the market plan.
</Warning>

## Configure a sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability → Sales">
    Navigate to the product's Sales section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new sale">
    Provide a sale name (customer-facing tag, e.g. "Launch Week") and choose Percentage, Fixed, Publisher, or Platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the window">
    Start and end UTC dates. Time zones affect customer experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the discount">
    Percentage or fixed price per market. Percentage sales automatically calculate per-market sale price from that market's base price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish through Review and publish">
    Sales publish via [Review & publish](/publishing/modular-submission). No cert required. Live within minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Coordinating with launches

### Launch-week sale

A launch-week sale (small discount for the first 7 days) is common. Configure it to start at the same UTC time as the release date so pre-order customers aren't retroactively cheaper. Communicate the launch-week discount clearly in marketing so day-one pre-orderers understand the value.

### Post-launch anchor sale

A larger sale 60–90 days after launch acts as a re-engagement moment for customers who wishlisted but didn't buy. Combine with a content-update announcement or new DLC drop.

### Sale \+ DLC drop

When shipping DLC or a Season Pass, running a base-game sale simultaneously boosts DLC attach rate. Common: base-game 30% off, Season Pass 20% off, for a 7–10 day window.

## Sales on add-ons and bundles

- Add-ons can go on sale independently of the base game.
- Bundles can go on sale, but the sale applies to the bundle price — constituent products are unaffected.
- Season Passes can go on sale, but the constituent DLC add-ons don't inherit the discount.
