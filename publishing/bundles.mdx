A **bundle** is a Store product that grants entitlements to two or more constituent products at a single, typically discounted, price. Bundles are their own product type in Partner Center — distinct from Season Passes (which are add-ons) and from Product Groups (which are ownership dedup logic).

## When to use a bundle

- Ship a **launch bundle** combining base game \+ Season Pass at a premium price.
- Package **catalog cross-sells**: two older titles offered together at a discount to drive interest.
- Offer a **franchise pack**: every title in the franchise sold together.
- Provide a **cross-gen or cross-platform bundle** combining the Xbox and PC versions before Play Anywhere was configured (rare today).

## Bundle rules

- Every referenced product must be **published** and **purchasable** for the bundle to publish.
- Bundle price does not have to equal the sum of its parts — discount is expected.
- A customer who already owns one of the referenced products sees a **completion price** offer (bundle price minus already-owned components).
- Bundles do not carry their own age rating; they inherit the **most restrictive** rating of their contents.

## Configuring a bundle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new bundle product">
    In Partner Center, **Games → New product → Bundle**. Reserve a name (see [Game name](/publishing/game-name)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contents">
    In [Product relationships](/publishing/product-relationships), add the constituent products as **bundle contents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Store listing and pricing">
    Add [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing) copy that explains what the bundle contains. Set [Pricing](/publishing/pricing) per market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Bundles do not need Final Cert (no package), but they do go through Store review for listing accuracy. Submit via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bundle variants

- **Standard bundle** — the most common shape described above.
- **Pre-order bundle** — a bundle configured for pre-order with a specific pre-order incentive. See [Pre-release](/publishing/pre-release).
- **Early access bundle** — a bundle where one component grants earlier access than others (for example a Deluxe edition with 3-day early access plus DLC).
- **Pre-order unlock bundle** — unlocks a bonus product on pre-order.

See the pre-order how-tos under [Partner Center how-to](/publishing/partner-center-how-to) for each variant.
