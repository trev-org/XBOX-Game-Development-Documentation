**Optional Certification** is an advisory XR pass Microsoft's test team runs against a near-final build. It surfaces the same failures you'd see in Final Cert but does **not publish** and does **not block your Final Cert submission** if it fails.

## When to use Optional Cert

- **Confidence check** 4–6 weeks before your Final Cert target when the build feels close but you want an outside test pass.
- **New team's first submission** — flush out the process end-to-end without launch pressure.
- **Cross-platform coverage** — confirm PC and Console pass the same XR bar with the same build.
- **After a major refactor** — validate no regressions before betting a Final Cert slot on the change.
- **Ahead of a large content update** — catch regressions in a shipping title's live-ops update before committing to a recert or [Cert Bypass](/publishing/cert-bypass-program) submission.

## When Optional Cert is **not** worth it

- The build has known BVT failures — fix those first (see [Console BVT](/publishing/console-bvt-guide) and [PC BVT](/publishing/pc-bvt-guide)).
- You have less than one full cert cycle before your Final Cert deadline — the Optional Cert result won't come back in time to act on.
- You've already passed a recent Final Cert and are shipping a small metadata-only change.

## How Optional Cert differs from Final Cert

| Aspect | Optional Cert | Final Cert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sandbox | Development sandbox | RETAIL sandbox |
| Publishes on pass | No | Yes (or holds for manual publish) |
| XR bar | Full XR suite | Full XR suite (same tests) |
| Cost / queue slot | Consumes a cert slot | Consumes a cert slot |
| SLA | 3–7 business days typical | 5–14 business days typical |
| FMA delivered on fail | Yes | Yes |
| Blocks your title on fail | No | Yes |

## Submitting an Optional Cert

The submission flow is identical to [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification) — you just choose **Optional Certification** as the submission type in Partner Center. Everything else (Supplemental Info, Packages, Branches, sandbox choice) works the same.

<Note>
  Treat the Optional Cert [Supplemental info](/publishing/supplemental-info) as seriously as a Final Cert one. Missing information causes the same intake returns and eats your Optional Cert window.
</Note>
