This guide covers game publishing for XBOX and how to use [Microsoft Partner Center](https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard) to create, manage, and publish your XBOX products to the Microsoft Store.

<Note>
  If there's any conflict between this documentation and an agreement you signed with Microsoft, **the agreement governs**.
</Note>

## Who this guide is for

Creators registered with Microsoft for XBOX game development and publishing, including [ID@Xbox](/onboarding) members.

If you're publishing an app (not an XBOX game) or aren't a Managed Creator, follow [Get started with the Microsoft Store](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish) instead.

## What Partner Center does

Partner Center is the web portal for all Microsoft Store product configuration and publishing — games, bundles, add-ons.

In Partner Center you'll:

- Create your game product and reserve a name.
- Configure XBOX services (achievements, multiplayer, leaderboards).
- Upload packages for each supported platform.
- Complete age ratings, Store listing metadata, and pricing.
- Submit for **Certification**.
- Publish to the Microsoft Store.

After launch, Partner Center also lets you:

- Respond to ratings and reviews.
- Flight packages to specific audiences.
- Access reports on product health and customer engagement.

## Team roles

| Team | Typical Partner Center work |
| --- | --- |
| **Management & publishing** | Pricing, release markets, release dates, product metadata, Store listing info, age ratings, sales monitoring |
| **Creative** | Art assets, Store listing imagery, promotional materials |
| **Development** | XBOX services config, publish packages to sandboxes, create add-ons, upload packages for cert and release |

## End-to-end publishing flow

The Publishing tab is organized to match the actual order you'll do the work. Follow it top to bottom for a new title, or jump into a section for an update or a specific task.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get started">
    Set up your Partner Center account, roles, and payout details in [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your product">
    Configure the product itself: [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup), [Packages](/publishing/packages), [Properties](/publishing/properties), [Store listing](/publishing/store-listing), [Ratings](/publishing/ratings), [Pricing](/publishing/pricing), [Add-ons](/publishing/add-ons), and [Franchise hubs](/publishing/franchise-hubs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up commerce">
    Choose product types and configure entitlements with [XStore deep dive](/publishing/xstore-deep-dive), and integrate the [XStore S2S APIs](/publishing/xstore-server-to-service) if you need server-side entitlement checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before submitting">
    Isolate builds and services state with [Sandboxes](/publishing/sandboxes) and [Branches](/publishing/branches). Flight to opt-in audiences with the [XBOX Insider Program](/publishing/xbox-insider-program). Prepare marketing with [Pre-release](/publishing/pre-release).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certify">
    Understand the process in [Certification](/publishing/certification), work through [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification), and address any failures with the [XR FMA](/publishing/xr-fma) guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship discs (if applicable)">
    Complete [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging), pass [Disc Certification](/publishing/certification), then [RTM](/publishing/approve-discs-for-manufacture) and [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and operate">
    Confirm the [Readiness checklist](/publishing/readiness-checklist), [Publish to RETAIL](/publishing/publish-to-retail), and manage the ongoing product lifecycle in [Content updates](/publishing/content-updates).
  </Step>
</Steps>
