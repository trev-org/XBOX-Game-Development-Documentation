A **hidden and purchasable** product is one that's not visible in Store search, browse, or recommendations — but any customer with the direct PDP URL can buy it. Think of it as an "unlisted" product.

## When to use this pattern

- **Private industry demo access** — you send a URL to a journalist or reviewer and they buy directly.
- **Soft launch validation** — test purchase and download flows on retail with a specific audience before mass discovery.
- **B2B / enterprise distribution** — education or enterprise customers buy via a direct URL surfaced through your sales team.
- **Marketing campaign landing pages** — a marketing site links directly to the PDP URL; the product doesn't clutter Store browse.

<Note>
  This is different from a **hidden token-only** product, which requires a promo code to purchase. Hidden and purchasable products are open to anyone with the URL; no code required.
</Note>

## Configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the product">
    Create as a normal game, add-on, or bundle product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Store listing, Pricing, Ratings as usual">
    Hidden products still need a complete PDP for the customer who lands on it. Age ratings, screenshots, description — everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Visibility = Hidden">
    In [Visibility & audiences](/publishing/how-to-visibility), set Visibility to Hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Purchasable = Yes">
    Purchasable stays enabled. This is the differentiator from a wishlist / coming-soon page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the URL is stable">
    The Store PDP URL is `https://www.xbox.com/games/store/<slug>/<product-id>`. Confirm it resolves before distributing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). The product is now live but not discoverable through Store surfaces.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Certification and ratings

Hidden and purchasable products still go through [Certification](/publishing/certification) with the same XR bar as visible products. Real customers are buying and playing, so the compliance bar doesn't change based on discoverability.

Age ratings still apply for every market you enable. The Store PDP does not skip rating enforcement just because the product is unlisted — customers in age-gated markets still get the age check.

## Making a hidden product visible later

A soft-launched hidden product usually graduates to a fully visible retail product. To flip:

1. Set Visibility from Hidden to Visible.
2. Publish via Review and publish.
3. Within minutes to hours, the product starts appearing in Store search and browse.

Existing purchasers keep their entitlements. New buyers can now discover the product organically.
