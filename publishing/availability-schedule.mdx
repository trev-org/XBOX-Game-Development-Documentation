An **availability schedule** lets you set precise UTC dates for the lifecycle events of your product per market. Rather than manually flipping visibility, pre-order, and price at launch time, you script the launch and let Partner Center execute.

## Lifecycle events you can schedule

| Event | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Visibility start** | Product page becomes visible on the Store. |
| **Pre-order start** | Pre-order button becomes active. |
| **Purchase / release date** | Full purchase / download / unlock becomes active. |
| **Sale start / end** | Sale price applies within this window. |
| **Availability end** | Product becomes hidden or non-purchasable. Existing owners retain the entitlement. |

## Configuring the schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability → Schedule">
    Navigate to the schedule module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule per market">
    You can use a single global schedule or override per market. Common pattern: global schedule with Australia / New Zealand offset earlier to launch "first" for that region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use UTC">
    All schedule dates are in **UTC**. Convert carefully for your target markets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the schedule">
    Publish through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). The schedule is stored; Partner Center executes the events at the specified UTC times.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Coordinating with cert

A scheduled release only works if the associated packages are already Final Cert-passed and staged in Partner Center. Order of operations:

1. Pass Final Cert with **Hold for manual publish** or **Scheduled** release option (see [Publish to RETAIL](/publishing/publish-to-retail)).
2. Configure the availability schedule.
3. Confirm the schedule matches the cert-selected release date.
4. At the scheduled UTC time, Partner Center automatically publishes the packages and flips availability.

## Common scheduling patterns

### Global simultaneous launch

Set the same UTC purchase-release date across every market. This creates a single global street date. Downside: not aligned to any specific local time zone; some markets get an odd-hour launch.

### Rolling regional launch

Schedule Australia / NZ first (typically 5 hours ahead of North America launch), then Asia, then Europe, then Americas. Feels like a natural "midnight launch" per region but requires careful coordination if you have a global marketing moment.

### Region-specific soft launch

Availability starts in one or two markets (typically Australia or a Nordic country) weeks before global. Used for validating monetization, matchmaking scale, and localization at real customer volumes.

### Pre-order \+ release \+ sale on day one

Schedule: pre-order start today → release date in 3 months → launch-week sale from release date \+ 7 days for one week. All configured up front.

### End-of-life delist

Set an availability-end date for a retiring product. Combined with a farewell communication window, this gives customers time to buy before the product is delisted.
