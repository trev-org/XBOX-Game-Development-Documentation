A **product group** is a Partner Center concept that ties together multiple products representing different editions, generations, or purchase paths of the same underlying title. Getting product groups right ensures customers don't accidentally re-buy the same game, upgrade paths work, and the Store presents your family of SKUs coherently.

## What a product group does

- **Dedupes ownership** — a customer who owns Standard Edition sees Deluxe Edition presented as an **upgrade**, not a fresh purchase.
- **Enables Smart Delivery** — lets the Store deliver the correct generation's package (Xbox One vs Xbox Series X|S) to a single owned SKU.
- **Powers cross-buy** — with [Play Anywhere](/publishing/managed-programs), a single purchase entitles both Xbox and PC.
- **Governs upgrade offers** — the "Upgrade to Deluxe for \$X" offer only appears to customers in the same product group.

## Members of a group

A product group typically contains:

- **Standard Edition** (base game)
- **Deluxe / Ultimate Edition** (base game \+ content)
- **Cross-gen versions** if you ship as separate products rather than Smart Delivery
- **Play Anywhere companion** if applicable
- **Upgrade add-ons** that let a Standard owner move to Deluxe

Bundles, season passes, and independent DLC are **not** members of the product group — they are separate products that reference members of the group.

## Configuring a product group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the first product">
    Create your first SKU (usually Standard Edition) via [Game setup](/publishing/game-setup). At creation, this product implicitly forms its own product group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add subsequent editions to the same group">
    When you create additional editions, choose **Add to existing product group** and select the Standard Edition. This ensures Partner Center treats them as related.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm relationships">
    Open the product group view under **Games → Product groups** and confirm each product's role (base, edition, upgrade, cross-gen).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test upgrade paths in a sandbox">
    Buy Standard on a test account in your development sandbox, then confirm the Deluxe upgrade offer appears at the correct price. See [Sandboxes](/publishing/sandboxes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot move a product between product groups after it has customer entitlements. Confirm the group at creation.
</Warning>
