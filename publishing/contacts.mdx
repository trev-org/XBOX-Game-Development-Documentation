XBOX publishing is a relationship-driven process. Knowing which Microsoft contact owns which question saves days of back-and-forth.

## Contact map

| Role | What they own | When to reach out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business / Account Manager** | Commercial relationship: contracts, addendums, curated program eligibility (Game Pass, Play Anywhere), price tier approvals, marketing consideration. | Contract questions, program applications, escalations that affect commercial terms. |
| **Portfolio / Publishing Manager** | Ongoing portfolio management: concept approvals, roadmap alignment, release date coordination. | New concept submissions, launch timing conflicts, curated program discussions. |
| **ID@Xbox contact** | For ID@Xbox members — the equivalent of the account manager plus portfolio manager. | Any publishing-related question if you're ID@Xbox. |
| **Certification lead** | Cert-cycle questions, deviation requests, FMA interpretation. | During and after a cert submission. |
| **Technical Account Manager (TAM)** | Available for enterprise-tier publishers — technical escalation across GDK, XBOX services, and publishing. | Cross-team technical blockers. |
| **Disc production contact** | Disc addendum, artwork review, royalty tiers, manufacturing scheduling. | Any disc-specific question. |
| **Partner Center support** | Tooling issues, account access, self-service ticketing. | Partner Center bugs, unblocking a stuck submission, account access issues. |

## How to find your contact

Your primary Microsoft contact is listed in Partner Center under **Account settings → Contacts**. If no primary contact is listed:

- **ID@Xbox members** — email the ID@Xbox alias with your Partner Center account ID.
- **Managed Creators outside ID@Xbox** — the concept-approval email chain from your first title contains your portfolio contact.
- **New publishers** — [Partner Center support](https://partner.microsoft.com/support) can route to the correct team.

## Escalation path

Standard escalation path when something is blocking:

1. Post the question to your account manager or ID@Xbox contact.
2. If not resolved within 2–3 business days, escalate to your account manager's manager.
3. For cert-cycle emergencies, add the certification lead in cc.
4. For Partner Center tooling issues, always file a Partner Center support ticket in parallel — the ticket number gives you an audit trail.
