Add-on **bundles** and **season passes** are Partner Center products that grant a collection of child add-ons on purchase. They share the same product type; the difference is intent — bundles are usually static, season passes usually grow over time.

## Configure the bundle or season pass

1. In Partner Center under the base product, open **Add-ons**.
2. Create a new add-on with product type **Add-on bundle**.
3. Add child add-ons that already exist in the same base product.
4. For a **season pass**, publish an empty (or partially filled) bundle at launch; add child items as new DLC ships. Existing owners automatically receive the new children.
5. Set price, markets, and Store listing; publish to sandbox and (eventually) RETAIL.

<Warning>
  Once a customer owns the bundle, you cannot remove items that were part of the bundle at the time of their purchase. Season-pass owners always keep everything included as of their acquisition.
</Warning>

## Runtime: detect the children

Buying a bundle grants an entitlement per child. `XStoreQueryAddOnLicensesAsync` returns one license per child; you don't have to know that the entitlement came from a bundle:

```cpp
XStoreQueryAddOnLicensesAsync(context, async);
// Enumerate licenses; each maps to a child StoreId
```

To detect the **bundle itself** (for example, to show "You own the Season Pass" in UI), use `XStoreQueryEntitledProductsAsync` and filter for the bundle's StoreId.

## Storefront UX guidance

- Show the bundle price and the sum-of-individuals price so customers see the discount.
- If the customer already owns some children, use `XStoreQueryEntitledProductsAsync` to compute the effective discount and offer to fill only the missing items (or communicate the value clearly).
- For season passes, list the items included today and the items coming in future updates.
