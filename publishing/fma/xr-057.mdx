[XR-057 reference](/publishing/xr/xr-057) is the reference.

## Common FMA text patterns

- **"Base achievement \[X\] cannot be earned without DLC \[Y\]"** — pay-to-earn on the base.
- **"Base gamerscore total includes achievements only unlockable with add-on"** — misattributed.

## Fixing

- Move DLC-specific achievements to the **DLC achievement schema**, not base.
- Rebalance base achievements to be earnable on the base game only.
- Update Store metadata's gamerscore total accordingly.
