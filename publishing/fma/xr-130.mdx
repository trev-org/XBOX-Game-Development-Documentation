### 130-05 Compatibility Mode

| Scenario | Severity | Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No current scenario. | Not Relevant (-4) |  |
| No current scenario. | Very Minor (1) |  |
| No current scenario. | Minor (2) |  |
| No current scenario. | Moderate (6) |  |
| The title doesn't allow users to enter, play, or complete a multiplayer session between the same console generation or cross generation when supported. | Critical (12) | Xbox One, Xbox Series |
| The title is built on GDK and doesn't function as expected when quick resumed on an Xbox Series X\|S. | Critical (12) | Xbox One, Xbox Series |
| The title doesn't retain a user's saved data after a full title reboot on an Xbox Series X\|S. | Critical (12) | Xbox One, Xbox Series |
| The title doesn't roam a user's saved data generated on a console from the Xbox One family of devices to an Xbox Series X\|S (and back) without a loss in data. | Critical (12) | Xbox One, Xbox Series |
| The title doesn't suspend or resume as expected on an Xbox Series X\|S. | Critical (12) | Xbox One, Xbox Series |
| No current scenario. | Catastrophic (22) |  |

**Notes**

- Titles which support multiplayer functionality are expected to, and MUST, work within the same console generation (that is, Series X|S must work together and consoles from the Xbox One family of devices must work together). Cross generational functionality is OPTIONAL.
- By design, all ERA titles are capped at 60fps on Gen9 devices. ERA\+ titles may request to go to 120hz within the manifest to increase this limit.
