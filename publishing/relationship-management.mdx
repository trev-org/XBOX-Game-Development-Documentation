**Relationship management** is the Partner Center area where you declare and manage business relationships between your account and other partners: developers you sub-publish for, publishers who sub-publish for you, and third-party vendors you grant access to.

## Types of relationships

| Relationship | You are… | Other party is… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Publisher-of** | The publisher | A developer whose title you publish |
| **Developer-of** | The developer | Your publisher |
| **Test vendor access** | The publisher / developer | An authorized [test vendor](/publishing/test-vendors) |
| **Localization vendor** | The publisher / developer | A localization vendor with product-scoped access |
| **Franchise partner** | Publisher A | Publisher B in a shared franchise (see [Franchise hubs](/publishing/franchise-hubs)) |

## Setting up a relationship

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account settings → Relationship management">
    In Partner Center, open **Account settings → Relationship management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the counterparty">
    Enter the other party's Partner Center account identifier and the relationship type. Partner Center sends an invitation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Counterparty accepts">
    The invited party accepts from their Partner Center. The relationship is now active but scoped to no products yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope the relationship to products">
    On each product you want to include, add the counterparty in [Product relationships](/publishing/product-relationships) or the product's access list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ending a relationship

Relationships can be ended by either party from **Relationship management**. Ending a relationship:

- Removes the counterparty's access to any products scoped to the relationship.
- Does **not** transfer product ownership — use [Reparenting](/publishing/reparenting) for that.
- Does not revoke customer entitlements or purchase history.
