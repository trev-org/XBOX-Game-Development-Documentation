**Publisher Insights** is the analytics surface in Partner Center. Every published product produces sales, engagement, and health telemetry that publishers use to monitor launch, tune marketing, plan updates, and forecast revenue.

## Available reports

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Acquisitions** | Purchase count by market, day, edition, and source (organic, promo code, Game Pass, bundle). |
| **Wishlist / follows** | Wishlist adds, follower count, conversion rate from wishlist to purchase. Useful pre-launch. |
| **Engagement** | Monthly active users (MAU), daily active users (DAU), average session length, sessions per user. |
| **Retention** | D1, D7, D30 retention. Critical for live-service titles. |
| **Revenue** | Gross revenue, refunds, net revenue by market and edition. Segmented by base game, add-ons, and bundles. |
| **Game Pass telemetry** | For Game Pass titles: subscriber engagement, install count, hours played. |
| **Health** | Crash rate, hang rate, and other stability signals. Feed post-launch patch prioritization. |
| **Store listing performance** | PDP impressions, conversion rate from PDP to purchase, and A/B test results (if configured). |

## Access and roles

Access to Insights is role-gated. In [Developer account](/publishing/developer-account) you assign roles:

- **Analytics viewer** — can view Insights but not other Partner Center modules.
- **Manager / Publisher** — view everything.
- **Developer** — no Insights access by default; must be explicitly granted.

## Data latency

| Report | Latency |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisitions | Same-day (updated hourly) |
| Engagement / retention | Next-day (T-1) |
| Revenue | Next-day for gross; monthly reconciliation for payout-ready net |
| Health / crash rate | Same-day (typically 1–2 hour lag) |
| Store listing performance | Next-day |

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my acquisition count higher than my revenue count?

Promo code redemptions, Game Pass installs, and family library shares all count as acquisitions but produce no direct revenue. This is expected. Segment by acquisition source to reconcile.

### Why do my Steam/other-platform numbers not match?

Insights only covers the Microsoft Store on XBOX and PC. Cross-platform totals need to be built from the platform-specific reports.

### Why is engagement lower on Series S than Series X?

Series S has a lower installed base and can also indicate memory-constrained gameplay issues. Check crash rate and average session length on Series S specifically.

### Can I export raw data?

Most reports support CSV export from the report page. For programmatic access, the [Partner Center Analytics API](https://learn.microsoft.com/partner-center/developer/) covers a subset of reports.

### How do refunds appear?

A refund is a negative acquisition \+ negative revenue on the day the refund is processed, not the day of the original purchase. This can create apparent day-over-day swings for high-refund titles.

### Are health signals privacy-safe?

Crash and hang telemetry is aggregated and does not include per-user identifiers. Individual crash dumps for cert-time repro are attached to FMAs — not to Insights.
