**Price adjustment preference** controls how Partner Center handles the per-market prices of your product when Microsoft updates the underlying price tiers or when exchange rates shift materially.

## Why this exists

Microsoft manages a set of standard **price tiers** per market (for example, tier `US_2999` maps to `$29.99 USD` in the US market, and to the equivalent local currency prices in every other market). Occasionally Microsoft updates these tier tables:

- Currency exchange rates shift and the local-currency price no longer matches the intended USD anchor.
- Microsoft adds new markets or restructures existing tiers.
- Regional pricing floors or ceilings change.

When this happens, each product's per-market price could either stay pinned to the old local-currency value or automatically reflect the new tier. Publishers choose which behavior they prefer.

## The two modes

| Mode | Behavior when Microsoft updates price tiers |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto-adjust (recommended)** | Per-market prices automatically update to the new tier-equivalent. USD anchor stays constant; local-currency prices track it. |
| **Manual only** | Per-market prices stay at their last-configured local-currency value regardless of tier changes. You must update prices manually. |

### When to choose auto-adjust

- Most commercial titles — keeps USD-equivalent revenue predictable.
- Titles with prices in many markets — avoids manual maintenance overhead.
- Titles that have deliberate USD-anchor pricing (e.g. "we ship at \$29.99 USD equivalent in every market").

### When to choose manual

- Titles with hand-tuned regional pricing that reflects deliberate market-level positioning ("we're \$19.99 in Brazil regardless of USD exchange").
- Titles under active regulatory review in a specific market where automatic changes could complicate the review.
- Legacy titles where the current per-market prices need to be preserved exactly for historical reporting.

## Configuring

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing and availability → Pricing preferences">
    Navigate to the product's pricing preferences section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Auto or Manual">
    Set the preference per product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the preference via [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission). The next tier update will honor the new preference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Interaction with sales

Sales set explicit prices per market and are unaffected by price-adjustment preference. If a tier update happens **during** a sale, the sale price stays; the base price adjusts (or doesn't, per your preference) when the sale ends.

## Auditing

Under **Pricing history** you can see every price change per market with source (manual edit vs auto-adjust vs sale start/end). Useful for reconciling revenue reports and for regulatory audits.
