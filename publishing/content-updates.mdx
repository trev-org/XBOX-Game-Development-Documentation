After launch, most changes to your product still go through Partner Center and — with a few exceptions — still go through certification.

## What triggers a recert

| Change | Requires recert? |
| --- | --- |
| Package update | Yes — Final Cert against the delta, or [Cert Bypass](/publishing/cert-bypass-program) if qualifying |
| New add-on / DLC | Yes — cert against the add-on package |
| Store listing copy or screenshots | No cert, but Store team re-review |
| Pricing / availability change | No cert; Partner Center applies a propagation delay |
| Age rating change (new IARC answers) | Yes — re-issue IARC; for discs, re-submit longform where affected |
| Delist a market | No cert |

## Recommendations for testing in RETAIL

- Reserve retail-provisioned Xbox devices for post-launch validation. Never touch a dev sandbox.
- Keep a canary account in every major region for region-specific validation.
- Load-test multiplayer in RETAIL before and after every major content drop.
- Watch for `403` / `401` from XBOX services after a sandbox refresh — the most common early-life failure mode.

## Delisting

To remove a product from sale:

1. Open **Pricing and availability**.
2. Set visibility to **Hidden** or set availability to end.
3. Publish. Existing owners keep the product.

A delist is not a full removal. To fully remove re-download ability, contact your Microsoft representative.
