The **Properties** module in Partner Center configures product-wide metadata: categories, system requirements, support contacts, accessibility, and privacy policy.

## Key fields

- **Category / subcategory** — drives store browse placement.
- **System requirements** (PC only) — minimum and recommended.
- **Privacy policy URL** — required for any title that collects user data.
- **Support contact** — displayed on the store listing.
- **Accessibility features** — self-declared accessibility flags.
