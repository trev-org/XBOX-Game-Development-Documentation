For conceptual background on how disc releases work end-to-end see [Discs](/publishing/discs) and [Order discs](/publishing/order-discs). This how-to covers the click-by-click Partner Center flow for submitting and managing disc orders.

## Prerequisites

Before you can submit a disc order:

- Disc addendum executed with Microsoft (Discs module visible in your product).
- Disc image passed [Disc Certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification).
- Artwork approved (see [Disc packaging requirements](/publishing/disc-packaging)).
- Physical longform ratings issued for every region on the order (see [Ratings](/publishing/ratings)).
- Royalty tier submitted and approved (see [Submit royalty tiers](/publishing/how-to-royalty-tiers)).
- RTM sign-off complete (see [Approve discs for manufacture](/publishing/approve-discs-for-manufacture)).

## Submit a disc order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discs module">
    Navigate to your product's Discs module in Partner Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm RTM state">
    The order form is only enabled when the linked disc image and artwork are RTM'd. If Order is greyed out, resolve the RTM prerequisites first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a manufacturing order">
    Click **New order**. Provide an order name (for your internal tracking) and select the RTM'd disc image / artwork version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter per-region quantities">
    For each region you'll ship into, enter the quantity. Meet or exceed the region's minimum order quantity (MOQ) from your disc addendum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide ship-to destination">
    For each region, provide the destination warehouse address (typically your distributor's warehouse). Microsoft's replicator ships directly to the destination; Microsoft does not warehouse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm target arrival dates">
    The system suggests target arrival dates based on the current manufacturing schedule (typically 4–8 weeks from order acceptance). Confirm they meet your street date; if not, escalate to your Microsoft representative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the order">
    Submit for Microsoft acceptance. Orders are validated against contract terms (MOQ, royalty tier, artwork version). Approval is typically within 2–5 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track order status

Once accepted, the Discs module shows order status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Order received** | Order accepted, not yet scheduled. |
| **Scheduled** | Slot allocated with the replicator; production date set. |
| **In production** | Currently being pressed. |
| **Quality control** | Post-press QC pass. |
| **Shipped** | Left the replicator; en route to destination warehouse. |
| **Delivered** | Confirmed received at destination. |

## Manage an existing order

### Increase quantity

Before an order enters **In production**, you can increase quantities. After that, you'll need to submit a follow-on order (reprint) rather than modifying the existing one.

### Decrease quantity or cancel

Cancellation is possible before **Scheduled**. After scheduling, cancellation may incur fees per your disc addendum. Contact your Microsoft representative for cancellations after scheduling.

### Redirect ship-to

Up until **Shipped**, you can update the destination warehouse address. After shipping, physical redirection is between you and the shipper — Partner Center only tracks the original destination.

### Reprints

Reprints use the same RTM'd image and artwork. Submit a new manufacturing order referencing the same RTM version. No new cert or artwork review required.
