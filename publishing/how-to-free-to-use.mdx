A **free-to-use** title has a base price of `$0` in every market. Free-to-use isn't a special product type — it's a specific configuration of a standard game product. This how-to walks through it.

## Common free-to-use shapes

| Shape | Monetization |
| --- | --- |
| **Free-to-play live service** | Consumable currency, cosmetics, battle passes, ads (where allowed). |
| **Free base game \+ paid DLC** | Base game free, expansions and content packs paid. |
| **Free demo / standalone demo** | Not monetized; upsells to a paid full product. |
| **Free promotional release** | Time-limited free release (event, promotion) that later becomes paid. |
| **Ad-supported free** | Free with in-game advertising. Rare on XBOX due to policy constraints. |

## Configure a free-to-use title

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set price to $0 in every market">
    In [Pricing](/publishing/pricing), set the base price to `$0` USD equivalent for every market you release in. This automatically applies the Store's free-to-use presentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure add-ons for monetization">
    Create the add-ons that drive revenue. Consumables (currency, boosters) and durables (cosmetics, expansions) are the common shapes. See [Consumable add-ons](/publishing/consumable-add-ons) and [Durable add-ons](/publishing/durable-add-ons).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle refunds appropriately">
    A free base game can't be refunded (it costs \$0). Add-on purchases follow the standard 14-day Store refund policy. Design your economy assuming refunds happen — don't grant progression that can't be recovered from a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass certification against F2P XRs">
    Free-to-use titles pass the same [Certification](/publishing/certification) bar as paid titles. Some XRs are specifically about F2P (clear pricing in in-game store, no dark patterns that trick minors into purchases, clear communication of loot mechanics where applicable).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare age-sensitive mechanics">
    In [Ratings](/publishing/ratings), declare loot boxes, gacha mechanics, and any real-money gambling analogues accurately. Several markets require specific consumer warnings on free-to-use titles with these mechanics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free with ads

Ad-supported free titles have specific constraints on XBOX:

- Ads must not be shown to accounts flagged as **child** in Xbox family settings.
- No pop-up ads during gameplay in a way that interrupts input.
- No ads that impersonate system UI or purchase confirmation dialogs.
- Full ad-network disclosure in your privacy policy.

If you plan an ad-supported release, work with your Microsoft representative before submission — several ad SDKs are pre-approved; others require review.

## Free promotional release

A time-limited free release ("Free this weekend") is configured as a **sale** rather than a base price change:

1. Configure the sale in [Sale pricing](/publishing/sale-pricing) with a `$0` sale price for the specific window.
2. When the sale ends, the price reverts to the base price.
3. Customers who claimed during the free window **keep the entitlement forever** — this is the whole point of a free promotional release.
