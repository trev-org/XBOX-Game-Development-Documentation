XBOX certification verifies your title against the [XBOX Requirements (XRs)](/publishing/xbox-requirements) before it can ship on retail.

## Phases

1. **Pre-certification** — internal compliance pass against [Top failing tests](/publishing/xr-top-failing).
2. **Submission** — upload package via [Partner Center Packages](/publishing/packages).
3. **Certification** — Microsoft test team runs the XR suite (typically 5–10 business days).
4. **Failures & resubmission** — review the FMA, fix, resubmit.
5. **Release** — publish to retail or schedule a release date.

## Tips

- Run the SubmissionValidator locally before uploading.
- Validate on a retail-equivalent sandbox.
- Run a full multiplayer and DLC flow with [Sandboxes](/publishing/sandboxes).
