XBOX Certification is Microsoft's compliance and quality gate. Every package that ships on the Microsoft Store on XBOX — new titles, updates, add-ons, and discs — must pass Certification against the [XBOX Requirements (XRs)](/publishing/xbox-requirements) before it can go live in the RETAIL sandbox.

This page explains what certification is and how the process works end-to-end. For the practical, click-by-click submission steps, see [Submit to certification](/publishing/submit-to-certification).

**Current XR version:** Console 16.3 (July 1, 2026) / PC 6.0 (November 1, 2025). See [XR change history](/publishing/xr-change-history) for a summary of what changed since your last cert cycle.

## Submission types

Not every submission is the same. Choose the one that matches what you are trying to achieve:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Optional Certification" icon="flask">
    Advisory XR run against a near-final build to surface issues early. Does not publish.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Final Certification" icon="stamp">
    Mandatory pass required to publish to RETAIL. Uses your locked release-candidate package.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Disc Certification" icon="compact-disc" href="/publishing/discs">
    Certifies the exact image sent to the disc replicator. Runs alongside Final Cert.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Content Update / Recert" icon="rotate">
    Any change to a live product. Same XR bar as Final Cert against the delta.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cert Bypass" icon="forward" href="/publishing/cert-bypass-program">
    Qualifying live-service titles publish updates via self-attestation instead of a cert queue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="MGATS" icon="universal-access">
    Optional Microsoft Gaming Accessibility Testing pass. Advisory, does not gate publish.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The certification pipeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Package processing">
    Partner Center scans the uploaded package for structural issues, signs and stages it, and validates the manifest against your product configuration. Failures here are returned in minutes to a few hours and never enter the test queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-test intake">
    A test lead reviews your Supplemental Info, test accounts, and repro steps. If Supplemental Info is missing information the submission is returned before testing begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="XR testing">
    The Microsoft test team runs the applicable XR suite across every device family in the submission — Xbox Series X, Series S, Xbox One, and PC where declared. This includes stability, suspend/resume, account switching, network loss, multiplayer, achievements, privacy, DLC, and platform-specific rules (for example Series X|S generation rules and Smart Delivery).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result">
    - **Pass** — submission proceeds to Publishing (auto-publish, scheduled, or manual per your choice).
    - **Fail** — an [XR Failure Mode Analysis (FMA)](/publishing/xr-fma) is attached. Fix and resubmit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    On a passing Final Cert the package moves to `In the Store` at your configured release time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Timing expectations

<Warning>
  Turnaround times are **guidance, not a contractual SLA**. Actual cert time depends on title complexity, device families in scope, current queue depth, and seasonality (Q4 is measurably slower).
</Warning>

| Submission | Typical turnaround |
| --- | --- |
| Package processing | Minutes to a few hours |
| Optional Certification | 3–7 business days |
| Final Certification (single-platform, small delta) | 5–7 business days |
| Final Certification (multi-platform, large title) | 7–14 business days |
| Disc Certification | Runs in parallel with Final Cert |
| MGATS | 7–14 business days |
| Cert Bypass | Same-day to next-day (self-attestation, no queue) |

Plan your release date with a **buffer of at least one full cert cycle** to allow for a resubmission if the first pass fails.

## Sandboxes and certification

Cert always tests against one specific sandbox state. For Final Cert this is always the **RETAIL** sandbox. Optional Cert runs against your development sandbox. Make sure the XBOX services state (achievements, multiplayer session templates, Rich Presence, stats) is published to the sandbox you are certifying from — see [Sandboxes](/publishing/sandboxes).

## Reading an FMA

An FMA lists every failing XR with:

- **XR number and title** (for example `XR-047 — Achievement unlocks`).
- **Test case executed** and expected vs actual behavior.
- **Repro steps**, test account used, device family, and sandbox.
- **Attachments** — screenshots, video captures, and crash dumps where relevant.

Do not resubmit without addressing every failing XR. Partial fixes cause repeat failures and eat your cert buffer. See [XR FMA](/publishing/xr-fma) for a walkthrough.
