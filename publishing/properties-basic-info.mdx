**Basic info** is the first subsection of the [Properties](/publishing/properties) module. It covers the classification metadata that drives Store browse, filters, and legal disclosures.

## Fields

### Category and subcategory

Every product must declare a **primary category** and a **subcategory**. Categories drive the Store's browse experience and search filtering.

Common game categories include:

- **Action & adventure** — shooters, action RPG, hack-and-slash, metroidvania.
- **Card & board** — CCGs, tabletop adaptations.
- **Educational** — learning-focused titles.
- **Family & kids** — kid-friendly across genres.
- **Fighting** — competitive fighters.
- **Multi-genre** — titles that span multiple categories meaningfully.
- **Other** — titles that don't fit a defined category.
- **Platformer** — 2D and 3D platformers.
- **Puzzle & trivia** — logic games, trivia.
- **Racing & flying** — driving and flight sim.
- **Role playing** — CRPGs, JRPGs, tactical RPG.
- **Shooter** — FPS, TPS.
- **Simulation** — sandbox, tycoon, life sim.
- **Sports** — licensed and arcade sports.
- **Strategy** — RTS, turn-based strategy, 4X.
- **Word** — word games.

Category drives:

- Browse placement on the Xbox Store and PC Microsoft Store.
- Discovery filters (customers filtering "Shooter" see only titles categorized as such).
- Recommendation engine inputs.

### Product tags

Optional free-form tags (up to ~10) that surface as additional discovery signals. Examples: `co-op`, `souls-like`, `deckbuilder`, `roguelike`, `narrative`, `PvP`. Choose accurately; misused tags produce customer refunds and negative reviews.

### Support contact

- **Support email** or **support URL** displayed on the Store PDP.
- Must be a live, monitored address / URL. Cert checks this exists at submission time and periodically after publish.
- Consumer-protection law in some markets (EU, Australia) requires a functional support contact for every product on sale.

### Privacy policy URL

Required for any title that:

- Collects user data (even just Xbox Live gamertag beyond what the platform surfaces).
- Uses analytics SDKs.
- Sends telemetry to publisher-owned servers.
- Uses third-party ad networks.

The privacy policy URL must:

- Point to a **publicly reachable** page (no login gate).
- Cover **every jurisdiction** the product ships in (GDPR-compliant text for EU, CCPA/CPRA disclosures for California, etc.).
- Be **kept current** — changes require re-publish through [Review and publish](/publishing/modular-submission).

### Website URL (optional)

A link to your game's marketing website. Surfaces on the Store PDP under "More from this publisher."

### Copyright / trademark information

Legal notice text displayed on the PDP. Required for many licensed titles ("© 2026 Publisher Inc. All rights reserved. LICENSED™ mark owned by...").

## Validation

Basic info is validated at submission time:

- Support email / URL must resolve.
- Privacy policy URL must return HTTP 200.
- Category must be selected.
- Product name must match the reserved name (see [Game name](/publishing/game-name)).
