Partner Center's **Pricing and availability** module coordinates every commercial dimension of your product. There are more axes than most teams realize, and understanding them up front prevents launch-day mistakes.

## The six axes

| Axis | Controls | Documented at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | Base price per market, with sale overrides | [Pricing](/publishing/pricing) |
| **Market** | Which countries the product is offered in | [Pricing](/publishing/pricing) |
| **Visibility** | Whether the product appears in Store search / browse | [Visibility & audiences](/publishing/how-to-visibility) |
| **Purchasability** | Whether the buy button is enabled (independent from visibility) | [Visibility & audiences](/publishing/how-to-visibility) |
| **Schedule** | Time-based transitions for the axes above | [Availability schedule](/publishing/availability-schedule) |
| **Audience** | Which customers see the product (Insider ring, private account list) | [Visibility & audiences](/publishing/how-to-visibility) |

Every product has values on every axis, always. The default state is `Price=X, all markets, Visible, Purchasable, no schedule, all audiences` — the normal retail configuration.

## Common configurations

| Config | Price | Market | Visibility | Purchase | Schedule | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard retail | \$X | All | Visible | Yes | None | All |
| Coming soon / wishlist | — | All | Visible | No | Purchase enabled on release date | All |
| Pre-order | \$X | All | Visible | Pre-order only | Full purchase on release date | All |
| Regional soft launch | \$X | Australia only | Visible | Yes | Full market on later date | All |
| Reviewer build | \$0 | Global | Hidden | Yes (via code) | None | Named accounts |
| Delisted | — | All | Hidden | No | None | All (owners keep entitlement) |

## The order of operations

When submitting a launch, Partner Center evaluates axes in this order:

1. Is the product **published** at all? (packages certified and staged)
2. Is the current UTC time inside the **schedule** window?
3. Is the accessing customer in an included **market**?
4. Is the accessing customer in an included **audience**?
5. Is the product **visible** in Store search?
6. Is the product **purchasable**?
7. What **price** does this customer see?

Each gate has to be open. A common launch bug is having the schedule set correctly but the product still marked as `Not visible` — the schedule never flips visibility to `Visible` unless you configure that transition.
