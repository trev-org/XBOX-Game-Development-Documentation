By default, dev sandboxes are permissive: they may report your test account as fully licensed for everything, which masks license-loss and trial paths in code. To exercise the real licensing behavior, enable licensing enforcement.

## When licensing enforcement matters

- Verifying **`XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost`** fires (family-sharing changes, refunds).
- Testing **trial** flows where `isTrial` is true.
- Validating **PC License Tokens** for services (see [License tokens](/publishing/xstore-license-tokens)).
- Testing that your title gracefully handles a lost DLC license mid-session.

## Enable licensing on the product

1. In Partner Center, open the product and go to **Product Setup**.
2. Under **Licensing**, ensure the product is **Marked as Provisioned** for your dev sandbox.
3. If your product is PC or PC XBOX, choose between **Open** and **Restrictive** licensing (see [Open and restrictive licensing](/publishing/xstore-open-restrictive-licensing)).
4. Publish/republish the product to the sandbox.

## Prime the test account

A test account only sees a license after it has **acquired** the product (even if free) in that sandbox:

- Sign in on a devkit / PC that is in the sandbox.
- Open the **Store** app.
- Find the product and select **Get** (dev sandboxes typically have \$0 pricing).
- Confirm the acquisition. The Store grants a license to the account.

For **trial** testing, choose **Try** instead of **Get**. The account will have `isTrial = true` with the configured cap.

## Exercising license-lost scenarios

To simulate a lost license:

- **Refund path** — in the Store app, remove the license ("Manage" → remove). `XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost` fires while your title is running.
- **Family sharing path** — sign in a family-member account on another device with the primary owner elsewhere and revoke home-Xbox designation.
- **Sandbox switch** — switch the device to a sandbox where the account does not own the product; on relaunch, the license query returns unlicensed.

## Validating from your title

```cpp
XStoreGameLicense license = {};
HRESULT hr = XStoreQueryGameLicense(context, &license);

if (SUCCEEDED(hr)) {
    // license.isTrial
    // license.isTrialOwnedByThisUser
    // license.trialTimeRemainingInSeconds
    // license.expirationDate
}

XTaskQueueRegistrationToken token = {};
XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost(
    packageLicense,
    taskQueue,
    context,
    [](void* ctx) { /* handle license loss */ },
    &token);
```

<Warning>
  Do not cache license state across `XStoreRegisterPackageLicenseLost` callbacks. Always re-query on suspend/resume and on user change.
</Warning>
