**PIX Live** shows real-time metrics without taking a capture — useful for playtests and QA sessions.

## Panels

- **System Monitor** — CPU/GPU/memory/disk.
- **Memory Pies** — pie chart of VRAM broken out by heap, resource type, and title tag.
- **Custom charts** — build title-specific graphs from PIX counters.

## Uses

- Watch a playtest without interrupting for a capture.
- Compare memory footprint across game modes.
- Confirm a fix landed without collecting new captures.
