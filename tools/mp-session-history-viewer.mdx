The **Multiplayer Session History Viewer** (`MultiplayerSessionHistoryViewer.exe`) shows the full write history of an MPSD session so you can see exactly how members, properties, and states evolved.

## When to use

- Diagnose "who left when" issues in matchmaking.
- Replay a session that dropped a player mid-match.
- Confirm session properties round-tripped from your title correctly.

## Filters

- By session name / template.
- By member gamertag.
- By time window.
