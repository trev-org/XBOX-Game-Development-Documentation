**Timing Captures** are PIX's CPU-side capture format. They record thread activity, context switches, PIX events, and file I/O over a wall-clock window.

## What you get

- **Timeline** — per-thread activity with color-coded PIX events.
- **CPU sampling profiler** — sampled call stacks aggregated by hot function.
- **Critical path** — highlight the longest dependency chain reaching a PIX event.
- **Context switches** — kernel scheduling decisions per core.
- **Metrics view** — counters (frame time, memory, page faults) on a shared axis.
- **Summary view** — aggregated frame stats.

## Capture

- **From PIX** — Connect → **Take Timing Capture** with duration.
- **Programmatically** — `PIXBeginCapture(PIX_CAPTURE_TIMING, ...)`.
- **From ETL** — [convert existing ETL](/tools/pix-convert-etl).
