**`XceTool.exe`** validates and emits **XBOX Common Events (XCE)** telemetry payloads. Use it during development to verify events fire with the right schema before they hit the analytics pipeline.

## Verbs

- `validate` — check a captured event against the schema.
- `emit` — send a synthesized event to a test endpoint.
- `diff` — compare two runs to spot missing or extra events.
