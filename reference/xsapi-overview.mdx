XSAPI (XBOX Services API) is the C/C\+\+ client library for XBOX Live services. It exposes achievements, social, multiplayer, presence, stats, leaderboards, title storage, and more.

## Architecture

- **Core context** (`XblContextHandle`) — created per `XUserHandle`.
- **Transport** — `libHttpClient` for HTTPS and WebSocket.
- **Async** — the standard GDK [async model](/gdk/async-programming) with `XTaskQueue`.
