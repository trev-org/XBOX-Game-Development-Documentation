**Porting** means taking a game you've already shipped somewhere else — Steam, PlayStation, mobile, an older XBOX generation — and getting it running on modern XBOX or Windows. It's usually faster than building from scratch because your gameplay code already works.

<Tip>
  **Is this the right path for you?** Pick this if you already have a shipped game and want to bring it to XBOX. If you're starting a brand-new game, pick a device path (like [XBOX Series X|S](/paths/series-xs/overview) or [Windows & ROG Ally](/paths/windows/overview)) or your engine's path ([Unreal](/paths/unreal/overview), [Unity](/paths/unity/overview), [Godot & community engines](/paths/community-engines/overview)) instead.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Before you start:** you should have finished [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding) and have the [GDK installed](/gdk/get-started). If not, go to the [Home page](/) and follow the guided path from step 1.
</Note>
