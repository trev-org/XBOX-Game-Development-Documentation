Before you dive into Unreal \+ GDK development, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **A working Unreal project** locally (any recent UE 5.x version).
- **XBOX GDK plug-in for Unreal** available in your Epic account (granted via ID@XBOX). See [Unreal setup](/pc-e2e/engines/unreal).
- **Partner Center product** — with the appropriate device rows checked (Retail / Desktop / handheld).
