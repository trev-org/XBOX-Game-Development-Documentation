Before you dive into Unreal integration, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **Unreal Engine source access** — required to build for XBOX target platforms.
- **XBOX platform extensions** for your Unreal Engine version — download from your Epic Games publisher-verified account.
- **Partner Center product reserved** if you'll be validating XBOX services.
