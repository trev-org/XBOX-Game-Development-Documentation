Before you dive into Godot, GameMaker, or custom-engine development, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **An engine of choice** installed locally: Godot, GameMaker, or your custom engine source.
- **Partner Center product** with the appropriate device rows checked.
- **Community support channels** identified (Godot/XBOX community, GameMaker export docs) — these engines are largely community-supported for XBOX.
