Before you dive into engine integration, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **Engine source or GDK-capable build** — Godot with XBOX sample, GameMaker with the GDK Extension, or a C/C\+\+ engine you can modify.
- **A GDK project that builds** — either your engine's XBOX sample or a fresh GDK template. See [Build your first title](/gdk/developing-new-titles).
- **Partner Center product reserved** if you'll be validating XBOX services.
