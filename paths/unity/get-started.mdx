Before you dive into Unity \+ GDK development, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — see [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — see [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **A working Unity project** (Unity 2022 LTS or later).
- **XBOX GDK plug-in for Unity** granted via ID@XBOX. See [Unity setup](/pc-e2e/engines/unity).
- **Partner Center product** with the appropriate device rows checked.
