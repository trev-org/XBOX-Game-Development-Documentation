Before you dive into Unity integration, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **Unity Pro license** (required for closed platform builds).
- **Unity for XBOX platform module** — request access through your Unity account and download the XBOX build module.
- **Partner Center product reserved** if you'll be validating XBOX services.
