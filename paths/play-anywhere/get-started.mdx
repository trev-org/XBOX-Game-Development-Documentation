Before you dive into cross-device development, make sure the following are in place. If anything is missing, go back to the [Home](/) guided path.

## Checklist

- **ID@XBOX onboarding complete** — NDA signed, concept approved, Partner Center registered. See [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding).
- **Dev PC ready** — Visual Studio, Windows SDK, and the GDK installed. See [Setup & install](/gdk/get-started).
- **A GDK project that builds** — either a new title or a sample. See [Build your first title](/gdk/developing-new-titles).
- **Partner Center product reserved** with Retail \+ Sandboxes checked (needed for Play Anywhere entitlements).

<Tip>
  Play Anywhere requires a **single product** in Partner Center that covers both console and PC. Do not create two products.
</Tip>
