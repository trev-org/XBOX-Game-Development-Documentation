**XBOX Play Anywhere** means one purchase, two devices — a player who buys your game on XBOX also gets it on Windows PC (and vice versa), with saves and progress shared between the two. This path shows you how to ship a title that works this way.

<Tip>
  **Is this the right path for you?** Pick this if you want your game to be a single product available on both XBOX **and** Windows, with shared saves. If you only care about one platform, use the [XBOX Series X|S](/paths/series-xs/overview) or [Windows & ROG Ally](/paths/windows/overview) path instead.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Before you start:** you should have finished [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding), have the [GDK installed](/gdk/get-started), and have a [Visual Studio project building](/gdk/developing-new-titles). If any of that is missing, go back to the [Home page](/) and follow the guided path.
</Note>
