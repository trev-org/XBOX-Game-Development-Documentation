Welcome. This is the home for developing and publishing games on XBOX Series X|S, XBOX One, and Windows PC with the Microsoft Game Development Kit (GDK).

If you're new here, follow the path below in order — it's designed to take you from zero to a running project without missing anything critical.

## Start here — the guided path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Onboard with ID@XBOX" icon="id-card">
    Before you can download the GDK or publish anything, your studio must complete ID@XBOX onboarding — NDA, concept approval, and Partner Center. Plan for ~2 weeks minimum.

    <Card title="Begin onboarding" icon="arrow-right" href="/onboarding">
      Step-by-step ID@XBOX workflow with realistic timing.
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up and install" icon="download">
    Install Visual Studio, the Windows SDK, and the GDK. Configure your dev PC and confirm you can build a sample.

    <Card title="Setup & install" icon="arrow-right" href="/gdk/get-started">
      Environment setup, GDK install, dev PC config, and samples.
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first title" icon="play">
    Create a new GDK project from a Visual Studio template, or begin porting an existing title.

    <Card title="Build your first title" icon="arrow-right" href="/gdk/developing-new-titles">
      Pick a project template and get your first build running.
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Getting Started Guide" icon="compass">
    Pick the path that matches your target — a device (Play Anywhere, Series X|S, Windows & ROG Ally), an engine (Unreal, Unity, Godot & community), or porting a shipped title.

    <Card title="Getting Started Guides" icon="arrow-right" href="/paths/porting/overview">
      7 curated paths covering every common starting point.
    </Card>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Already onboarded and set up? Skip ahead to the [Getting Started Guides](/paths/porting/overview) and pick your path.
</Note>
