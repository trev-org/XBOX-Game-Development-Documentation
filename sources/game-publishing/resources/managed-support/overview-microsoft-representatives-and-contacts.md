# Who are your XBOX contacts?

[!INCLUDE [reminder](../../includes/managed-creators-only-feature.md)]

Throughout this documentation, you encounter references to your XBOX representative, XBOX account team, or XBOX contacts. Who the contacts are depends on your support model with XBOX. If you're unsure about whether you have a dedicated XBOX account team or should contact ID@XBOX, contact the team that supported your XBOX registration.

## XBOX account teams

A dedicated XBOX account team is called a *WAM* (Worldwide Account Managers). The WAM includes designated points of contact that are responsible for your day-to-day interactions with XBOX. Your WAM might include the following points of contact, but you might only have some of these contacts.

 - **Strategic Partner Manager (SPM):** Manages the business relationship between XBOX and a Publisher. An SPM covers topics such as royalties, financials, business planning, partnership opportunities, and business-to-business updates.
 - **Production Partner Manager (PPM):** Manages the production relationship between XBOX and a Publisher. A PPM covers topics such as SKU planning, submission and release management, Certification coordination, and post-release updates.
 - **Developer Partner Manager (DPM):** Manages the development relationship between XBOX and a Publisher or Developer. A DPM covers topics relating to game development on XBOX platforms.
 - **Business Development Manager (BDM):** Manages deal opportunities between XBOX and a Publisher.
 - **ID@XBOX Regional Manager/Lead (ID@XBOX creators with XBOX account teams):** Manages the business relationship between XBOX and a Publisher. ID leads cover topics such as digital publishing strategy, business planning, pitching & partnership opportunities, and business-to-business updates. 
 - **Global Product Marketing Manager:** Manages marketing opportunities between XBOX and a Publisher.

The WAM is contacted using a dedicated email address that includes each member of the account team. 

## ID@XBOX contacts

If you don't have a dedicated XBOX account team and you're part of the ID@XBOX program, use the following contacts.

 - **ID Games (idgames@xbox.com):** Game concept details and approvals. 
 - **ID Setup (idsetup@xbox.com):** Anything related to development kits, contract agreements (NDA, TLA, GDK), Partner Center access, middleware access, tool permissions (for example, XBOX Developer Forums), newsletter opt in/opt out, business information changes (address, game title, publishers), and Vendor ID setup. 
 - **ID Account Management (idam@xbox.com):** Royalties, deal opportunities, outreach, marketing, and everything not covered elsewhere. 
 - **ID Developer Support (idds@xbox.com):** Assistance with product onboarding, XBOX services gameplay settings, networking/multiplayer support, sandbox access/testing issues, and technical questions. Contact this alias if you're directed to reach out to a Developer Partner Manager. 
 - **ID Certification and Submissions (idcert@microsoft.com):** Cert submission planning and support, release management, revenue SKU and payee info requests, certification questions, publishing issues, post-release updates, and add-on content releases.
 - **ID Marketing (idmarketing@microsoft.com):** Marketing support.
 - **ID Discounting (iddiscounting@microsoft.com):** Discounting, themed sales, and sales support.
 - **ID Events (idevents@microsoft.com):** Information regarding events, showcase opportunities, and sponsorships (for example, Game Developer Conference (GDC), Gamescom, PAX, Twitch Showcase, Game Fest demo events).
 - **ID Community Management (idcomm@microsoft.com):** ID@XBOX blog, X, Discord, Instagram, YouTube, community engagement, social media support, and amplifications.

## Other contacts

You might also need to reach out to other XBOX contacts that aren't part of your XBOX account team or the ID@XBOX team.

 - **Microsoft Artwork Certification**
   - **Microsoft Artwork Certification Support: msartcertsup@microsoft.com**
      - Inquiries relating to physical packaging submitted via the Apollo tool, including Apollo user access and title setup.
      - Access to RAD for packaging templates and XBOX assets, including logos and trailer mnemonics.
      - Branding and Advertising guidance.
    - **XBOX Digital Artwork Certification: xbdigiart@microsoft.com**
      - Inquiries relating to digital artwork and trailers submitted within Partner Center's Store Listings UI.
 - **XBOX Insider Program Team: XBOXFlightHelp@microsoft.com**
    - Inquiries relating to Flight setup through the XBOX Insider Program.
 - **3rd Party Publishing Operations (3PP Ops): 3ppops@microsoft.com**
    - Manufacturing inquiries, including royalty tier submissions, disc orders and sub-publishing agreements.
 - **3rd Party Token Specialist: token3pp@microsoft.com**
    - Paid token orders and requests for tokens exceeding your Partner Center allowance.
 - **XBOX Certification: xboxcert@xbox.com**
    - Inquiries relating to Package Certification reported issues and Certification testing processes.
