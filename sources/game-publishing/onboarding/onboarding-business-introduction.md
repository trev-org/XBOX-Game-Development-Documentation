# Step 2: Provide your business introduction

After you [select the ID@XBOX program](onboarding-register.md), it's time to introduce your business. Your business introduction tells the ID@XBOX team more about your studio so they can begin your registration.

## Confirm your program

1. On the **Welcome** page, check that **ID@XBOX** is the selected program.
1. If it isn't, select **Change program**, choose **ID@XBOX**, and continue.
1. Select **Get started** to begin providing your details.

## Provide your contact details

Provide the contact information for your business, which includes:

- Your **name** and **business title**.
- A **shipping address** for XBOX development kits.
- Your **public contact information**. This information is optional at this stage, but you must provide it later if you intend to release your game in European markets.

## Provide your studio information

Next, tell us about your studio. You provide:

- Your **studio name**, which might be your *doing business as* (DBA) or trading name.
- Your **legally registered business name**.
- **Links to your studio presence**, such as trailers or a studio website.
- Whether you want to join ID@XBOX as a **developer** or as a **publisher**.

### Speed up verification with a DUNS Number

If you have a company **Dun & Bradstreet (DUNS) Number**, provide it for faster business verification.

> [!TIP]
> If you don't provide a DUNS Number now, you need to provide extra identification information later when you register for Partner Center. Adding it now helps your onboarding move faster.

## Submit your introduction

After you complete your details, select **Submit your introduction**.

After you submit, the ID@XBOX team sends you a **welcome email** and a separate email containing the mutual **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**. You'll sign the NDA in the next step.
