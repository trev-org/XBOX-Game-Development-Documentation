# Step 3: Sign your Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

After you [submit your business introduction](onboarding-business-introduction.md), the ID@XBOX team sends you a mutual **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**. Signing the NDA lets you continue your registration and share details about the game you're creating.

## What to expect

After you submit your introduction, you'll receive:

- A **welcome email** from the ID@XBOX team.
- A separate email containing the mutual **NDA** for you to sign.

> [!NOTE]
> The NDA email is typically received **within 20 minutes**, but it might take up to **three business days** to arrive.

## Sign the NDA

Open the NDA email and follow the instructions to review and sign the agreement.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must sign the NDA before you can continue your ID@XBOX registration and submit your game concept.

## What happens next

After you sign the NDA, you're ready to provide information about the game you're creating. Return to the [XBOX Onboarding Hub](https://developer.microsoft.com/games/publish/id/welcome) to submit your game concept.

### Optional: Start exploring the XBOX Game Development Kit

If you're not ready to submit your game concept, you can still get started with development using the XBOX Game Development Kit (GDK) with limited capabilities: 

 - You can only build and test in XBOX development [sandboxes](../concepts/sandboxes.md).
 - You can use XBOX identity and basic player features, including sign-in, presences, and some social features.
 - You can use XBOX cloud storage for game saves.
 - You can use XBOX multiplayer sessions and networking APIs.
 - You can't use XBOX achievements and gamerscore.

Follow these steps to get started.

 1. Return to the [XBOX Onboarding Hub](https://developer.microsoft.com/games/publish/id/welcome).
 2. In the **Start exploring the Game Development Kit (GDK)** section, select **Get Started**.
 3. Review the guidance about the GDK limitations.
 4. Review and accept the terms and conditions of the **App developer agreement**.
 5. Select **Enroll**.

The XBOX Onboarding Hub triggers Partner Center enrollment. After enrollment completes, you can:
 - View your Partner Center account details, including your **Seller ID**.
 - Visit [Partner Center](https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard) to start account setup.
 - [Download the GDK](https://github.com/microsoft/gdk) to start developing your game.

> [!TIP]
> You can complete your ID@XBOX application at any time to unlock full GDK capabilities. Your development progress is kept, and you don't lose anything you already set up in Partner Center.
