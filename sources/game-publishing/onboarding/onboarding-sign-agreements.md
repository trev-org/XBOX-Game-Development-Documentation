# Step 5: Sign your title licensing and development agreements

Congratulations, your [game concept](onboarding-submit-game-concept.md) is approved! Before you register for Partner Center and start development, review and sign the agreements that govern your game and your development hardware.

## What you receive

When your application is successful and your game concept is approved, you receive an email confirming the approval decision, along with new agreements to sign. These agreements include:

 - A **Title Licensing Agreement (TLA)** for your game.
 - An **XBOX Game Development Kit (GDK) License Agreement** for development hardware and software.

## Sign your agreements

Review and sign each agreement by following the instructions provided in the approval email. The agreements you sign here establish the terms of your concept approval under your licensing agreement with Microsoft and terms under which you can use the GDK.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your agreements need to be fully executed before you can open your Partner Center account in the next step.
