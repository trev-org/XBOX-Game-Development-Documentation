# Step 4: Submit your first game concept

After you [sign your non-disclosure agreement](onboarding-sign-nda.md), you're ready to tell us about the game you're creating. Every XBOX release starts with a game concept. The concept is your chance to introduce your game and help us understand your vision.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All XBOX games require an approved game concept. Repeat this process for any other game concepts you submit in the future. For more information, see [Game concepts](../publishing-processes/managed-creators/publishing-processes-game-concepts.md).

## How to submit your concept

 1. Go to the [XBOX Onboarding Hub](https://developer.microsoft.com/games/publish/id/welcome) and sign in with your Microsoft account (MSA).
 1. In the **My Game Concepts** section, select **New application**.
 1. Complete the **Game Concept** form in full.
 1. If available, provide a **link to a game build**, or keys to acquire a build.
 1. Submit the concept form.

> [!TIP]
> If you're building multiple games, you don't need to wait for your first game concept to be reviewed before submitting the next. You can submit up to 10 concepts for review simultaneously.

## Information you provide

Complete the game concept form with details that support your application, including:

 - The **developer** and **publisher** for your game.
 - Your game's **key features** and a description of your game.
 - Your **target release date**, and any actual release dates if your game is already available on other platforms.
 - The **platforms** your game releases on, or is already released on.
 - Your game's **market availability and pricing**.
 - The **XBOX network functionality** and **User Generated Content (UGC)** capabilities your game supports.
 - Any **supplementary material**, such as gameplay videos or trailers.

> [!TIP]
> Before you submit, make sure you provide accurate information that represents your intended final game. It's also a good idea to review your game to ensure your content feels at home on XBOX and isn't overly vulgar, offensive, or objectionable.

## Concept review and timeline

The XBOX team reviews your submission and provides an application decision.

We review new concepts every business day (Monday–Friday), but it can take up to **3 weeks** (typically **10–15 business days**) to complete the review and provide a decision.

## What happens after a decision

After we review your concept, you receive an email letting you know the outcome:

 - **If your concept is approved**, you receive a confirmation email and the agreements you need to sign to continue. We provide more details about these agreements in the next step.
 - **If we need more information**, we let you know what we need before you can move forward.
