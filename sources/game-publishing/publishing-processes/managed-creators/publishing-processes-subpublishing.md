# Sub-publishing

[!INCLUDE [reminder](../../includes/managed-creators-only-feature.md)]

Sub-publishing permits another publisher that holds a valid publishing agreement with Microsoft to publish your digital product in specific markets (digital sub-publishing) or as a physical release (physical sub-publishing).

## Forms for sub-publishing agreements
When you're working with another publisher to sub-publish a game digitally or physically, you must complete and submit an XBOX Sub-Publishing Form. The primary publisher that submitted the [game concept](publishing-processes-game-concepts.md) is responsible for initiating this process. Forms for both digital and physical, or physical-only sub-publishing are available in the [XBOX Publisher Guide](https://aka.ms/xboxpublisherguide) in the **Forms** section. If you don't have access, speak to your XBOX contacts.

## Requirements for sub-publishing
Each **XBOX Sub-Publishing Form** contains full guidance on the requirements for sub-publishing. Review this guidance and speak to your XBOX contacts if you have questions.

> [!NOTE]
> The sub-publisher of your product must have a valid license agreement with Microsoft to publish the product, and the license agreement must be valid for the distribution method being considered. Sub-publishing might require the sub-publisher to submit a [game concept](publishing-processes-game-concepts.md). Ask your XBOX contacts if you're unsure about whether a new concept is necessary.
