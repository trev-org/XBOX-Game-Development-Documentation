Bringing gamers with disabilities into your development process early — playtests, interviews, design jams — is essential to building accessible games. For developers new to this work, fear of accidentally causing offense can lead to under-inviting the community, which loses valuable feedback.

Xbox has produced a short 16-minute training video covering the basics of interacting with people across major categories of disability. After watching, you'll be more comfortable inviting players from the Gaming and Disability Community into your development process.

## Watch the video

Available at [**aka.ms/Gaming-and-Disability-Etiquette**](https://aka.ms/Gaming-and-Disability-Etiquette).

<Tip>
  Share this with the whole team — not just accessibility champions — before running your first accessibility playtest or workshop.
