The **Gaming and Disability Player Experience Guide (GADPEG)** is a supplemental resource to the [XBOX Accessibility Guidelines](/principles/xbox-accessibility-guidelines).

Where the XAGs are organized by game _area_ (text display, contrast, subtitles, input, and so on), GADPEG is organized by **type of disability**. It walks through the barriers players commonly encounter, describes typical assistive technologies, and points to the XAG best practices that address each barrier.

## Who should read it

- **Design leads and UX** starting a new project — brief the team on player experience before scoping features.
- **Producers** — use it to plan when in production to schedule XAG work.
- **Engineers and QA** — use it as background for the concrete XAG implementation guidelines.

## What's inside

- Motor disabilities
- Vision disabilities
- Hearing disabilities
- Cognitive disabilities
- Speech disabilities

Each section covers common barriers, assistive technology, and cross-links to the specific XAGs that address them.
