Partner Center provides energy consumption data for your title on the Xbox console, so you can monitor and optimize your title's environmental impact. See how much power, energy, and carbon your titles are generating, and compare month over month to measure the impact of updates.

See [Downloads Hub datasets — Partner Center](/partner-center/downloads-hub-datasets) for the full dataset reference.

## Download sustainability data for your titles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Partner Center">
    Go to [partner.microsoft.com](https://partner.microsoft.com) and sign in.

    If you don't have a Partner Center account, contact the account owner in your organization and ask to be added to the **Analytics AAD group** with **Marketer** permissions on the insights data. You'll also need **product-level permissions** for your catalog. Your Microsoft point of contact can help identify the right person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Insights">
    From the Dashboard Home, go to **Insights**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new report">
    Open **Downloads Hub › Create New Report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your app or product group">
    Sustainability data is available for either an **App** or a **Product Group** of a released title. Select the option that fits, then pick your game or app from the drop-down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Sustainability data source">
    Scroll to the bottom of **Data Source**, select **Sustainability**, and pick all columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range and download">
    In **Date Range**, choose **Lifetime**. Enter a report file name and click **Download Now**. Dismiss the confirmation dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report from My Reports">
    Return to the top of the page and open **My Reports**. Download the generated CSV.

    Each row represents one month, retail name, and country combination. Key sustainability data is in the right-hand columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Convert energy or CO₂ data to real-world equivalencies

Sum the emissions in the **TitleCO2e\_MetricTon** column, then use the EPA's [Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator](https://www.epa.gov/energy/greenhouse-gas-equivalencies-calculator#results). Select **Emissions Data** and paste in your total.

For reference, 1,000 metric tonnes of CO₂-equivalent is roughly:

- 2.5M miles driven by an average petrol/gas car
- 1.1M pounds of coal burned
- Carbon sequestered by ~1,150 acres of forest

(Actual equivalencies vary — use the calculator for authoritative numbers.)

## Use the data to prioritize

Once you have monthly baselines for a released title:

- Identify the highest-consumption countries or SKUs.
- Compare pre- and post-update months to measure the impact of any energy-efficiency work.
- Cross-reference against the [Energy efficiency essentials](/principles/energy-efficiency-essentials) to plan the next round of optimizations.
