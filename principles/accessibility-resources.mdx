A curated list of Microsoft and third-party resources for XBOX developers. Nothing here is paid outside of Microsoft or Xbox. Questions or updates? Email the [Gaming Accessibility Team](mailto:xaccess@microsoft.com).

## Accessibility guidance

### Microsoft

- [XBOX Accessibility Guidelines (XAGs)](/principles/xbox-accessibility-guidelines) — best practices developed with the Gaming and Disability Community.
- [Gaming and Disability Player Experience Guide (GADPEG)](/principles/gadpeg) — supplemental to the XAGs, organized by disability type.
- [Gaming Accessibility Fundamentals learning path](/principles/gaf-info) — 5-module Microsoft Learn course.
- [Game Accessibility Workshop Toolkit](/principles/accessibility-workshop-toolkit) — facilitator-ready workshop assets.
- [Accessibility of gaming AI experiences](/principles/accessibility-of-gaming-ai) — accessibility considerations for AI-driven game features.

### Outside Microsoft

- [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/) — three-tier (basic / intermediate / advanced) developer-friendly guidelines.
- [Accessible Player Experience (APX)](https://accessible.games/apx) — AbleGamers Charity design patterns from hundreds of players with disabilities.

## Xbox Adaptive Controller (XAC)

The XAC is designed for players with physical disabilities. It works with external buttons and joysticks so players can build a controller that suits them.

- [Xbox Adaptive Controller hub](https://www.xbox.com/accessories/controllers/xbox-adaptive-controller)
- [How it works — video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEKIAHrugZ4)
- [Get to know the XAC](https://support.xbox.com/help/account-profile/accessibility/get-to-know-adaptive-controller) — button and port breakdown.
- [XAC history](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7y7cAQb21DQ)
- [Configuring the XAC in the Xbox Accessories app](https://support.xbox.com/help/account-profile/accessibility/customize-adaptive-controller-in-xbox-accessories-app)
- [Xbox accessories and controllers](https://www.xbox.com/accessories) — filter by assistive technology.
  - [Logitech Adaptive Gaming Kit](https://www.microsoft.com/d/logitech-adaptive-gaming-kit-for-xbox-adaptive-controller/936ndvcjzp78) — buttons made specifically for the XAC.

## Connecting with players

> "Nothing about us without us." Getting input from the Gaming and Disability Community should be an integral part of development.

- **XBOX Accessibility Insiders League (XAIL)** — a group of 100,000\+ Xbox Insiders interested in accessibility. Use it to ship pre-release builds and gather structured feedback from players who use assistive technology. Request access through your Microsoft point of contact.
- [AbleGamers Player Panels](https://accessible.games) — connects studios to hundreds of players with disabilities for design input and playtesting.
