For late-stage or shipped titles, XBOX Certification can produce a **Sustainability Power Report** measuring your title's power draw across common in-game areas — menus, loading screens, lobbies, cutscenes, active gameplay, constrained state, and multiplayer. The report is the fastest way to identify which parts of your title deviate from the [Lab platform baselines](/principles/lab-platform-baselines).

## How to request a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact your Microsoft representative">
    Reach out to your Microsoft point of contact, or email [XboxSustainability@Microsoft.com](mailto:XboxSustainability@Microsoft.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide the title, sandbox, and scenarios">
    Share the target sandbox and the specific scenarios you want measured. Common asks: main menu, matchmaking lobby, single-player campaign, multiplayer gameplay, pause menu, constrained state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certification runs the standardized test">
    Certification runs your title against standardized test rigs and instrumentation. See [Sustainability PIX guide](/principles/sustainability-pix-guide) for the exact PIX configuration used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the per-scenario breakdown">
    You receive a per-scenario breakdown of power draw with platform-average comparisons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prioritize with Power Monitor">
    Use the report to prioritize where local Power Monitor profiling will find the biggest wins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reports are available **pre-release** (for unreleased titles approaching cert) and **post-release** (for shipped titles considering updates).

## What a report includes

Each scenario is captured on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S with:

- **Title average** — your title's average Power Load % in that scenario.
- **Industry average** — the [Lab platform baseline](/principles/lab-platform-baselines) average for the same scenario.
- **Wattage** — approximate wall-socket wattage.
- **Deltas** — where you're above or below the industry average.

For an example of a real report used to guide production decisions, see the [Gears of War: Reloaded case study](/principles/case-studies-gears-of-war-reloaded).

## Lab vs. global platform baselines

Two baselines inform your reports:

- [**Lab baseline**](/principles/lab-platform-baselines) — controlled measurement on standardized hardware in a normalized environment. Best for comparing your title to others.
- [**Global platform baseline**](/principles/global-platform-baselines) — aggregated real-world telemetry from shipping titles. Best for understanding how your title actually behaves at scale.

The [Partner Center sustainability dashboard](/principles/partner-center-sustainability-data) surfaces global telemetry once your title is live; cert reports use lab measurement.

## Real examples using cert reports

Cert reports have driven the optimizations in multiple shipping titles:

- [**The Elder Scrolls Online**](/principles/case-studies-elder-scrolls-online) — cert report flagged a ~30 W jump in menus, leading to the 30 FPS menu cap.
- [**Star Wars Outlaws**](/principles/case-studies-star-wars-outlaws) — cert report showed the pause menu at ~75% Power Load vs. an industry average of 55–60%, leading to the 1 FPS pause behavior.
- [**Gears of War: Reloaded**](/principles/case-studies-gears-of-war-reloaded) — cert measured every mode × area combination to publish comparative data.

See the full library of results in [Case studies](/principles/case-studies-overview).
