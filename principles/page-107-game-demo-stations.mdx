Game demo stations are usually the most-attended element of a gaming event. Making them accessible ensures every guest can experience the games your teams have worked so hard on.

## Scoping questions

This guideline applies if you answer **yes** to:

- Does your event have demo stations or kiosks where attendees can play games?

## Implementation guidelines

### Monitors

- **Height:** adjustable, ideally 48 to 72 inches from the ground.
- **Angle:** tiltable — ideally 30 degrees from center, up and down.
- **Brightness:** easily adjusted.

### Tables

- **Height:** on-the-fly adjustable (sit/stand desk style). Approximately 28–48 inches range so wheelchairs can roll under.
- **Sturdy:** able to support someone of substantial weight leaning on it.
- **Color and contrast:** stand out in darker rooms so guests don't trip or collide.

### Cleanliness

**Wipes**

- Sanitizing wipes at every station for before-and-after use.
- Provide a receptacle for used wipes.
- Staff should monitor stations and wipe as needed.

**Hand sanitizer**

- Every station has hand sanitizer available, ideally in an automatic dispenser.

### Controllers

**Standard controllers**

- Minimum of two controllers per station for co-pilot play.
- Long cables — at least 3 feet from the edge of the table.

**Adaptive controllers**

- Every station should have an [XBOX Adaptive Controller](/principles/accessibility-feature-tags) present, or immediate access to cables to connect one.
- Have a variety of buttons, switches, and joysticks readily available for staff when an adaptive setup is required.
- Buttons should have Velcro backing, with lapboards for mounting.

### Accessibility info

**Title-specific**

- Each station displays a QR code with a short URL (and braille underneath) linking to the demo's accessibility information. The linked page doesn't need final content — just what settings this demo includes. Short URLs let you redirect for different pages and reuse the signs, since printing braille is expensive.

### Seating

- Provide seating, or easy access to folding chairs, for guests who have difficulty standing for extended periods.
