Sustainability on XBOX means lowering the energy cost of running your title without sacrificing gameplay quality. Microsoft sets sustainability goals across hardware and services, and titles play a meaningful role.

## Why it matters

- A small per-title power reduction multiplied across millions of players is significant.
- Energy-efficient titles run cooler, quieter, and last longer on handheld hardware.
- Cert includes power-related XRs and Microsoft publishes [case studies](/principles/sustainability-case-studies) from shipping titles.

## Where to start

- Review the [Energy efficiency essentials](/principles/energy-efficiency-essentials) checklist.
- Read [case studies](/principles/sustainability-case-studies) from titles like Call of Duty and Halo.
- Profile with PIX to identify idle GPU / CPU waste.
