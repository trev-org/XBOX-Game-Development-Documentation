Complementing the [Lab platform baselines](/principles/lab-platform-baselines) captured with PIX, the **global** platform baselines come from anonymized real-world telemetry.

In 2022 XBOX released a limited run of enhanced Xbox Series X|S consoles with power-supply energy monitoring. This telemetry provides insights across a wide range of user setups — including SSD, USB, networking, and power-regulation losses — to train data models that report the AC power consumption of titles as measured at the wall socket.

XBOX is also partnering with **Carbon Trust** and other global tech companies to develop a common methodology for tracking usage emissions from connected devices.

## What the telemetry shows

Enhanced Xbox Series X|S consoles send anonymized power telemetry back to XBOX services. This lets us determine the AC power consumption of consoles across a range of scenarios, including game and app usage — and to break averages down by studio, developer, publisher, genre, or feature set.

**Xbox Series X**

- Global average game power: **122 W**
- Global average app power: **51 W**

**Xbox Series S**

- Global average game power: **61 W**
- Global average app power: **31 W**

<Note>
  These averages remain fairly consistent over time. They are updated periodically.
</Note>

## Lab vs. global baselines

- **Lab baselines** — controlled measurement via PIX on standardized hardware. Best for comparing your title to others.
- **Global baselines** — aggregated real-world telemetry from shipping titles. Best for understanding how your title actually behaves at scale.

The [Partner Center sustainability dashboard](/principles/partner-center-sustainability-data) surfaces global telemetry once your title is live.
