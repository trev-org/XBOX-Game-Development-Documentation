## Goal

Ensure players with disabilities can reach customer support — call centers, live chat, email — through multiple accessible methods.

## Overview

Accessible customer support means the ways players discover information and contact support teams are usable by customers with disabilities. Offer multiple contact options: voice calls for players unable to read, and text chat or email for d/Deaf players. Ensure support websites meet web accessibility standards so all players can troubleshoot problems, ask questions, and share feedback.

## Scoping questions

- What support resources do your title, studio, or publisher offer?
- Does your customer support website meet [WCAG 2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2AA-Conformance)?
- Are call center numbers, email addresses, and live-chat entry points easily discoverable and accessible (screen-reader compatible and visually accessible)?

## Implementation guidelines

<Card title="Free access to support" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
  Players and individuals with disabilities should be able to reach call centers and customer support about the product and its accessibility features at no additional cost.
</Card>

<Card title="Multiple accessible contact methods" icon="list">
  Provide multiple accessible ways to contact support, including:

  - Phone
  - TTY
  - Email
  - Live chat
</Card>

<Tip>
  The [Microsoft Disability Answer Desk](https://www.microsoft.com/Accessibility/disability-answer-desk) is a strong reference example — customers can use text chat, a toll-free phone line, or a video call with an ASL-fluent specialist, and can also access community forums and assistive-technology resources.
</Tip>

## Potential player impact

This XAG reduces barriers for:

- Players without vision or with low vision
- Players with little or no color perception
- Players without hearing or with limited hearing
- Players without speech
- Players with limited cognitive skills
- Players with limited reach, strength, or manual dexterity
- Players using prosthetic devices
- Players with limited ability to use time-dependent controls
- All players, depending on personal, environmental, or situational factors
