`takx templates` prints the layout templates that [`takx create`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-create-command) can use. Templates are the starting point for a new bundle or a new layout.

## Synopsis

```powershell
takx templates
```

## Available templates

| Template | Best for | Sample page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `first-person` | First-person shooters and adventure games | [First person layout](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-tak-sample-first-person) |
| `platformer` | 2D platformers | [2D platformer layout](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-tak-sample-platformer) |
| `fighter` | Fighting games and arcade brawlers | [Fighter layout](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-tak-sample-fighter) |
| `driving` | Driving and racing games | [Driving layout](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-tak-sample-driving) |
| `standard-controller` | Reference layout mirroring the physical controller | [Standard controller layout](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-tak-sample-full-layout) |

<Note>
  The templates are illustrative starting points, not shipping layouts. Adjust ergonomics, control set, and art for your title before you publish.
</Note>
