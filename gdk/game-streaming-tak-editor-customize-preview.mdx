The preview panel supports overrides so you can validate a layout under the exact conditions a real client will experience.

## What you can customize

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Target resolution | Renders the preview at a specific client resolution (720p, 1080p, portrait) |
| Aspect ratio | Constrains the canvas to a specific aspect (16:9, 16:10, portrait) |
| Safe area | Draws the on-screen safe area for target clients so you avoid the edges |
| Language | Swaps the localization strings to the selected language |
| High contrast | Toggles the high-contrast asset variant |
| Show HUD outlines | Overlays HUD-safe zones you have defined for your title |

## Recommended sweeps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Design at 1080p">
    Start with 1080p landscape for the design phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify at 720p">
    Switch to 720p and confirm text remains legible and controls do not become disproportionate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify portrait">
    Switch to a portrait aspect and confirm your layout still works or you have a purpose-built portrait layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify longest-string language">
    Switch the preview to the language with the longest translations (typically German or Finnish) and confirm labels do not overflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify high contrast">
    Toggle high contrast and confirm the paired assets look right — see [High contrast mode](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-high-contrast-mode).
  </Step>
</Steps>
