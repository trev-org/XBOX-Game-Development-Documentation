Cross-generation development lets you extend the business value of your XBOX One titles onto XBOX Series X|S so you can retain existing active users and attract new ones — with minimal friction unifying users across generations. You can build new gameplay that takes advantage of XBOX Series X|S hardware without alienating current XBOX One users.

Players can enjoy new and beloved content on existing XBOX One family consoles and seamlessly upgrade to XBOX Series consoles with their library and progress intact.

## What is a cross-generation game?

A cross-generation XBOX game is any game that is playable across XBOX One family consoles and XBOX Series consoles. This includes newly released titles that ship simultaneously across both generations, as well as existing XBOX One titles that are refreshed or expanded for XBOX Series X|S.

There are three distinct approaches to packaging, selling, and releasing games to multiple console generations.

### Smart Delivery titles

Use Smart Delivery to provide the most frictionless cross-generation experience. Build native versions of your title that are largely similar or differentiated by hardware capabilities. Smart Delivery ensures that users receive the best available version whether they play on XBOX One family consoles or XBOX Series consoles.

For packaging options, see [Smart Delivery](/gdk/cross-gen/smart-delivery).

### Dual SKU titles

Create your game as two titles that users purchase, install, and play separately (one for XBOX One and one for XBOX Series X|S). Each title is presented independently in the Microsoft Store and carries separate user experiences.

For packaging options, see [Dual SKU cross-generation games](/gdk/cross-gen/dual-sku).

### Backward compatibility-only titles

If you choose not to create a native XBOX Series X|S version of your XBOX One title, the game can still be played on XBOX Series X|S hardware in backward compatibility mode at parity with XBOX One hardware.

For guidance, see [Backcompat only titles](/gdk/cross-gen/backcompat-only).
