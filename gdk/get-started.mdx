This is **step 2 of the guided path**. Before you start, you'll need to have completed [ID@XBOX onboarding](/onboarding) — Microsoft's approval process is what gives you access to download the GDK.

By the end of this page you'll know what the GDK actually is, have all the tools installed on your PC, and have built one of Microsoft's sample games to prove your setup works.

## What is the GDK?

The **Game Development Kit (GDK)** is the free bundle of tools, code libraries, and documentation that Microsoft ships for making XBOX and Windows games. You install it on your Windows PC the same way you'd install any other program.

Think of it as three things stacked together:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Code libraries" icon="cube">
    Ready-made C\+\+ APIs for saves, sign-in, achievements, controllers, and audio. You call these from your game.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Visual Studio integration" icon="code">
    Templates and build targets so making a new "XBOX game" project is as familiar as making any other Visual Studio project.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Command-line tools" icon="terminal">
    Small utilities for packaging your game, deploying to a dev kit, and validating your build before submission.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The GDK is the modern way to ship games on XBOX and the Microsoft Store — it replaces the older XDK and UWP toolchains you may have heard of.
