Before you publish a touch layout, you sideload it to a Content Test Application so real touch sessions pick it up. This works with the [Web CTA](/gdk/game-streaming-web-content-test-application) using the [`takx serve`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-serve-command) command running on a Windows PC.

## Prerequisites

- A packed `.tab` file, produced with [`takx pack`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-pack-command).
- [TAK CLI](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line) installed on a Windows PC on the same network as the CTA client.
- A streaming-configured [dev kit](/gdk/game-streaming-setup-xbox-developer-kit) hosting your title.
- The [Web CTA](/gdk/game-streaming-web-content-test-application) provisioned for your account.

## Deploy in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Serve the bundle">
    On your Windows PC, run:

    ```powershell
    takx serve my-title.tab
    ```

    Note the URL and port that `serve` prints — the CTA will connect to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the CTA">
    Launch the [Web CTA](/gdk/game-streaming-web-content-test-application) and connect it to your dev kit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point the CTA at your served bundle">
    In the CTA's touch adaptation settings, enter the URL that `takx serve` printed. The CTA fetches the bundle over the network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Start the streaming session and interact with the layout. Iterate by re-packing and refreshing the CTA — `takx serve` keeps serving from the same URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Iterating quickly

<Tip>
  Keep `takx serve` running while you iterate. Re-pack the bundle with `takx pack` and the next CTA session pulls the new version. No need to restart the server.
</Tip>

## When you are ready to publish

Once the layout works in the CTA across your target device set, follow [Publishing layouts](/gdk/game-streaming-touch-publishing-layouts) to distribute it through the streaming service.
