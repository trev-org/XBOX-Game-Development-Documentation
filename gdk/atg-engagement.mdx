The **Advanced Technology Group (ATG)** at Microsoft works directly with studios on performance, graphics, and platform-feature deep dives.

## What ATG provides

- 1:1 reviews of your title's GPU / CPU traces.
- Early access to platform-feature samples and best-practice guidance.
- Help unlocking Game DVR, capture, and broadcasting integration.

## Engage ATG

Reach out through your Microsoft account manager or via the developer support channel in [Partner Center](/publishing/properties). ATG engagements are typically prioritized for titles approaching ship.
