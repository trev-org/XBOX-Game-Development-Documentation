Game Saves enable users to move seamlessly across devices by keeping their progress available whenever they sign in. The system saves data locally, then synchronizes it to the cloud when the device connects to the internet. After the sync is complete, users can access their saved data on any supported device.

## Xbox Play Anywhere and Game Saves

Xbox Play Anywhere uses Game Saves to enable users to share game progression seamlessly across Windows devices, Xbox consoles, PCs, and handheld devices. Xbox Play Anywhere relies on consistent save-data synchronization so users can pick up where they left off on any supported device. For more information, see [Xbox Play Anywhere](https://www.xbox.com/games/xbox-play-anywhere).

## Game Saves development lifecycle

The Game Saves development lifecycle gives you a structured path to build, validate, and maintain reliable cloud-connected save systems. It walks you through how to introduce Game Saves to a new title and continues through debugging, testing, certification, and post-publishing support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get started with Game Saves">
    Initial onboarding with Game Saves concepts, APIs, storage models, and platform requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New title setup">
    Integrate Game Saves early in development:

    - Add user-sign-in–dependent initialization.
    - Configure identifiers like the SCID and MSAAppID.
    - Establish your storage design and container structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Debugging issues during development">
    Troubleshoot early implementation problems:

    - SCID configuration issues
    - Handle-lifetime issues
    - Quota/memory problems
    - NTSTATUS error interpretation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test planning">
    Define structured test plans covering read/write correctness, offline behavior, sync logic, system dialog flows, and multi-device scenarios.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Testing scenarios">
    Perform functional and stress testing across connectivity changes, conflict states, user switching, corruption handling, and storage-limit edge cases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your title for publishing">
    Validate your Partner Center setup, confirm quotas, ensure system dialogs function correctly, and verify safe-write patterns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certification requirements">
    Meet Xbox certification requirements for proper sync behavior, connectivity management, and PII compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage issues after publishing">
    Monitor and resolve live-site issues such as sync failures, user data-loss reports, and storage-limit problems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Migration scenarios">
    Update storage layouts, move between Game Saves APIs, and transition between title versions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
