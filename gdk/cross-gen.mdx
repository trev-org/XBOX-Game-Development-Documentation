Cross-generation ("cross-gen") development means shipping a single Microsoft Store product that runs natively on XBOX One, XBOX One S, XBOX One X, XBOX Series S, and XBOX Series X. Players buy your game once and the platform delivers the right binaries and assets for their console — a benefit known as **Smart Delivery**.

<Note>
  Cross-gen also intersects with [XBOX Play Anywhere](/publishing/overview), where the same purchase entitles the player to the Windows PC version of the title. This page covers the console-to-console cross-gen story.
</Note>

## Two cross-gen models

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Single binary" icon="layer-group">
    One executable that runs on every supported console SKU. You branch at runtime on hardware capability and adapt asset quality, framerate, and effects.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Multiple platforms (recommended for new titles)" icon="split">
    Separate native binaries for the XBOX One family and the XBOX Series X|S family, both packaged into the same Microsoft Store product. The store delivers the right binary; players never see the difference.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The **multi-platform** approach is recommended for any title that wants to take real advantage of Series X|S hardware (DirectStorage, mesh shaders, sampler feedback, hardware-accelerated ray tracing). It keeps your old-gen build small and lets you ship Series-only features without runtime branching.

### Backcompat-only titles

If you do not ship a native Series X|S binary, your XBOX One title still runs on Series X|S through **backwards compatibility** — the system emulates the XBOX One environment. You do not need to do anything to enable this, but you also do not get any Series X|S hardware benefits beyond the platform-level FPS Boost and Auto HDR features that the system applies for free.

## Project configuration

In Visual Studio, set the project platform to **Gaming.XBOX.XboxOne.x64** for the XBOX One family and **Gaming.XBOX.Scarlett.x64** for the XBOX Series X|S family. Most teams maintain both configurations side-by-side in the same solution and use shared source files with `#if defined(_GAMING_XBOX_SCARLETT)` guards for the truly platform-specific paths.

```cpp
#if defined(_GAMING_XBOX_SCARLETT)
    // Series X|S — enable hardware ray tracing path
    EnableRayTracing();
#else
    // XBOX One — fall back to screen-space reflections
    EnableScreenSpaceReflections();
#endif
```

## Per-target asset packs

Use package chunking and `MicrosoftGame.config` `<Resources>` entries to ship higher-resolution textures, higher-quality audio, or extra geometry as a separate chunk that only Series X|S consoles install. Combined with Intelligent Delivery, the platform pulls the right chunks for the player's SKU without your title doing any work.

## Save compatibility

Game saves must roam between SKUs. A player who buys on XBOX One and upgrades to XBOX Series X|S expects their saves to follow them, and your Connected Storage schema must support that. Keep your `XGameSave` blob format stable across SKUs, or version it and migrate on load.

## Performance targets

| Console | Target frame rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XBOX One | 30 FPS baseline | 1080p typical; HDR optional |
| XBOX One X | 30 / 60 FPS | Up to 4K render targets |
| XBOX Series S | 60 FPS baseline | 1080p – 1440p, HDR, VRR |
| XBOX Series X | 60 / 120 FPS | Up to 4K, HDR, VRR, ray tracing |

Expose the performance / quality choice as a player-facing setting where possible.

## Smart Delivery deep dive

Smart Delivery is the store-side mechanism that ensures a player gets the right binary and asset set on every console they sign in on:

- Tied to the **Store identity** (a single `StoreId`) that fronts one or more packages
- The store evaluates the target console at install time and delivers the best matching package
- Save data, achievements, friends progress, and licenses follow the player to the new SKU automatically
- Smart Delivery works across console generations only — PC entitlements are managed separately through XBOX Play Anywhere

Enable Smart Delivery in Partner Center on the product's properties page. Two packaging models exist on top:

- **Single package, branching at runtime** — simplest, but loses cert-friendly per-SKU optimization
- **Dual-package (recommended)** — one package per console family, both linked to the same Store identity

## Dual-SKU pattern

The dual-SKU pattern keeps Gen8 and Gen9 builds independent in the toolchain while shipping under a single Store identity:

- Maintain two `MicrosoftGame.config` files with the same `Identity` `Name` and matching `StoreId`
- Tag each package with its target device family (`XboxOne`, `Scarlett`) in the layout
- Use shared assets where bandwidth matters and per-SKU assets for high-fidelity content (4K textures, hi-res audio)
- Ship the **same SCID** for both packages so XBOX services, Connected Storage, and achievements roam transparently

Test the dual-SKU flow by switching between an XBOX One dev kit and a Series X dev kit signed in to the same test account; verify that saves, achievements, and entitlements roam without intervention.

## Backcompat-only titles

If your title is XBOX One only and you never ship a native Gen9 build:

- The console runs your XBOX One binary in backcompat mode on Series X|S
- Platform features like **FPS Boost** and **Auto HDR** can be enabled by the platform team on a per-title basis — request review through your account manager
- Performance and feature differences over the original Gen8 run are platform-applied; your title does not need any code changes
- You still benefit from the faster I/O and CPU of Gen9 hardware automatically; you do not benefit from DirectStorage, mesh shaders, ray tracing, or sampler feedback

Backcompat-only is the lowest-effort path. Most active titles graduate to a Gen9 native build to take advantage of the hardware.
