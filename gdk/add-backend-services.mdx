Pair your GDK title with [**PlayFab**](/playfab/overview) early. It's Microsoft's backend-as-a-service for games and gives you identity, multiplayer, leaderboards, economy, and live-ops without standing up your own servers.

## Why add PlayFab now

- **Cross-platform identity** — link XBOX, Steam, PSN, mobile, and custom logins under a single PlayFab player account.
- **Live game services** — leaderboards, statistics, player segments, scheduled tasks, A/B experiments.
- **Multiplayer infrastructure** — PlayFab [Lobby, Matchmaking, and Party](/playfab/multiplayer-lobby) for cross-platform play.
- **Economy** — Catalog v2, Inventory v2, and Microsoft Store reconciliation through [Economy v2](/playfab/economy).
- **Analytics** — PlayStream events, dashboards, and Insights through [Analytics](/playfab/analytics).

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a PlayFab title">
    Sign in at [developer.playfab.com](https://developer.playfab.com) and create a title. Note the **Title ID** — you'll pass it to the SDK.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the PlayFab GDK SDK">
    Add the [PlayFab GDK SDK](https://github.com/PlayFab/XPlatCppSdk) to your project. It links cleanly alongside XSAPI and shares libHttpClient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in players with XBOX identity">
    Call `LoginWithXbox` from the PlayFab Authentication API using the XSTS token your title already acquires via [`XUser`](/reference/xuser). PlayFab creates or links a master account on first call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the services you need">
    Add Economy, Lobby/Matchmaking, Leaderboards, or Analytics as your design requires. Each one is opt-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When PlayFab is optional

If your title only needs XBOX-side features — achievements, presence, social, MPSD multiplayer, Game Chat 2 — you can ship without PlayFab using [XBOX services](/services/overview) directly. Most live, cross-platform titles benefit from PlayFab even so.
