Quick Resume lets XBOX Series X|S players switch between multiple suspended games and pick up exactly where they left off.

## What your title must handle

- **Long suspend windows** — your process may be frozen for hours or days.
- **State invalidation** — network sessions, sign-in tokens, and matchmaking tickets can expire while suspended.
- **System clock changes** — the wall clock may jump significantly between resume cycles.
- **Constrained / unconstrained transitions** — use `PLM` events (`Suspending`, `Resuming`) to save state and reacquire resources.

## Best practices

- Reconnect to PlayFab Party / multiplayer sessions on resume and gracefully fall back to a safe menu.
- Refresh XSAPI tokens via [XUser](/reference/xuser).
- Recompute time-of-day and live-event state instead of trusting cached values.
- Save a recovery point during suspend so you can reload safely if the OS evicts you from Quick Resume.
