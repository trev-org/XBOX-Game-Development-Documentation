`takx notice` prints the third-party notice text you must include with a shipped bundle. Include the output in your title's third-party notices and/or in the bundle itself when required.

## Synopsis

```powershell
takx notice
```

## When to use

- Before a first publish, to confirm the notice text you need to ship.
- After a CLI upgrade, in case the notice text has changed.
- During submission review, to attach up-to-date notice text to your submission.

<Note>
  Ship the exact notice text emitted by the current CLI version. Do not modify or paraphrase.
</Note>
