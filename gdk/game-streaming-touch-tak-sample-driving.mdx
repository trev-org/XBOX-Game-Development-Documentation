Sample layout for a driving game.

## Design at a glance

| Element | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| Steering | Left thumb, single-axis joystick |
| Gas pedal | Right thumb, most accessible location |
| Braking (regular \+ handbrake) | Right thumb, secondary accessible locations |
| Interact / close | Left and right for occasional actions |
| Switch camera | Lower center |

## Remarks

The driving layout shows how a game can provide a simple optimized experience that lets the player focus on gameplay.

Key elements include:

- Steering via the left hand using a single-axis joystick.
- Gas pedal placed in the most easily accessible control location for the right hand.
- Two braking options (regular brake and handbrake) placed in the next most accessible locations.
- Two basic interaction buttons (interact and close) available for when needed.
- A switch-camera option available in the lower center to let the player change view.

## Availability
