**XDSP** is a hardware digital signal processor available on Xbox Series X|S. It offloads compute-intensive audio DSP work — FFT/IFFT, convolution (including with envelope), IR reduction, and enveloping — from the CPU.

<Note>
  XDSP is available on Series X|S. Xbox One titles fall back to CPU DSP paths or the XAudio2 built-in effects.
</Note>

## When to use XDSP

XDSP is designed for workloads that would otherwise saturate the CPU:

- **Convolution reverb** across many voice sends
- **Head-related transfer function (HRTF)** processing for spatial audio
- **Impulse-response** based effects (guitar amp cabs, environmental IRs)
- Custom **frequency-domain** effects

<Warning>
  XDSP is a shared hardware resource. Coordinate its use with any middleware or engine subsystems that may also target the DSP.
</Warning>

## Best practices

- Create clients up front and reuse for the life of the game.
- **16-byte alignment** on all buffers.
- Prefer fewer clients with more streams over many clients with few streams.
- Wait for command completion before freeing referenced buffers — the hardware may still be writing.
- On suspend, stop submitting new commands — in-flight commands complete normally.
