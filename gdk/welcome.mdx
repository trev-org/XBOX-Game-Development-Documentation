The XBOX Game Development Kit (GDK) is a unified, comprehensive SDK that gives you everything you need to develop, publish, and run games on XBOX Series X|S, XBOX One, and Windows PC. Built on a shared Game Core programming model, the GDK lets you write cross-platform game code once and target both console and PC with the same set of APIs. Whether you're integrating XBOX services such as achievements, multiplayer, and cloud saves, packaging your title for the Microsoft Store, or squeezing every bit of performance out of XBOX hardware, the GDK is your starting point. From a solo indie developer shipping their first PC title to a large studio shipping a cross-generational console blockbuster, the GDK scales to fit your workflow and engine of choice.

## Your path from zero to shipping

Getting a GDK title from zero to submission is a linear process. Follow it in order — later steps depend on earlier ones. Plan for **~2 weeks minimum** before you can download the GDK, because onboarding is gated by contract and concept-review cycles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete ID@XBOX onboarding">
    Sign the NDA, get your concept approved, sign the Title Licensing Agreement, and register in Partner Center. This unlocks the GDK download, XBOX services, and publishing.

    <Card title="Start onboarding" icon="arrow-right" href="/onboarding">
      Step-by-step ID@XBOX workflow.
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your dev PC and install the GDK">
    Install Visual Studio, the Windows SDK, and the GDK on a 64-bit Windows 10 (May 2019 Update or later) or Windows 11 machine. Confirm you can build a sample.

    <Card title="Setup & install" icon="arrow-right" href="/gdk/get-started">
      Environment setup and GDK install.
    </Card>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first title">
    Pick a Visual Studio project template (`Gaming.Desktop.x64` for PC, `Gaming.Xbox.Scarlett.x64` for Series X|S, or `Gaming.Xbox.XboxOne.x64` for XBOX One), reserve a product in Partner Center, and fill in your `MicrosoftGame.config`.

    <Card title="Build your first title" icon="arrow-right" href="/gdk/developing-new-titles">
      Project templates and first build.
    </Card>
  </Step>

      7 curated paths.
    </Card>
  </Step>
</Steps>
