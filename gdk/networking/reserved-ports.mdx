Xbox consoles have several reserved ports that GDK titles must be aware of.

<Warning>
  Do **not** pass any of these ports to [`bind`](/windows/desktop/api/winsock/nf-winsock-bind). Use of a reserved port produces indeterminate behavior and may fail testing.
</Warning>
