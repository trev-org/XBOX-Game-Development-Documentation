Once a Touch Adaptation Bundle passes CTA testing, publish it so the streaming service ships it to real clients on xCloud and Remote Play.

## Publishing checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the bundle">
    Run [`takx verify`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-verify-command) against the packed `.tab`. Fix any warnings before submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Version the bundle">
    Bump the version in `manifest.json` for every publish. The streaming service treats the version as the identity of the bundle for caching and rollback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include notices">
    Attach any required third-party notices with [`takx notice`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-notice-command).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm license">
    Confirm the license configuration with [`takx license`](/gdk/game-streaming-tak-command-line-license-command).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit through Partner Center">
    Submit the bundle through the Partner Center touch adaptation submission surface for your title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate in a Private Offering">
    Confirm the layout on real xCloud servers before you make it available to a broader audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The submission path is coordinated with your Microsoft Program Representative. Contact them for the current Partner Center flow and any account provisioning required for touch adaptation submissions.
</Note>

## After publishing

- The streaming service ships the new bundle to touch clients on their next session.
- xCloud caches bundles per version. Bumping the version forces a fresh download.
- Rollback is by re-publishing the previous version.
