Once your PC GDK title runs, layer in optional XBOX and PlayFab services as your design requires.

## Common adoption order

1. **Sign-in & identity** — [XUser](/gdk/user-model) and [PlayFab sign-in](/playfab/player-sign-in).
2. **Achievements & stats** — [XSAPI Achievements](/services/achievements) and [Stats](/reference/xsapi-stats).
3. **Cloud saves** — [XGameSave](/gdk/game-saves).
4. **Social** — [friends and recent players](/services/social).
5. **Multiplayer** — [MPSD \+ Game Chat 2](/services/multiplayer) or [PlayFab Lobby \+ Party](/playfab/multiplayer-lobby).
6. **Commerce** — [XStore](/publishing/xstore-deep-dive) and [DLC](/publishing/add-ons).
7. **Live ops** — [PlayFab live service management](/playfab/live-service-management).

Pick what fits your title — none are mandatory beyond what XR requires for the markets you target.
