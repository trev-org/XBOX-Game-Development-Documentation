Some XBOX services calls flow client → service, but many flow **server → service**. Your title's back end authenticates as itself (or as your title) and calls XBOX services on behalf of players. This is **service-to-service (S2S)** authentication.

Use S2S when your title needs to:

- Write authoritative stats or achievements from a dedicated server (client cannot be trusted).
- Batch-query presence or profile data for many users.
- Create or modify MPSD sessions from a matchmaker service.
- Publish global Title Storage blobs from a content pipeline.
- Ingest the **deleted accounts list** for GDPR compliance.

<Warning>
  Never embed publisher signing keys or business partner certificates in shipped client code. S2S credentials belong on a hosted back end.
</Warning>

## Authentication surfaces

S2S auth comes in a few flavours depending on the caller:

- **Title service authentication** — your back end authenticates as the **title**, using a business partner certificate registered in Partner Center. Used for stat/achievement writes and MPSD-S2S.
- **Web service authentication** — your back end authenticates as an **independent web service** (identified by a relying party). Used when your service is not tied to a specific title.
- **Website authentication** — companion websites delegate XBOX sign-in via a Partner Center-configured web endpoint.

## XSTS tokens (details)

Every authenticated XBOX services call carries an **XSTS token**. The token is a signed JWT-like envelope whose claims are validated by the receiving service.

Typical claims:

| Claim | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `xuid` | Signed-in XUID (empty for pure S2S). |
| `usr` | User class (user vs. dummy). |
| `agg` | Age group (Child, Teen, Adult). |
| `tid` | Title ID. |
| `sbx` | Sandbox ID (`RETAIL`, `XDKS.1`, ...). |
| `dvr` | Device region. |
| Privacy / permission flags | Communication, cross-network, UGC, etc. |

On your back end:

1. Validate the token signature against the XBOX services JWK.
2. Confirm `tid` matches your title's title ID.
3. Confirm `sbx` matches the environment (do not accept `RETAIL` traffic in a `DEV` service).
4. Enforce privilege / privacy claims before honouring the request.

The GDK ships a **sample XSTS token** and **token-claims reference** for validation testing.

## Custom service configuration in Partner Center

Before your back end can mint or receive S2S tokens, configure the pieces in Partner Center:

### Relying parties

A **relying party** is a named endpoint your back end will call. Register the URL and the certificate that identifies the caller. Relying parties gate what XSTS tokens can be issued for.

### Access policies

**Access policies** describe which relying parties can issue tokens with which claims. Set per-scope permissions here (e.g. "allow this relying party to write to my Title Storage container").

### Single sign-on (SSO)

If you have multiple XBOX-authenticated services (companion site, back-end telemetry), configure **single sign-on** so a signed-in user's session flows across them without re-prompting.

### Web services

Register the **web services** your back end exposes so XSTS tokens can target them. Web service configuration takes a service URL, a certificate, and permitted claim sets.

## S2S calls to XBOX services

Once configured, common S2S call patterns include:

- **Title service calls** — direct HTTPS from your back end to XBOX services REST endpoints, authenticated with a title service token.
- **MPSD service-to-service** — create sessions, migrate hosts, and update session state from a matchmaker or dedicated server. Preferred over client-authored sessions when the server owns match state.
- **Get deleted accounts list** — poll the deleted-accounts feed on a schedule (daily is typical) and remove the returned XUIDs from your persistent stores to stay GDPR-compliant.

## Website sign-in for title websites

Companion websites (event portals, LFG boards) can sign users in with their XBOX identity via **website authentication**:

- Configure the web endpoint in Partner Center.
- Redirect users through the XBOX sign-in flow.
- Receive an XSTS token scoped to your web service; validate as above.

## Best practices

- **Cache tokens** — S2S tokens live for hours; re-minting on every request is wasteful. Cache with a safety margin before expiry.
- **Rotate certificates** — Partner Center supports overlapping certs so you can rotate without downtime.
- **Log denied claims** — 403s from XBOX services usually mean a missing access policy or a stale relying-party binding.
- **Test with a DEV sandbox first** — never prove S2S out against RETAIL.
- **Segregate credentials by environment** — separate DEV / CERT / RETAIL certificate stores in your back end.
