The XBOX **People System** (formerly known as the Friends List) is the unified social graph that powers friends, followers, favorites, recent players, and clubs across XBOX consoles, Windows, and the mobile companion.

Your title reads the People System to render social UI (a friends list, invite pickers, join affordances) and writes to it via the platform APIs when the player adds a friend or leaves feedback.

## Concepts

- **Person** — any XUID the local user has a relationship with (friend, follower, favorite, recent player, club member).
- **Friend / follower** — mutual (`isFriend`) or one-way (`isFollowedByCaller` / `isFollowingCaller`) relationships. XBOX uses a follow-based model; a mutual follow is a friend.
- **Favorites** — user-prioritized people; the shell surfaces favorites at the top of the friends list.
- **Recent players** — session-derived; a player becomes "recent" when they share a Multiplayer Session Directory (MPSD) session or a Multiplayer Activity with the local user. See [Multiplayer Activity](/services/multiplayer-activity).
- **Blocked / avoided** — people the user has explicitly blocked; the platform enforces communication restrictions automatically, but titles should still respect the state for UI.

## Query the graph

Two API surfaces expose the People System:

- [XSAPI Social](/reference/xsapi-social) — raw relationship reads and writes. Best for one-off queries (does this XUID follow me?) and for server-side callers.
- [Social Manager](/reference/social-manager) — a cached, filterable **social user group** API designed for in-game UI. Register a group with the filter you want (all friends, favorites, in-title now), call `DoWork` each frame, and receive change events when the underlying graph updates.

<Tip>
  Use **Social Manager** for any live friends-list UI. It batches change notifications, avoids re-fetching the entire graph on every open, and integrates with Real-Time Activity (RTA) so presence, favorite state, and follow state stay fresh automatically.
</Tip>

## Displaying people

When rendering people in your title:

- Fetch the canonical display name (modern gamertag) via `XblProfileGetUserProfileAsync`; never reconstruct from cached parts.
- Show the **gamerpic** at the right resolution — the shell offers small, medium, and large variants; picking the right one avoids up-scaling artifacts.
- Sort favorites and in-title players above generic friends, matching shell conventions.
- Group by **presence** (online, in-title, offline) so the player finds someone joinable quickly.
- Respect privacy — check `XblPrivacyCheckPermissionAsync` before offering "view profile", "send message", or "invite" affordances.

See the sample flows for [getting a social relationship](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/gdk/docs/services/community/people-system) and handling a relationship change.

## Handling relationship changes

Subscribe to Social Manager change events (or raw RTA subscriptions) to keep UI in sync when:

- A friend signs in or out
- A follow / unfollow occurs
- A user is added to or removed from favorites
- Presence changes (device or title)
- A block or avoid is added on either side of the relationship

Coalesce these events on your side — a burst of sign-ins should not fire a redraw per event.

## People System vs. PlayFab Friends

For **cross-platform** social features (players who are not on XBOX), pair the XBOX People System with [PlayFab Friends & Groups](/playfab/friends-groups). The two graphs are complementary:

- XBOX People System — the source of truth for XBOX platform friendship, invites, and communication rules.
- PlayFab Friends & Groups — a cross-platform friend graph for players on PC (Steam, Epic), mobile, and other consoles, plus durable Groups (clans, guilds) with roles.
