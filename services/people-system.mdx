The XBOX **people system** is the unified social graph that powers friends, followers, recent players, and clubs across XBOX, Windows, and mobile.

## Concepts

- **Person** — any XUID the local user has a relationship with.
- **Friend / follower** — mutual or one-way relationships.
- **Favorites** — user-prioritized people.
- **Recent players** — driven by [Multiplayer Activity](/reference/xsapi-multiplayer-activity).

## Query the graph

- [XSAPI Social](/reference/xsapi-social) — raw relationship reads / writes.
- [Social Manager](/reference/social-manager) — cached, filterable social user groups for in-game UI.
