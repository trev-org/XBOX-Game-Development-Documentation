Player data on XBOX is split across multiple services depending on use case:

- **Profile** — gamertag, gamerpic, real name, account tier ([XSAPI Profile](/reference/xsapi-profile)).
- **Stats** — event-based and title-managed numeric / string stats ([XSAPI Stats](/reference/xsapi-stats)).
- **Leaderboards** — ranked stat queries ([XSAPI Leaderboard](/reference/xsapi-leaderboard)).
- **Title Storage** — per-user blobs ([XSAPI Title Storage](/reference/xsapi-title-storage)).
- **Connected Storage** — cross-device game saves ([XGameSave](/reference/xgamesave)).
- **Achievements** — per-user progress and unlock state.

## When to use PlayFab instead

For complex live-service data (custom segments, economy, A/B tests, custom analytics) use [PlayFab](/playfab/overview).

## Stats: event-based vs title-managed

Just like achievements, stats come in two flavours:

| Model | How stats update | When to choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Event-based (EB)** | Service derives stats from events you send | Default for new titles; supports leaderboards and EB achievements |
| **Title-managed (TM)** | Title pushes stat values directly | Legacy titles; very-low-volume stats; per-session counters |

### Authoring event-based stats

1. Author **events** in Partner Center (event name, fields, types)
2. Author **stat rules** that reduce events into stats (sum, max, last value, count distinct)
3. Author **leaderboards** that order users by a stat
4. Send events from the title via `XGameEventWrite`; the service updates stats and any dependent leaderboards within seconds

### Authoring title-managed stats

- Update with `XblTitleManagedStatsUpdateStatsAsync` (numeric and string stats)
- Read with `XblTitleManagedStatsGetStatsAsync`
- TM stats can also back leaderboards configured in Partner Center

### Featured stats

**Featured stats** show on the player's XBOX profile in the shell. Author them in Partner Center alongside regular stats:

- Up to a small fixed number of featured stats per title
- Choose stats that are meaningful at a glance ("Win %", "Hours played")
- Featured stats are available in both EB and TM models

### Stat configuration tips

- **Stat name / value** — names are case-sensitive and immutable post-publish
- **User stat rules** — published configuration is shipped with your service config; review with QA before promoting to RETAIL
- **Stat change notifications** — subscribe via RTA to react to your own stat changes (e.g. instant UI updates)
- **Stat scenarios** — many titles publish per-mode variants (e.g. `KillsRanked`, `KillsCasual`) rather than a single combined stat

## Leaderboards

Leaderboards rank users by a configured stat:

- **Event-based leaderboards** — derived from EB stats; updated automatically
- **Title-managed leaderboards** — backed by TM stats; updated when your title writes the stat
- **Social leaderboards** — pre-filtered to the caller's friend graph
- Author up to a configurable number of leaderboards per stat in Partner Center
- Query with `XblLeaderboardGetLeaderboardAsync`; specify ordering, view (global/social), and page size

## How the data platform works

The XBOX data platform is event-sourced:

1. Title sends typed events to ingest
2. Ingest validates schema and persists raw events
3. Stat rules execute on the event stream to produce stats
4. Leaderboards index stats and serve ranked reads
5. RTA pushes stat-change notifications to subscribed clients

Latency from event send to leaderboard update is typically seconds, sometimes longer under load. Surface a "syncing…" affordance instead of assuming an instant write.

## Game events

`XGameEvent` is the typed event channel:

- Author schemas in Partner Center; the title uses generated header constants for type-safe sends
- Pair with stats and achievements as above
- Also used by the broader data platform for opt-in telemetry and partner analytics
