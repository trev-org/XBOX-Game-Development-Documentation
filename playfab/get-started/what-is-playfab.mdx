PlayFab is a ready-made backend for your game. Instead of building and running your own servers, you use PlayFab's services for the things most games need: signing players in, matching them together, running an economy, storing progress, and understanding how they play.

It runs on Azure, so it scales from your first player to millions. Use only the pieces you need, and add more as your game grows.

## What you can do with PlayFab

- **Sign players in** across Xbox, Windows, Steam, PlayStation™ Network, Nintendo, iOS, Android, and more.
- **Host multiplayer** with matchmaking, lobbies, party networking, and dedicated servers.
- **Run an economy** with catalogs, stores, virtual currencies, and real-money purchases.
- **Save player progress** with profiles, statistics, and cloud saves.
- **Talk to your players** using push notifications, email, and in-game messages.
- **See what's happening** with real-time events, reports, and telemetry.
- **Add custom logic** with Azure Functions when you need server-side rules.

## The main service areas

### Identity

Sign in players once and keep their account with them across devices and platforms. PlayFab handles the login providers so you don't have to.

### Live service management

Update your game's config, send messages to players, and change behavior for specific groups — all without shipping a new build. Includes Game Manager (the web portal), Title Data, segmentation, and Azure Functions.

### Economy, monetization, and UGC

Set up items, currencies, and stores. Validate purchases so fraud can't slip through. Optionally let players create and share their own content (UGC).

### Multiplayer

Matchmaking pairs players together. Lobbies group them before a match. Party networking connects them in real time. Dedicated servers host the gameplay.

### Community

Leaderboards, friends, groups, and text/voice chat (with transcription and translation) — the social features players expect.

### Player progression

Player profiles, statistics, and game saves keep progress consistent no matter where players log in.

### Game data

Every action in your game can emit an event. Use PlayStream for real-time reactions, or Telemetry to collect data for later analysis. Built-in reports summarize the essentials.

## Compliance

PlayFab supports GDPR workflows (view and delete player data) and is used by COPPA-compliant games.
