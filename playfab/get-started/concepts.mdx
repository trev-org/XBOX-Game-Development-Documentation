These are the core ideas behind PlayFab. Understanding them makes everything else easier.

## Namespaces and titles

A **title** is your game in PlayFab. Each title has its own player accounts, data, and configuration.

A **namespace** is your studio-level container. It groups titles that belong together — for example, a game and its dev/staging versions, or several games from the same studio that share players.

Rules of thumb:

- A title belongs to exactly one namespace and can't be moved later.
- Titles in the same namespace can share player identities and data.
- Use separate titles for different environments (dev, staging, production) or major versions of your game.

## Game Manager

**Game Manager** is PlayFab's web portal. You'll use it to create titles, invite teammates, configure features, view analytics, and run day-to-day live operations.

Almost everything in Game Manager also has a REST API, so you can automate setup later.

## Developer accounts and permissions

You sign in with a Microsoft account to get a **developer identity**. Invite teammates and assign roles to control who can change what.

## Player identity

Before your game can call most PlayFab APIs, the player has to sign in. PlayFab supports the common login providers (Xbox, Steam, PlayStation™ Network, Nintendo, iOS, Android, and more) and issues a token your game uses for the rest of the session.

Once signed in, the player's account is where you store their progress, inventory, friends, bans, and anything else specific to that player.

## Title Data and Publisher Data

**Title Data** stores game-wide config that any client or server can read — things like feature flags, tuning values, or seasonal settings. Update it from Game Manager without shipping a new build.

**Publisher Data** works the same way but at the namespace level, so you can share it across every title in your studio.

Need to target a subset of players? Use **Segmentation** to group players by behavior or properties, or **Experimentation** to A/B test config on part of your player base.

## PlayStream: events and actions

**PlayStream** is a stream of events from your game. Some events come from PlayFab services automatically (like "player signed in"); others you send yourself.

You can wire up **rules** so that when a specific event happens, PlayFab runs an action — grant an item, ban a player, call your own webhook, or run custom code in Azure Functions.

## Telemetry and reports

If you just want to record what's happening (without reacting in real time), use **Telemetry**. Events land in a data lake you can explore in Game Manager, feed into built-in **reports**, or export to your own storage.

## Economy

**PlayFab Economy** handles items, currencies, stores, bundles, and purchases. It also supports **user-generated content (UGC)** if you want players to create and share their own content.

## Multiplayer

The multiplayer services — matchmaking, lobbies, party networking, chat, and dedicated servers — work independently or together. Use only the parts your game needs.
