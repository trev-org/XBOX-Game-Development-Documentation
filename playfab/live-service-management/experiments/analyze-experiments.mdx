Experiment results appear on the **Experiment details and scorecard** page. Scorecards regenerate on a cadence — first at 12 hours, then every 24 hours until the experiment ends.

The page lists:

- Name, description, start/end date, intended duration.
- Target audience and variant count.
- Actions (Stop, Run, Clone as draft), depending on state.

## During a run

Open the running experiment to see the interim scorecard. Use it directionally, not as final proof.

## After completion

On the **Completed** tab, select the experiment name for the final scorecard.

<Note>
  If the scorecard flags **Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM)**, see [Best practices](/playfab/live-service-management/experiments/best-practices) for how to resolve.
</Note>

## Deciding on a winner

- Confirm the primary metric moved in the expected direction with statistical significance.
- Check guardrail metrics (retention, revenue) didn't regress.
- Consider practical significance and cost to implement.
