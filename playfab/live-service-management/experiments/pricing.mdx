**Development Mode:** Experiments is free while your title has fewer than 1,000 player accounts.

**Live titles (Standard/Premium/Enterprise, pay-as-you-go):** Experiments is billed under the Insights credit system, with no consumption limits. Insights credits are a virtual currency shared by all Insights services.

View usage on the [billing summary page](https://developer.playfab.com/) — daily snapshot of month-to-date charges with an Experiments Insights-credit breakdown. Admin or billing permissions required.

## What contributes to Insights consumption

Metering begins when an experiment starts and stops when it ends. Three factors:

1. **Target audience size** — larger audience → more resources for assignment and variant-ID stamping.
2. **Metric computation** — job minutes scale with metric complexity and volume of the underlying events (`player_logged_in`, `player_realmoney_purchase`).
3. **Experiment duration** — longer runs consume more.

<Note>
  Experiment APIs and their PlayStream events are billed outside Insights credits — API calls via the profile read/write meter, events via the events meter.
</Note>
