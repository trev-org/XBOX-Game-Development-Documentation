Audit logs let you review the history of important changes made to your title by you or other developers. Use them to spot destructive or unexpected actions and react.

## Access audit logs

Open **Settings \> Title audit history**. Select any entry to view its complete details, or use the **SEARCH** button to filter.

Columns:

- **Event time** — time of the change and its author.
- **Log type** — the kind of change.
- **String change** and **Value** — short details of the change.
- **View** — opens the full JSON body of the change in a modal.

The JSON view contains the same fields as the table, plus a raw JSON body specific to each change type. Use it when you need the full record for support, incident response, or compliance.
