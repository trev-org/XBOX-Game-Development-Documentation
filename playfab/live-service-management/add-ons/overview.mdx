Add-ons are tools and services for extending and operating your game. Configure them in the **Add-ons** section of Game Manager.

Extend your game — and reduce SDK fatigue — with Add-ons from the PlayFab Marketplace. In most cases they can be provisioned and scaled with a few clicks, without a separate SDK. Add-ons cover analytics, community, payments, authentication, communication, multiplayer, and more.

## Common categories

- **Analytics** — route your data to external systems.
- **Community & moderation** — chat filtering, forums, voice.
- **Payments** — store, fraud detection, receipt validation.
- **Authentication** — identity providers beyond the built-in ones.
- **Communication** — SMTP for email, SMS.
- **Multiplayer** — Photon integrations for real-time networking.

See [Photon quickstart](/playfab/live-service-management/add-ons/photon-quickstart) for a full worked example.
