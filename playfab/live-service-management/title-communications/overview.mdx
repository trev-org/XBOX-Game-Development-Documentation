Title communications are the PlayFab tools for engaging your player community and keeping them coming back. The features are title news, email templates, and push notifications.

**Title news** broadcasts updates to your player base. Use it to announce upcoming events, share release notes, or send any other information you want players to have. See the [Title news quickstart](/playfab/live-service-management/title-communications/news/quickstart).

**Email messaging** sends customized emails to players on a schedule, in response to a PlayStream event, or by API call. Emails are fully customizable with HTML templates and can include player profile properties. See the [Email templates quickstart](/playfab/live-service-management/title-communications/emails/quickstart).

**Push notifications** send unlimited customized messages to a player's home screen in real time. See the [Push notifications quickstart](/playfab/live-service-management/title-communications/push-notifications/quickstart).
