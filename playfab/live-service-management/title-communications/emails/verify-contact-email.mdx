Adding a contact email to a player's profile emits a `player_added_contact_email` PlayStream event. Attach a rule that sends a verification email in response.

## Steps

1. Make sure the SMTP add-on is installed. See [SMTP add-on](/playfab/live-service-management/title-communications/emails/smtp-add-on).
2. In Game Manager, create an email template under **Content → Email Templates** using the built-in `$ConfirmationUrl$` token for the verification link.
3. Go to **Automation → Rules** and create a rule.
4. **Trigger:** `player_added_contact_email`.
5. **Action:** `Send email from template`, selecting your verification template.
6. Save the rule.

## Testing

Call `AddOrUpdateContactEmail` for a test player. The rule fires, PlayFab sends the verification email, and the player's status updates when they click the link.
