Email messaging sends customized emails to players:

- On a prearranged schedule.
- Triggered by a PlayStream event rule.
- On demand by API.

Email content is fully customizable HTML with player profile properties. Emails are sent from your own SMTP server so **From** and **Reply to** stay consistent with your brand.

Common uses:

- Verify a user's email address.
- Reset a password.
- Reach lapsed players with a gift link.
- Notify active players of an upcoming event.
