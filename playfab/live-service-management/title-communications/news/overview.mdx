---

Title News broadcasts information to your players. Use it to post updates such as upcoming in-game events, patch notes, future downtime, community activity, or anything else your players should know.

Each post contains a few basic elements:

- **Title**
- **Date**
- **Status**
- **Body**

Posts can publish immediately on creation, or be scheduled for a future time. After publishing in Game Manager or via the `AddNews` API, your client calls `GetTitleNews` to retrieve active posts and display them.
