**Rewarded ads** let players opt in to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward — currency, a consumable, or a bundle. PlayFab integrates with ad networks so grants happen server-side after a validated impression.

## Flow

1. Player taps "Watch ad for reward."
2. Client requests an ad from the network's SDK.
3. Player views the ad; the network calls PlayFab with a signed callback (SSV, server-side verification).
4. PlayFab validates the callback and grants the linked reward via Economy.
5. Client re-reads inventory.
