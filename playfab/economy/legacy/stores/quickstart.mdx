---
title: "Stores quickstart (legacy)"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a store and let a player purchase from it."
---

1. In **Engage → Economy → Stores**, create a new store and add catalog items with optional price overrides.
2. Client reads store contents with `GetStoreItems(storeId)`.
3. Player purchases: `PurchaseItem(itemId, price, storeId)`.
4. Server refreshes the player's inventory with `GetUserInventory`.

Store items respect virtual-currency prices. For real-money items, use the appropriate purchase flow for the platform.
