---
title: "Trading quickstart (legacy)"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Open, accept, and cancel a legacy Economy trade."
---

1. Player A calls `OpenTrade` with the item instance ids they're offering and (optionally) a target player id.
2. The trade appears to player B (poll or push).
3. Player B calls `AcceptTrade` referencing the trade id and the counter-items they're offering.
4. Server atomically swaps inventories.
5. Either side can `CancelTrade` before completion.

Enforce anti-fraud checks (max trade value per day, cooldowns) via PlayStream and CloudScript.
