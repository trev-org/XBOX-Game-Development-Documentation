Legacy Economy supports player-initiated trades between two players. A trade is an offer of specific item instances plus optional virtual currency, targeted at another player who can accept or decline.

## APIs

- `OpenTrade` — offer items and terms.
- `GetTradeStatus` — read a trade.
- `AcceptTrade` — counterparty completes the trade.
- `CancelTrade` — initiator withdraws.

## Server-mediated

Trades are executed server-side; each side's inventory is updated atomically only after both consent.
