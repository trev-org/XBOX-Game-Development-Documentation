---
title: "Best practices for store segmentation (legacy)"
sidebarTitle: "Store segmentation"
description: "Serve the right store to the right cohort."
---

- Define coarse segments first (`NewPlayers`, `Returning`, `Whales`), then refine as you learn.
- Keep one **canonical store** as the fallback so no segment ends up storeless.
- Never rely on the client to pick the segment — route through server logic that reads segment membership.
- Instrument every store impression as a PlayStream event to compare conversion per segment.

See also [Custom stores for player segments](/playfab/economy/legacy/tutorials/custom-stores-for-player-segments).
