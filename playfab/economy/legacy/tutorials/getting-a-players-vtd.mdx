---
title: "Getting a player's VTD (legacy)"
sidebarTitle: "Player VTD"
description: "Look up a player's Value-To-Date real-money spend."
---

**VTD** is a player's cumulative real-money spend. Legacy Economy tracks VTD as a virtual currency named `RM` (real money) that's incremented after each validated real-money purchase.

Read it with:

- `GetUserAccountInfo` (partial) or
- `GetPlayerStatistics` (custom rolled statistic) or
- The **Analytics → Players** report in Game Manager.

Use VTD to segment high-value players and to trigger loyalty rewards.
