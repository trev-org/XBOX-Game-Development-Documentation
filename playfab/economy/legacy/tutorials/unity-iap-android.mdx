---
title: "Getting started with Unity IAP (Android)"
sidebarTitle: "Unity IAP + Android"
description: "Connect Unity IAP on Android to legacy Economy."
---

1. Import Unity IAP and the PlayFab Unity SDK.
2. In Google Play Console, create managed products with matching product IDs.
3. Configure Unity IAP with those IDs and initialize the IAP service.
4. On purchase success, hand the Google receipt token to PlayFab via [`ValidateGooglePlayPurchase`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/client/platform-specific-methods/validate-google-play-purchase).
5. Player inventory is granted server-side after validation.

Source: [`tutorials/getting-started-with-unity-iap-android.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/economy-monetization/economy/tutorials/getting-started-with-unity-iap-android.md).
