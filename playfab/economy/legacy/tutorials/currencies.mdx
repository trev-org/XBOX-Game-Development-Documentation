Define virtual currencies in **Engage → Economy → Currencies**:

- **Code** — two-character identifier (`GO`, `RM`).
- **Initial deposit** — granted to every new player.
- **Recharge rate** and **max amount** — for energy-style resources.

Grant with `AddUserVirtualCurrency`, spend with `SubtractUserVirtualCurrency` or via a store `PurchaseItem`.
