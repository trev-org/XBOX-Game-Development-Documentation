sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Catalog items in legacy Economy."
---

Legacy Economy items belong to a **catalog version** (`primary`, or a named alternate). Each item has:

- `ItemId`, `DisplayName`, `Description`.
- `VirtualCurrencyPrices` and `RealCurrencyPrices` maps.
- `Tags`, `CustomData`.
- Optional `Consumable`, `Container`, `Bundle` behavior.

Items in a catalog are copied into a player's inventory on grant, purchase, or bundle-open. Inventory instances have their own `ItemInstanceId`.
