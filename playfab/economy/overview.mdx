PlayFab Economy powers your in-game store, catalog, and player inventory. The current generation is **Economy v2**, which adds bundles, subscriptions, real-money prices, and User-Generated Content (UGC). A **legacy economy** is still supported for existing titles.

## What Economy does

- Author a **catalog** of items, currencies, bundles, and subscriptions.
- Track each player's **inventory** across collections.
- Sell items via **stores** with real money or virtual currency.
- Grant rewards from server logic, PlayStream rules, or scheduled tasks.
- Publish and moderate **User-Generated Content**.
- Protect revenue with **fraud prevention** and receipt validation.
