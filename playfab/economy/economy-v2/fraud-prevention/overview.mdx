---

PlayFab Economy applies fraud checks at every mutation:

- **Server-only mutations** — client tokens cannot grant items; only entity tokens with server scope may.
- **Receipt replay defense** — marketplace redemptions keep a de-duplicated transactions ledger.
- **Redemption cool-downs** — rate limits per player and per code.
- **UGC moderation** — automated \+ human review \+ player reports.
- **PlayStream \+ segmentation signals** — build custom rules that flag suspicious behavior.
