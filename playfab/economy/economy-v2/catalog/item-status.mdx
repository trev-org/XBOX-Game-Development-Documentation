Catalog items move through a lifecycle:

- **Draft** — authored but not visible to clients.
- **Under Review** — pending moderation (typical for UGC).
- **Published** — visible in the client-facing catalog.
- **Deleted** — removed from the catalog. Existing inventory grants persist.

## Transitions

- Draft → Published: automatic after a manual publish, or on moderation approval for UGC.
- Published → Deleted: marks the item as unavailable but does not revoke existing player inventories.
- Under Review → Published or Draft (rejected): decided by moderators.
