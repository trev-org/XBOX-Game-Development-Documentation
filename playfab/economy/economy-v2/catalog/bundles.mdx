A **bundle** is a catalog item whose contents are one or more other catalog items and/or currencies. When a bundle is granted or purchased, its contents are unpacked into the player's inventory.

## Use cases

- Starter packs.
- Season passes.
- Reward-track payouts.

## Authoring

Create a new catalog item with `ContentType = "Bundle"`. Add child items and quantities. Set price(s) if the bundle is directly purchasable.

## Behavior

When a player receives a bundle:

- Each contained item is added to their inventory using its own stack rules.
- Contained currencies are added to their currency totals.
- The bundle itself is _not_ held in inventory; it's a grant-time construct.
