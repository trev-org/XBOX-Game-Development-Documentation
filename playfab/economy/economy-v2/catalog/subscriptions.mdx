Subscriptions are catalog items whose ownership renews on a schedule via an external marketplace (Microsoft Store, Apple, Google, Steam).

## What Economy tracks

- Current subscription state (`Active`, `Grace`, `Expired`).
- Renewal timestamp reported by the marketplace.
- Grace-period behavior when a renewal fails.

## Server responsibilities

- Validate marketplace receipts on renewal.
- Grant subscription-only rewards through server logic or PlayStream when the state transitions to `Active`.
- Gate content in-game against the subscription entitlement, not just its initial purchase.
