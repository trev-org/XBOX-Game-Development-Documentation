Players can submit a **rating** (1–5 stars) and optional **review** text on any catalog item. Ratings and reviews are especially useful for UGC — they help other players find good content and provide signal for moderation.

## APIs

- `ReviewItem` — submit a rating \+ review.
- `GetItemReviews` — paged list of reviews for an item.
- `GetItemReviewSummary` — aggregated rating counts and average.
- `ReportItem` and `ReportItemReview` — flag content or reviews for moderator attention.

Reviews may themselves be moderated (see [UGC moderation](/playfab/economy/economy-v2/ugc/moderation)).
