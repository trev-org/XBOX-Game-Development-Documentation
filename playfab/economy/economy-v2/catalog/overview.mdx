The Economy v2 catalog is where you define everything a player can own, buy, or receive:

- **Items** — durable, consumable, or virtual goods with metadata.
- **Currencies** — virtual currencies with per-title cap and daily grant rules.
- **Bundles** — groups of items sold or granted together.
- **Stores** — curated views into the catalog with segmented prices.
- **Subscriptions** — recurring entitlements.
- **UGC** — player-authored content that lives in the same catalog.

Catalog items are versioned, localized, and can be tagged and filtered. Use **content types** to differentiate item kinds (`Weapon`, `Skin`, `Bundle`, `UGC`).
