A **deep link** is a URL attached to a catalog item that points to an external destination — typically a marketplace product page or a related web resource.

## Use cases

- Redirect a client to the platform storefront for real-money purchase.
- Point at web-hosted item details.
- Link a UGC item to its author's page.

Deep links have a **platform** discriminator (e.g. `MicrosoftStore`, `AppleAppStore`, `GooglePlay`, `Steam`), so the client can pick the right link for the runtime.
