Economy v2 supports first-class **real-money prices** on catalog items — stored as currency-typed amounts (USD, EUR, JPY, ...) alongside any virtual-currency prices.

## Behavior

- Reported to analytics as real-money revenue when redeemed via a marketplace.
- Displayed to players in their local currency when available.
- Enforced only through validated marketplace transactions; the client cannot self-report a real-money purchase.
