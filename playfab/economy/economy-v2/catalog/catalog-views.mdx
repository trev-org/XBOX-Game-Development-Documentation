title: "Catalog views"
sidebarTitle: "Catalog views"
description: "Search-optimized views that expose subsets of the catalog to clients."
---

**Catalog views** are search-optimized projections of the catalog surfaced to clients via [SearchItems](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/catalog/search-items). Instead of dumping the whole catalog, you offer curated queries: _featured_, _new arrivals_, _on sale_, _by tag_.

Behind the scenes each view is an OData-style filter run against the published catalog.
