Three mechanisms shape catalog items:

- **Content type** — required, defines the kind of item (`Weapon`, `Skin`, `Bundle`, `Currency`). Configure allowed content types in **Catalog settings**.
- **Tags** — free-form labels for filtering and search. Typical: `season2`, `rare`, `holiday`.
- **Properties** — typed key/value custom data attached per item (strings, numbers, booleans, JSON). Read by your game to drive behavior.

All three are queryable in `SearchItems`.
