A **store** is a curated view over the catalog with its own item list and price overrides.

## Common patterns

- **Featured store** — hand-picked highlights on the main menu.
- **Segment-specific store** — discounts for `NewPlayers`, `Whales`, or region-based cohorts.
- **Event store** — seasonal, limited-time.

## Authoring

Stores are catalog items with `ContentType = "Store"`. Each entry references another catalog item and can override its virtual-currency price.

## Segmentation

Combine stores with [player segmentation](/playfab/live-service-management/segmentation/overview) and [player-segmented monetization](/playfab/economy/economy-v2/player-segmented/segment-actions) to run targeted promotions.
