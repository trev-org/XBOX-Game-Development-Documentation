Clients discover catalog contents through [SearchItems](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/catalog/search-items):

- **Filter** by OData-style expressions on tags, content type, properties, or price.
- **OrderBy** by `creationDate`, `lastModifiedDate`, `title`, or numeric properties.
- **ContinuationToken** for paging through large result sets.

## Best practices

- Pre-define common queries as **catalog views** for one-tap access.
- Cache results client-side and refresh on catalog change events.
- Never send server-side price lists through a client filter — keep server-only content behind a server-side entity token.
