When an item's real-money price varies by platform or region, PlayFab keeps a canonical price on the catalog item and reconciles the actual paid amount at redemption time.

## Pattern

- Store the intended reference price on the catalog item (e.g. `USD 4.99`).
- Platform storefronts localize the display price and process the actual payment.
- On redemption, the receipt reports the true paid amount and currency; PlayFab records it against the transaction.
- Analytics can slice revenue by paid currency and reference currency.
