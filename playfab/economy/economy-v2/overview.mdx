Economy v2 is the current generation of PlayFab Economy. Compared to legacy Economy it adds bundles, subscriptions, first-class real-money prices, richer content types, and User-Generated Content.

## Building blocks

- **Catalog** — items, currencies, bundles, stores, subscriptions.
- **Inventory** — per-player, organized into collections with stacks.
- **Marketplace** — external storefronts (Microsoft Store, Apple, Google, Steam) linked to Economy items with receipt validation.
- **UGC** — player-authored content with moderation.
- **Fraud prevention** — receipt replay defense, abuse signals, redemption limits.
- **Player-segmented monetization** — targeted offers and segment-triggered actions.
