Each player has an **inventory** of items and currencies. Inventory is server-authoritative; only server-scoped tokens or CloudScript may mutate it.

## Building blocks

- **Item instances** — an entry in a player's inventory referencing a catalog item.
- **Stacks** — identical items grouped by quantity to reduce entry count.
- **Collections** — named subsets of a player's inventory (e.g. `"default"`, `"vault"`, `"loadout1"`).
- **Transactions** — immutable ledger of every add/remove.
