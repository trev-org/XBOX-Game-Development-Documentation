Every inventory mutation is recorded as a **transaction** with:

- Timestamp.
- Operator (client, server, CloudScript, marketplace, PlayStream).
- Items and currencies affected.
- Result state (success, failure).

## APIs

- [`GetTransactionHistory`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/get-transaction-history).

## Use for

- Player-support lookups ("where did my item go?").
- Detecting duplicate grants during refactors.
- Fraud investigations.

History is append-only and cannot be edited.
