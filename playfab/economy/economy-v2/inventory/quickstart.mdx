## APIs

- [`AddInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/add-inventory-items) — grant items/currency.
- [`GetInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/get-inventory-items) — paged read.
- [`SubtractInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/subtract-inventory-items) — consume/spend.
- [`TransferInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/transfer-inventory-items) — move between collections or players.
- [`PurchaseInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/purchase-inventory-items) — spend a virtual currency price in one call.
- [`GetInventoryCollectionIds`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/get-inventory-collection-ids).

## Golden rules

- Server-only mutations. Never grant items from a trusted client.
- Pass the previously returned **ETag** on writes for concurrency.
- Use `IdempotencyId` on grants that may be retried.
