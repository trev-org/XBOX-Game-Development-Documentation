Targeted offers combine stores, segments, and player-context to surface the right monetization at the right time.

## Patterns

- **Segment-specific stores** — hide/show a store based on segment membership.
- **Personalized prices** — the same catalog item offered at different virtual-currency prices per store.
- **Time-bounded offers** — use catalog item `StartDate`/`EndDate` to create limited windows.
- **Bundle up-sells** — promote a bundle to players who just consumed a matching consumable.

## Client behavior

Call `SearchItems` scoped to the store; the server returns only the items and prices that segment allows. Never rely on the client to filter offers.
