A **segment action** is a rule that fires when a player transitions in or out of a segment (see [Segmentation](/playfab/live-service-management/segmentation/overview)).

## Monetization uses

- Grant a welcome bundle to `NewPlayers`.
- Offer a targeted starter pack to `NonSpenders`.
- Grant a loyalty currency to `Whales`.
- Show a `WinbackOffer` to `Churned` players.

## Authoring

- Define the segment in **Engage → Segmentation → New segment**.
- Attach an "On entered segment" or "On left segment" action.
- Common actions: grant catalog item, grant currency, send push, run CloudScript.

All actions run server-side and are trusted; the client cannot skip or spoof them.
