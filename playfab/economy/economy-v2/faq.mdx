## Should new titles use v2 or legacy?

**v2.** Legacy is maintained for existing titles only.

## Can I migrate a legacy title to v2?

Yes, but plan it carefully. Migration involves re-mapping catalog items, re-issuing inventories, and reconfiguring stores. See the [source FAQ](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/economy-monetization/economy-v2/faq.md) for the current guidance.

## Are inventory writes eventually consistent?

Economy inventory writes are strongly consistent within a single collection and use ETags for concurrency. Cross-player and cross-collection reads may lag briefly under high write load.

## Where do I look up API rate limits?

See [Limits](/playfab/economy/economy-v2/limits) and [Throttling](/playfab/live-service-management/throttling/overview).

## Does Economy support real money without a marketplace?

No. Real-money grants must go through a validated marketplace receipt.
