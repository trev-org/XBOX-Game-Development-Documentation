Marketplace integration lets players buy Economy catalog items on external storefronts — Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam — and receive them in their PlayFab inventory once the platform receipt is validated by PlayFab.

## Flow

1. The client completes a purchase on the platform storefront.
2. The client hands the receipt/token to your server or to PlayFab.
3. PlayFab validates the receipt with the platform.
4. On success, the linked Economy items are granted to the player's inventory.
