---
title: "Google Play integration"
sidebarTitle: "Google"
description: "Link Economy catalog items to Google Play billing products."
---

Create a managed product or subscription in Google Play Console, then link its SKU to the catalog item.

1. Create the product in **Google Play Console → Monetize → Products**.
2. Note the **Product ID** and product type (managed, subscription).
3. Add a **Google** marketplace mapping to the catalog item.
4. Configure Real-time Developer Notifications (RTDN) via Pub/Sub for subscription events.

Source: [`marketplace-integrations/google.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/economy-monetization/economy-v2/marketplace/marketplace-integrations/google.md).
