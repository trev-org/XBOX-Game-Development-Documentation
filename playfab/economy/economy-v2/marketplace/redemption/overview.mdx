Redemption is how a platform receipt turns into inventory. It's the enforcement point for both grants and fraud checks.

## APIs

- [`RedeemMicrosoftStoreInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/redeem-microsoft-store-inventory-items).
- [`RedeemAppleAppStoreInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/redeem-apple-app-store-inventory-items).
- [`RedeemGooglePlayInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/redeem-google-play-inventory-items).
- [`RedeemSteamInventoryItems`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/economy/inventory/redeem-steam-inventory-items).

## Idempotency

All platform receipts are single-use. PlayFab tracks a redeemed-transactions ledger; the same receipt cannot be redeemed twice. See [Fraud prevention](/playfab/economy/economy-v2/fraud-prevention/overview).
