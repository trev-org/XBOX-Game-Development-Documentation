sidebarTitle: "Google subscription"
description: "Redeem and renew Google Play subscriptions."
---

Google Play subscriptions require:

- **Initial redemption** — same flow as one-off purchase.
- **Renewal notifications** — configure Google Real-time Developer Notifications (Cloud Pub/Sub) so PlayFab receives renewal, cancellation, and pause events.
- **Client verification** — on launch, query the current subscription from Play Billing and compare with PlayFab state.

Gate features against the current subscription state, not just the initial purchase.
