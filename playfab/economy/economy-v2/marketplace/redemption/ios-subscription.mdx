---

Apple auto-renewable subscriptions require:

- **Initial redemption** — same flow as one-off purchase; PlayFab records the entitlement.
- **Renewal handling** — configure App Store Server Notifications so Apple pushes renewal / lapse events. PlayFab updates the subscription state (`Active`, `Grace`, `Expired`).
- **Client verification** — on launch, refresh the current entitlement from Apple and compare with PlayFab's state.

Gate content against the subscription's _current_ state, not just the initial purchase.
