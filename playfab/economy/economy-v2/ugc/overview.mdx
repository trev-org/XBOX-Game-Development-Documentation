UGC lives in the Economy v2 catalog as items with `ContentType = "UGC"`. Players author, publish, and consume UGC the same way as first-party items.

## Lifecycle

1. **Author** — client creates a draft catalog item and uploads content (a level, a skin, a mod).
2. **Submit for review** — draft moves to `Under Review`.
3. **Moderate** — automated \+ human moderation approves or rejects.
4. **Publish** — published UGC appears in search and can be granted to players' inventories.
5. **Rate & report** — players rate content and can report abuse.
