## Enable

1. In Game Manager, open **Settings → Economy (V2) → UGC** and enable UGC.
2. Define UGC content types (e.g. `Level`, `Skin`, `Mod`).
3. Configure moderation settings (see [Moderation](/playfab/economy/economy-v2/ugc/moderation)).

## Author from the client

- Sign in with a normal player entity token.
- Call `CreateDraftItem` (from Economy Catalog API) with the desired content type, tags, and metadata.
- Upload the payload via presigned URL returned by the API.
- Call `PublishDraftItem` to move it to `Under Review`.

## Discover

- Use `SearchItems` with `ContentType eq 'UGC'` and OrderBy `rating average desc` or `creationDate desc`.

## Grant to a player

- Same as any catalog item: `AddInventoryItems` (server-only).
