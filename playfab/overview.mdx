[PlayFab](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/games/products/playfab/) is Microsoft's backend platform for live games. It integrates directly with XBOX services and the Microsoft GDK, making it the natural choice for titles shipping on XBOX and Windows.

New to PlayFab? Start with [What is PlayFab?](/playfab/get-started/what-is-playfab) for the concepts, then head to [Foundation Mode onboarding](/playfab/get-started/foundation-mode-onboarding) to set up your title.
