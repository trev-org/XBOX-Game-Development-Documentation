PlayFab Party is the low-latency transport for cross-platform voice and reliable / unreliable game data on XBOX, PC, mobile, and other consoles.

## What Party provides

- **Voice chat** — codec-quality voice with mute, push-to-talk, and per-user gain.
- **Data plane** — reliable and unreliable messages over UDP with NAT traversal.
- **Endpoints and chat permissions** — model speakers / listeners separately for spectators and broadcasters.
