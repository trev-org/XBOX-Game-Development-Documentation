PlayFab Party is the low-latency transport for cross-platform voice and reliable / unreliable game data on XBOX, PC, mobile, and other consoles.

## What Party provides

- **Voice chat** \\u2014 codec-quality voice with mute, push-to-talk, per-user gain, real-time audio manipulation, and transcription / translation.
- **Data plane** \\u2014 reliable and unreliable messages over UDP with NAT traversal.
- **Endpoints and chat permissions** \\u2014 model speakers / listeners separately for spectators and broadcasters.
- **Direct peer-to-peer connectivity** \\u2014 opt-in P2P links between endpoints in the same network for reduced latency and cost.\\n- **Invitations and security model** \\u2014 who can join, who can be muted, how permissions propagate.

## Objects and their relationships\\n\\nParty models a session as a small graph:\\n\\n- **Network** \\u2014 the top-level session all players join.\\n- **Endpoint** \\u2014 a logical connection per player (or per role, e.g. spectator).\\n- **Device** \\u2014 the physical machine backing one or more endpoints.\\n- **Chat control** \\u2014 mic \+ speaker control per endpoint, with permission rules.\\n- **Text chat** \\u2014 shared text channel with moderation hooks.\\n\\nUnderstanding these objects makes debugging permissions and reconnect flows much easier.\\n\\n## Regions and QoS\\n\\nParty runs in Azure regions. Clients ping regional QoS beacons at startup and pick the lowest-latency region for the network. Multi-region networks are supported for cross-region play but incur higher latency.\\n\\n## How it relates to other Party pages
