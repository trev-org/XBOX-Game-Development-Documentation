User-Generated Content (UGC) lets your players create catalog items that other players can discover, buy, and use — levels, skins, decals, mods, entire game modes. PlayFab Economy v2 handles storage, publishing, discovery, and moderation so you don't have to run your own asset pipeline.

## The UGC lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ingest">
    A player uses your title's authoring flow to create an item. Your title uploads the asset payload (image, mesh, JSON, whatever) and metadata to PlayFab. The item enters the catalog with a `Draft` status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Moderate">
    Draft items sit in a moderation queue. You can gate publication behind human review, an automated content-safety pipeline (Azure Content Safety, custom ML), or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Approved items go live and become discoverable through search, browsing, and player recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Other players find the item through in-game search, curated stores, or featured collections. The creator earns whatever revenue share you configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate">
    Creators can update items in place (subject to re-moderation) and pull items down. Ratings and comments feed back into ranking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content types and tags

Define **content types** in Game Manager to describe what a UGC item is — a level, a decal, a texture. Each content type constrains:

- Allowed file formats and max size.
- Required and optional metadata fields.
- Which properties are visible to which viewers (creators can preview drafts; the public sees only published items).

Use **tags** for cross-cutting classification — `holiday`, `winter-2025`, `high-quality` — that drive discovery and store curation.

## Moderation

PlayFab supports two moderation modes:

- **Pre-publish moderation** — nothing is public until a moderator (human or automated) approves. Higher friction, safer.
- **Post-publish moderation** — items go live immediately and are audited afterwards. Lower friction, requires reactive tooling.

Build a moderation pipeline as an [Azure Function](/playfab/cloudscript) triggered by the `item_moderation_pending` event: it can call Azure Content Safety for image and text analysis, then approve or reject through the PlayFab admin API. Escalate anything the model can't decide to human review.

## Fraud and abuse

UGC is a common vector for platform abuse. See [Fraud prevention & receipt validation](/playfab/fraud-prevention) for the specific mitigations. At minimum:

- Rate-limit ingest per creator.
- Hash-check uploads against known bad content.
- Provide a low-friction in-game report flow.
- Give moderators one-click take-down that cascades to already-purchased copies (refund policy up to you).

## Revenue share

If your title lets creators sell their UGC for hard or soft currency, configure the split in Game Manager — for example, 70% creator / 30% platform. PlayFab tracks the accrued balance per creator entity; payout is up to your publisher's payments pipeline.

## Best practices

- Start with a small, curated allow-list of creators before opening the floodgates.
- Make moderation state visible to creators — opaque "pending forever" queues erode trust.
- Instrument every step (draft, submit, approve, reject, publish, purchase) as PlayStream events so you can spot pipeline stalls.
- Version content types so you can evolve required metadata without invalidating older items.
