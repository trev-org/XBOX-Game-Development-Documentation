PlayFab Community is a set of cross-platform tools and services that enable game creators to build engaging social experiences and foster long-term player connections. It's organized into three areas:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Player Associations" icon="user-group" href="/playfab/friends-groups">
    Friends lists and Groups (clans, guilds) with a shared identity model that works across titles in a publisher.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Leaderboards" icon="trophy" href="/playfab/community-leaderboards">
    Cross-platform, entity-aware leaderboards — seasonal, group, manual-tier, and stat-linked.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Synchronous Player Communications" icon="microphone" href="/playfab/voice-chat">
    Voice chat via PlayFab Party, with permissions, muting, text moderation, noise suppression, and TTS / STT accessibility.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How Community relates to other pillars

- **Identity** — Community objects (Friends, Groups) are keyed by PlayFab entities. See [Identity](/playfab/identity).
- **Multiplayer** — Voice runs on top of [Party](/playfab/party); Lobby / Matchmaking often consume friends and group state.
- **Player progression** — [Community Leaderboards](/playfab/community-leaderboards) and [Statistics](/playfab/player-progression) intersect — stats power scores, leaderboards rank them.
- **Live service management** — Segments can key on friend count, group membership, or leaderboard rank.

## Accessibility

Community features have specific accessibility guidance because they involve real-time human communication. See:

- Text-to-speech and text-input UX guidelines
- Speech-to-text and text-display UX guidelines

Both are covered on [Voice chat](/playfab/voice-chat) with concrete UX patterns to include in your title.
