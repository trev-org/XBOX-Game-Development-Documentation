These samples show how to use PlayFab features in real projects. Clone, build, and run.

| Sample | Platforms | Engine | Demonstrates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [PlayFabEconomy](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Samples/Android/PlayFabCommerce) | Android | Android Studio | Android in-app billing paired with PlayFab Economy — login, in-app purchases. |
| [PlayFabSignIn](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Samples/Unity/PlayFabSignIn) | iOS, Android, PC | Unity | Email/password sign-in, account creation, guest login, and remembered sessions. |
| [PlayFabEventsUnity](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Samples/Unity/PlayFabEventsUnity) | Android, iOS, PC | Unity | Sending player, title, and character events. |
| [PlayFabLeaderboardsUnity](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Samples/Unity/PlayFabLeaderboardsUnity) | Android, iOS, PC | Unity | Reading and writing leaderboards — high score, best time, cumulative. |
| [ThunderRumble](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Samples/Win32/ThunderRumble) | PC, Xbox | C\+\+ DirectX | Full end-to-end multiplayer using Multiplayer Servers — login, matchmaking, servers. |
