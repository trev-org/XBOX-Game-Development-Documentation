Each recipe is a small, focused example that shows how to build a familiar mechanic on top of PlayFab services.

## Player retention

- [Daily Prize Wheel](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Recipes/PrizeWheel) — Give players a randomized gift every day.
- [Progressive Reward System](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Recipes/ProgressiveRewards) — Reward consecutive daily sign-ins based on streak length.

## User acquisition

- [Referral Code Bonus](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Recipes/ReferralCodes) — Generate codes players share with friends; both sides get a reward.
- [Cross-Promotional Rewards](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Recipes/SimpleCrossPromotion) — Reward players who play more than one of your titles.

## Monetization

- [Regenerating Currency](https://github.com/PlayFab/PlayFab-Samples/tree/master/Recipes/RegeneratingCurrency) — A play-limiting mechanic (like Candy Crush lives) that refills over time.
