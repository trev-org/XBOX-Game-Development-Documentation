PlayFab models identity in two layers: a **master player account** (cross-title) and a **title player account** (per-title).

## Entity model

- **Master account** — a single identity per provider linkage (XBOX XUID, Steam, custom ID, ...).
- **Title player** — the title-scoped projection used for stats, inventory, and segments.
- **Entity tokens** — short-lived tokens that authorize client and server calls.

## Sign-in flows on XBOX

The recommended XBOX flow is **`LoginWithXbox`** using an XSTSToken from XSAPI. See [Player sign-in](/playfab/player-sign-in) for code.
