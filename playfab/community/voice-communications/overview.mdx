Synchronous Player Communications (SPC) on PlayFab covers voice chat concepts and their moderation and accessibility features:

- Chat permissions and muting
- Text moderation
- Noise suppression
- Real-time audio manipulation
- Text-to-speech (TTS) and speech-to-text (STT) UX guidelines
- Audio troubleshooting
