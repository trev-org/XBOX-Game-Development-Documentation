---
title: "Friends"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Direct 1:1 player relationships with optional tags."
---

Friends lists let each player maintain a directed list of other PlayFab players, optionally tagged (for example: `favorite`, `blocked`, `imported-from-steam`). Use tags to filter and organize the friends UX in your game.

Source: [Friends](https://github.com/microsoft/playfab-docs/tree/master/sources/playfab-docs/community/associations/friends)
