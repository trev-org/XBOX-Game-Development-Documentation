description: "Durable communities with roles and shared entity state."
---

Groups are entity-based communities (clans, guilds, teams). Each Group is a first-class Entity with its own state, roles, invitations, and application/acceptance flow. Group members can share Entity Objects and Files scoped to the Group.

Source: [Groups](https://github.com/microsoft/playfab-docs/tree/master/sources/playfab-docs/community/associations/groups)
