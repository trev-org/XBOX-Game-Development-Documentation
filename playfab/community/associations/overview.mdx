PlayFab Player Associations gives you two building blocks:

- **Friends** — direct 1:1 player relationships with optional tags.
- **Groups** — durable multi-player entities (clans, guilds) with roles, membership, and shared entity state.

Use Friends when you need lightweight social graphs. Use Groups when you need shared entity state, roles, and long-lived community structure.

Source: [Player Associations](https://github.com/microsoft/playfab-docs/tree/master/sources/playfab-docs/community/associations)
