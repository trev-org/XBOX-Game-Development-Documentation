title: "Leaderboards"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Entity-aware, community-scoped ranking."
---

PlayFab Leaderboards rank any entity (player, character, group) by a numeric score with tie-breakers, seasonal resets, manual tiers, and stat-linked updates. Prefer entity Leaderboards over the legacy statistics-based Leaderboards for new games.

Source: [Leaderboards](https://github.com/microsoft/playfab-docs/tree/master/sources/playfab-docs/community/leaderboards)
