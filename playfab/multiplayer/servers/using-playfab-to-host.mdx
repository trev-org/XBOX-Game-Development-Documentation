---

Choose PlayFab Multiplayer Servers when you want:

- Multi-region hosting without operating datacenter contracts.
- Standby scaling and dynamic capacity out of the box.
- Deep integration with PlayFab Matchmaking, Lobby, and analytics.
- Managed Azure networking and cert lifecycle.

Host yourself when you have specific hardware, custom networking, on-prem, or unusual OS requirements.
