The **Wrapper** sample launches an existing server binary as a child process, then integrates GSDK on its behalf. Use it when you can't recompile the server (third-party binaries, community mods).

The wrapper watches the child's stdout for readiness markers, forwards heartbeats to the agent, and exits cleanly when the child exits.

Source: [`wrapper-sample.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/servers/wrapper-sample.md).
