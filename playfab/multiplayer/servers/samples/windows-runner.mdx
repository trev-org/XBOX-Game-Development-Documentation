**Windows Runner** is the simplest published sample: a Windows console app that:

- Initializes GSDK.
- Reports `ReadyForPlayers`.
- Sleeps until allocated.
- Logs allocation payload and connected players.
- Exits cleanly on shutdown.

Use it to validate your account, quota, and pipeline before wiring up your real server.

Source: [`windows-runner-sample.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/servers/windows-runner-sample.md).
