The **MPS allocator** sample is a small ASP.NET service that:

- Accepts custom allocation requests from your clients.
- Applies business rules (per-title concurrency caps, region overrides, cheat-check gates).
- Forwards to [`RequestMultiplayerServer`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/multiplayer/multiplayer-server/request-multiplayer-server).
- Returns the resulting connection info.

Use it when you need logic between the client and PlayFab — or when your matchmaker is homegrown rather than PlayFab Matchmaking.
