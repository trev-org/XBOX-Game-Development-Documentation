---
title: "Enable Multiplayer Servers"
sidebarTitle: "Enable"
description: "Turn Multiplayer Servers on for a title."
---

1. Sign in to [Game Manager](https://developer.playfab.com/).
2. Open your title → **Multiplayer** → **Servers**.
3. Click **Enable Multiplayer Servers**. This provisions a per-title Azure resource, which can take a few minutes.
4. Complete the linked Azure subscription flow so PlayFab can bill VM usage to your subscription.

Once enabled, you can upload builds and manage VMs.

Source: [`enable-playfab-servers.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/servers/enable-playfab-servers.md).
