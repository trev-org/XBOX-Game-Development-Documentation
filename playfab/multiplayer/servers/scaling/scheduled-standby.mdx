Use **scheduled standby** to raise standby during known peaks (weeknight prime time, event weekends) and lower it overnight.

Schedules attach to a specific build region and specify: recurrence, start/end times, standby target, max override.

## When to prefer scheduled over dynamic

- You have well-known peak windows.
- You need the higher capacity to be fully warm at the start of the peak, not ramped up as demand rises.
