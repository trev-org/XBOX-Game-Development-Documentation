Call [`UpdateBuildRegion`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/multiplayer/multiplayer-server/update-build-region) to change `StandbyServers` and `MaxServers` on the fly.

## Use cases

- Bring 500 extra standby servers online 10 minutes before an announced tournament.
- Pre-warm for a marketing push driven by an external campaign.
- Emergency capacity increase during a viral moment.

Wrap the call in an ops tool with least-privilege credentials — misconfigured maxes are the top cause of surprise bills.
