Scaling in Multiplayer Servers is a balance between **speed** (enough standby for instant allocation) and **cost** (standby VMs bill even when idle).

Three scaling modes stack:

- [**Scaling standby**](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/scaling/scaling-standby) — the base per-region standby count.
- [**Scheduled standby**](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/scaling/scheduled-standby) — time-based overrides (higher standby at peak hours).
- [**Dynamic standby**](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/scaling/dynamic-standby) — automatic adjustment based on live allocation rate.

Programmatic control via [`UpdateBuildRegion`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/multiplayer/multiplayer-server/update-build-region) is also available (see [Scaling programmatically](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/scaling/programmatic)).
