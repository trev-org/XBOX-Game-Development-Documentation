---

**Dynamic standby** auto-scales standby up when the allocation rate accelerates and back down when demand falls.

Configure per region:

- `MinStandbyServers` and `MaxStandbyServers`.
- `StandbyIncrementSize` — how many VMs to add per scale-up step.
- `RampDownSeconds` — cooldown before scaling down.

Combine with scheduled standby for the best of both: schedule the base for peak, let dynamic handle spikes on top.
