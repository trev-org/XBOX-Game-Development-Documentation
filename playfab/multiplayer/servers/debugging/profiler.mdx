---

Profilers give you tick-time distributions and CPU hotspots on live servers.

- **Windows** — ETW via `PerfView` or `wprui`; capture on the VM, then move the ETL back to your dev machine.
- **Linux** — `perf record` inside the container, or use `dotnet-trace` for .NET servers.
- Attach transiently — profiling adds overhead. Keep windows short (30–60 seconds).
