Debug your Multiplayer Servers build at four levels:

- **Local** — [LocalMultiplayerAgent](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/debugging/local-agent-overview) simulates the PlayFab agent on your dev box.
- **Direct debugging** — [attach a debugger](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/debugging/direct-debug) to a live server.
- **Profiling** — [attach a profiler](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/debugging/profiler) to a running instance.
- **Postmortem** — [retrieve logs](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/debugging/logs) and [crash dumps](/playfab/multiplayer/servers/debugging/crash-dumps).
