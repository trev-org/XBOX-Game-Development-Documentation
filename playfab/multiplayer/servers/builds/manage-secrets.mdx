description: "Store secrets used by your servers in PlayFab."
---

Store server secrets in **Multiplayer → Servers → Secrets**. Each secret has a name, value, and expiration. Reference secrets from a build's `GameSecrets` list; the agent mounts them into the server's environment.

Best practices:

- Rotate secrets before expiry.
- Never commit secrets to source control or bake them into asset zips.
- Prefer per-environment secrets (dev, staging, prod) over reused values.

Source: [`manage-secrets.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/servers/manage-secrets.md).
