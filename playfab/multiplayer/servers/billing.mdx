Charges are pay-as-you-go against the Azure subscription linked to your PlayFab title:

- **VM compute** — per second while VMs are `StandingBy`, `Active`, or `Terminating`.
- **Egress bandwidth** — outbound network from Azure to players.
- **Storage** — build assets, logs, crash dumps.

## Cost levers

- Right-size VM SKU.
- Trim standby to actual demand.
- Turn off dev/staging builds when not in use.
- Purge old build assets and log archives.
