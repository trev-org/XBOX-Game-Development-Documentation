---
title: "Matchmaking usage and billing"
sidebarTitle: "Usage & billing"
description: "How matchmaking usage is measured and billed."
---

Matchmaking is billed on **matched player minutes** — the number of players in successfully matched sessions multiplied by session duration reported by your server.

- Failed / expired tickets are not billed.
- Backfill tickets bill on the joining player, not the whole match.
- Peer-to-peer matches without dedicated servers still bill; report session end via the matchmaking API to close the meter.

Source: [`usage.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/matchmaking/usage.md).
