title: "Peer-to-peer matchmaking"
sidebarTitle: "Peer-to-peer"
description: "Matchmaking without dedicated servers."
---

Without dedicated servers, one matched client becomes the **host** and the others connect to them directly — typically via [PlayFab Party](/playfab/multiplayer/networking/overview) as the transport.

Flow:

1. Matchmaking assembles the match.
2. The queue's config picks a host member (e.g. lowest ping to the group).
3. Clients read the host id from `GetMatch`.
4. Host establishes a Party network; other members join.

Source: [`peer-to-peer.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/matchmaking/peer-to-peer.md).
