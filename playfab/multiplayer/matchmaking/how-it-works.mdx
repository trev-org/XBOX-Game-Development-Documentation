Internally, matchmaking runs a continuous loop per queue:

1. Tickets arrive and sit in the queue's pool.
2. The matcher periodically evaluates pool combinations against the queue's rules.
3. A candidate group that satisfies all rules and hits the target size becomes a match.
4. Waiting tickets can **expand** their rules over time (widen skill window, accept higher ping) if configured.
5. Tickets that expire without matching return a canceled status.
