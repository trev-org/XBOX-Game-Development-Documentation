Party emits diagnostic logs through a delegate you register with `PartyManager::SetProfilingEventsForCallbacks` / debug logging hooks. Route logs to your telemetry pipeline; sample rate can be tuned per event category.

Source: [`party-logging.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/party-logging.md).
