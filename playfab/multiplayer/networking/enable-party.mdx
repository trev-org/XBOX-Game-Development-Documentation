---
title: "Enable Party"
sidebarTitle: "Enable Party"
description: "Enable the Party service."
---

In Game Manager → your title → **Multiplayer** → **Party**, click **Enable Party**. This provisions the per-title Party resource; usage bills to the linked Azure subscription.

Source: [`enable-party.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/enable-party.md).
