**PlayFab Party** is the data plane for real-time multiplayer: reliable and unreliable messaging, voice chat with accessibility features (speech-to-text, text-to-speech), and NAT traversal.

## Highlights

- **Cross-platform** — Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android, Linux.
- **Voice \+ data** — opinionated audio pipeline with per-chat-control permissions and text/speech translation.
- **Networking model** — endpoints, chat controls, and networks; endpoints belong to devices and can send/receive.
