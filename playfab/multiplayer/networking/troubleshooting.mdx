See source: [`troubleshooting.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/troubleshooting.md). Recurring issues:

- NAT traversal failures — verify [port usage](/playfab/multiplayer/networking/concepts/port-usage).
- Audio dropouts — check region selection and platform-specific requirements.
- Missed callbacks — confirm you pump `PartyManager::StartProcessingStateChanges` every frame.
