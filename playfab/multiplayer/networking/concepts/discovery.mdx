Discovery uses a shared **network descriptor** — an opaque token distributed via your own signaling (Lobby, MPSD, invites). Any device with the descriptor can connect.

Source: [`concepts-discovery.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/concepts-discovery.md).
