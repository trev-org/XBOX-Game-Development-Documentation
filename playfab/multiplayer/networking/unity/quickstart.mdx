1. Import the Party Unity package.
2. Add the `PlayFabMultiplayerManager` to your scene.
3. Call `SetLogLevel` and `EnableSpeechToText` as needed.
4. Create/join a network from your existing lobby code.

Source: [`party-unity-plugin-quickstart.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/party-unity-plugin-quickstart.md).
