title: "Party quickstart"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a Party network."
---

1. Add the Party SDK for your platform ([SDKs](/playfab/multiplayer/networking/party-sdks)).
2. `PartyManager::Initialize(titleId)`.
3. `CreateNewNetwork` — returns a network descriptor.
4. `ConnectToNetwork` on every device using the descriptor.
5. `CreateEndpoint` → `SendMessage` for data; `CreateChatControl` for voice.
6. On shutdown, `LeaveNetwork` and `Cleanup`.

Source: [`quickstart.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/quickstart.md).
