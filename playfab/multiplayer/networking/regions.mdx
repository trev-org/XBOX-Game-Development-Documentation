Party runs in most public Azure regions. Client SDKs pick the lowest-latency region by default, or you can override the region list per network.

Source: [`supported-azure-regions.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/networking/supported-azure-regions.md).
