Party SDKs ship for Windows (desktop and GDK), Xbox (GDK), PlayStation 4/5, Nintendo Switch, iOS, macOS, Android, and Linux. Access to console SDKs requires the appropriate platform partnership.

See also: [Party for Unity](/playfab/multiplayer/networking/unity/overview) and [Party for Unreal](/playfab/multiplayer/networking/unreal/overview).
