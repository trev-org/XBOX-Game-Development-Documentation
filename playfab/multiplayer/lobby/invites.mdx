title: "Lobby invites"
sidebarTitle: "Invites"
description: "Send targeted invitations to specific players."
---

An **invite** attaches a join permission for a specific player. The invitee sees an invite prompt (delivered via [Real-time messages](/playfab/multiplayer/real-time-messages/overview)) and can accept or decline.

Owner-only actions by default; you can grant invite privileges to other members. Cancel a pending invite before it's accepted.

Source: [`lobby-invites.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/lobby-invites.md).
