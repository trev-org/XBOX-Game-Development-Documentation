---
title: "Getting started with Lobby"
sidebarTitle: "Getting started"
description: "First-lobby walkthrough."
---

1. Include the PlayFab Multiplayer SDK in your client.
2. Sign a player in with PlayFab.
3. Call `PFLobbyCreateAndJoinLobby` — the calling player becomes owner.
4. Read the lobby id back and share it via [invites](/playfab/multiplayer/lobby/invites) or [search keywords](/playfab/multiplayer/lobby/search-keywords).
5. Poll or subscribe to lobby change events; render membership in your lobby UI.

Source: [`lobby-getting-started.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/lobby-getting-started.md).
