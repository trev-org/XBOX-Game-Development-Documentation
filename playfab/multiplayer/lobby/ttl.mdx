---
title: "Lobby time to live"
sidebarTitle: "TTL"
description: "Lifetime of empty lobbies."
---

Lobbies have a service-side **TTL**. Once a lobby has no members, the service starts a countdown; if nobody joins before the TTL expires, the lobby is deleted.

Adjust the default at the title level, or override per lobby on creation for special cases (e.g. lobbies that need to persist while the owner reconnects).

Source: [`lobby-ttl.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/lobby-ttl.md).
