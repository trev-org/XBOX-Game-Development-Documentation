**PlayFab Lobby** is where players group up before a match: friends invite each other, adjust settings, and browse open lobbies.

## Concepts

- **Lobby** — named session with an owner, members, and property bag.
- **Owner** — the player who can change lobby settings; ownership can migrate.
- **Search key** — indexed lobby property used for public discovery.
- **Invite** — targeted join permission for a specific player.
- **TTL** — lifetime after which an empty lobby is deleted.
