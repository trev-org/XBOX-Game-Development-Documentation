title: "Owner privileges"
sidebarTitle: "Owner privileges"
description: "Owner-only actions."
---

Owner-only actions by default:

- Update lobby properties.
- Change `MaxMembers`, access policy, or search data.
- Send lobby invites (delegatable).
- Kick members.
- Migrate ownership.
- Delete the lobby.

Some privileges can be delegated to other members via lobby settings.

Source: [`owner-privileges.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/owner-privileges.md).
