---

Call [`FindLobbies`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/lobby/lobby/find-lobbies) with an OData-style filter over your `SearchData` keys.

Common filters:

- `string_key1 eq 'ranked'` — exact match on game mode.
- `number_key1 lt 300` — numeric range on ping estimate.
- `member_count_remaining gt 0` — skip full lobbies.

Page through results with a continuation token.

Source: [`find-lobbies.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/find-lobbies.md).
