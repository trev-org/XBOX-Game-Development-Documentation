title: "Create a lobby"
sidebarTitle: "Create"
description: "Create a lobby and become its owner."
---

API: `PFLobbyCreateAndJoinLobby` (client SDK) or [`CreateLobby`](https://learn.microsoft.com/rest/api/playfab/lobby/lobby/create-lobby) (REST).

Specify:

- **MaxPlayers** — hard cap.
- **AccessPolicy** — `Public`, `Friends`, `Private`.
- **LobbyProperties** and **MemberProperties** — initial payloads.
- **SearchData** — indexed keys other players will query on.

The caller becomes the owner and receives the `LobbyId` and `ConnectionString` for shareable invites.

Source: [`create-a-lobby.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/create-a-lobby.md).
