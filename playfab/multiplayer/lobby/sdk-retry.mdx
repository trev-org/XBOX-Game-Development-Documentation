title: "Client SDK: retry behavior"
sidebarTitle: "Retry behavior"
description: "SDK retry and backoff."
---

The multiplayer SDK auto-retries transient failures (throttling, network blips) with exponential backoff. For non-transient failures it surfaces the error immediately.

Guidance:

- Don't wrap every call with your own retry loop — you'll compound backoff.
- Do retry on `S_OK` failures reported by your own gameplay logic (e.g. a bad user input).
- Cancel operations that no longer matter (player left the lobby screen).
