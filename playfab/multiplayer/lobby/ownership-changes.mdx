When the owner leaves or disconnects, ownership can migrate based on the lobby's **owner migration policy**:

- **Automatic** — the service picks a new owner from remaining members.
- **Manual** — an operation from another member claims ownership.
- **Server** — an authorized game-server entity holds ownership.

All members receive a change event with the new owner id.
