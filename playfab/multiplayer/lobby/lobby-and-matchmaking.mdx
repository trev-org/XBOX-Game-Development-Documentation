Lobby and Matchmaking pair naturally:

- Friends group in a **lobby**.
- The lobby owner submits a **matchmaking ticket** on behalf of the whole group.
- On match, everyone in the lobby joins the same session together.

The multiplayer client SDK exposes helpers that combine lobby state with matchmaking so you don’t have to hand-roll it.
