title: "Define search keywords"
sidebarTitle: "Search keywords"
description: "Declare which lobby properties are indexed."
---

Only indexed properties can appear in `FindLobbies` filters. Declare them per title:

- **String search keys** — `string_key1..N`.
- **Number search keys** — `number_key1..N`.
- **System keys** — `member_count`, `owner_migration`, `lobby_type`.

Assign business meaning per key in your client (e.g. `string_key1 = mode`, `number_key1 = ping_estimate`).
