---

Join methods:

- **By lobby id** — `PFLobbyJoinLobby(lobbyId)`.
- **By connection string** — platform-shared token that identifies the lobby.
- **By invite** — targeted; the invitee doesn't need to know the id.

Before joining, the client can pre-set its member properties via a join payload. On success, the client subscribes to the lobby's change stream.

Source: [`join-lobbies.md`](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/playfab-docs/blob/main/playfab-docs/multiplayer/lobby/join-lobbies.md).
