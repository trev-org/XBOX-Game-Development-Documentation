PlayFab uses consumption-based pricing. You pay only for what your live titles use. Titles in Development Mode or Foundation Mode don't count against paid usage.

For current rates, see the [PlayFab pricing page](https://www.playfab.com/pricing).

## How you're billed

- **Base rate** — a monthly fee tied to your account plan (Standard, Premium, or Enterprise). Includes a bundle of meter usage.
- **Pay-as-you-go** — per-meter charges for usage above the included amounts.
- **Additional charges** — Multiplayer Servers, Party, and Add-ons billed separately.
