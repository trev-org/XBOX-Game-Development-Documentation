The **Billing Summary** page in Game Manager shows a daily estimate of your month-to-date charges. Use it to spot which meters drive your costs and to compare month over month.

<Note>
  Values on the Billing Summary are estimates. Some meters have a 48\+ hour delay, and Add-ons are billed separately. Final invoices may differ.
</Note>

You need **Admin** or **Billing** permissions to view it.

## Open the Billing Summary

1. Go to the **My Studios and Titles** page.
2. Select the ellipsis (`...`) next to your studio.
3. Choose **Billing summary**.

## What's on the page

- **Month-to-date estimated charge** — daily snapshot of the current calendar month.
- **Title filter** — see consumption and cost per title. Development Mode titles show usage but no cost.
- **Month filter** — switch to any past month since your account moved to usage-based pricing.
- **Meter breakdown** — usage, rate, and cost per meter, grouped by category.
- **CSV download** — export summary or daily breakdowns.

<Note>
  Storage meters bill on average consumption, not totals. In the daily CSV, the first row is the month-to-date average; other rows are intentionally blank.
</Note>

## Understanding the base rate

The **base rate** is the monthly fee for paid account plans. It includes:

- A bundle of **included meter usage**. Usage across live titles above this bundle is billed as **pay-as-you-go**.
- **Technical support** based on your [account plan](/playfab/pricing/account-upgrades).

Titles in [Development Mode](/playfab/pricing/development-mode) don't count against your included usage.

## Additional charges

Some costs sit outside the standard meter bundle:

- **Multiplayer Servers (MPS)** — billed per VM hour and network egress.
- **Party** — billed per player-minute and network egress.
- **Add-ons** — Community Sift, Snowflake, Photon, and others.

For the current rates, see the [PlayFab pricing page](https://www.playfab.com/pricing).
