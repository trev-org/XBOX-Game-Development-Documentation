Every title has one of two modes:

- **Development Mode** — free to use up to the [included limits](/playfab/pricing/development-mode). Capped at 1,000 unique players.
- **Live** — metered. You're billed per your account plan's base rate plus overage.

<Warning>
  Launching a title is **permanent**. You can't return to Development Mode after launching.
</Warning>

A title can only go live if its studio is on a **paid** account plan.

## Launch a title (paid plan)

1. Sign in to [developer.playfab.com](https://developer.playfab.com/).
2. Open **My Studios** and find the Development Mode title.
3. Select the down arrow to open the drop-down menu.
4. Select **Launch Title**.
5. Confirm in the pop-up.

Once launched, the title is no longer bound by Development Mode limits.

## Launch a title (Free to Start)

You can't launch on Free to Start. Selecting **Launch Title** starts the [account upgrade flow](/playfab/pricing/account-upgrades). After the upgrade completes, launch the title as above.

## FAQ

**What happens if I ship my game while the title is still in Development Mode?**

New player accounts fail to create once you hit 1,000 unique users. Existing players can still play, but growth stops until you launch.

**Do I need to switch to Live at launch on Free to Start?**

Yes. Free to Start has no **Launch** option — you must first upgrade to Pay-as-you-go or higher.
