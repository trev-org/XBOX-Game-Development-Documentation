Upgrades happen at the **account** level. Launches happen at the **title** level. An account is linked to one studio, and a studio can hold many titles — changing your plan affects every studio on the account.

## Account plans

| Plan | Monthly base rate | Included meter usage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free to Start** | None | Development Mode limits only | Must upgrade to launch a title |
| **Pay-as-you-go** | None | None | Charged only for live-title consumption |
| **Standard** | \$99 | Included bundle | Private support tickets |
| **Premium** | \$1,999 | Larger bundle | Private support, faster response |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Largest bundle | 24/7 support, contact PlayFab |

Base-rate meter usage is cumulative across all titles linked to the account.

All plans follow the [PlayFab terms of service](https://playfab.com/terms/).

## Change your plan

Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing period.

### From Free to Start

1. Sign in to [developer.playfab.com](http://developer.playfab.com/).
2. On **My Studios and Titles**, find your studio and select **Upgrade Account**.
3. On the **Plan Recommendation** page, review your 30-day usage and estimated cost per plan. Select **Proceed with plan**.
4. On **Plan Selection**, pick your plan and optionally launch any Development Mode titles. Select **Next**.
5. Enter contact and payment info, then select **Confirm this Plan** on the **Review** page.

After upgrading, the **Studio Settings** page shows your current plan and any scheduled change.

### Between paid plans

1. On **My Studios and Titles**, open the ellipsis menu (`...`) and choose **Studio Settings**.
2. Select **Change Plan** and follow the same recommendation → selection → review flow.

### Upgrade to Enterprise

Enterprise plans require [contacting the PlayFab team](https://playfab.com/contact/).

## Cancel charges and delete your studio

Deleting a studio is **permanent** — all titles are removed, developer access is revoked, and data can't be recovered.

Before you delete:

- Back up critical data.
- Download billing reports for your records.
- Notify your team.

To cancel all charges, request deletion **at least 10 calendar days before the end of the month**:

1. On **My Studios and Titles**, open the studio's ellipsis menu and choose **Studio Settings**.
2. Select **Delete Studio** at the top right.
3. Confirm cancellation.

Only studio admins can delete. You'll be billed for usage up to the cancellation and receive a final invoice on your regular due date. Plan charges aren't prorated.

Deleting a studio doesn't delete your PlayFab developer identity. As an alternative, you can delete all titles and change the plan to Pay-as-you-go to stop charges.

## FAQ

**How do I know which plan I'm on?**

On **My Studios**, look for the **Upgrade Account** button. If it's there, the studio is on Free to Start. If not, check the base rate on the **Billing Summary** page: `$99` = Standard, `$1,999` = Premium.

**Which plan do I get when I upgrade through Game Manager?**

Self-serve upgrades in Game Manager go to the **Standard** plan. To reach Premium, [contact support](/playfab/pricing/support).
