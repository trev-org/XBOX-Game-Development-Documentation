Economy v2 Catalog bills on two meters: **Requests** and **Storage**. Storage bills the average size of catalog data over the month.

## Meters

| Meter | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Economy v2 Catalog — Requests | Total Catalog API calls per month. |
| Economy v2 Catalog — Storage | Average catalog data size (bytes) over the month. |

## Included amounts

| Plan | Requests included | Storage included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 1M | 1 GB |
| Premium | 10M | 5 GB |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

## Example bill

A Standard-plan title using 2M requests and averaging 2 GB of catalog data in a month bills:

- 1M requests over the included amount — charged at the Catalog Requests overage rate.
- 1 GB storage over the included amount — charged at the Catalog Storage overage rate.

For current overage rates, see the [PlayFab pricing page](https://www.playfab.com/pricing).
