Content & Config reads cover title-level configuration, files, and CDN URL requests. Reads bill in 1 KB blocks based on response size.

## Client APIs

- `GetCatalogItems`
- `GetContentDownloadUrl`
- `GetPublisherData`
- `GetStoreItems`
- `GetTime`
- `GetTitleData`
- `GetTitleNews`
- `GetTitlePublicKey`
- `GetPushNotificationRegistrations`
