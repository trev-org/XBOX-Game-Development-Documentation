PlayFab Identity covers two separate systems:

- **Player identity** — how players sign in, link accounts, and stay connected across devices and platforms.
- **Developer identity** — how you and your team sign in to Game Manager and call PlayFab APIs.

## Player identity

One PlayFab account per player, no matter how they sign in. Players can link Google, Facebook, Apple, Steam, PlayStation™, Xbox, Nintendo, and more — you always work against a single PlayFab player identity.

## Developer identity

Sign in to Game Manager with a Microsoft account. Give teammates access with per-title roles and permissions.
