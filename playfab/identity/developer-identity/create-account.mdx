Before you call any PlayFab API, you need a PlayFab developer account.

## Sign up

1. Go to [developer.playfab.com](https://developer.playfab.com).
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account. All new PlayFab accounts require Microsoft authentication.
3. When you create a studio, PlayFab makes a placeholder title called **My Game**. Edit it any time from the **Create Title** screen.

<Note>
  Your title's **Title ID** is unique and is required in every PlayFab API call.
</Note>

## Add more titles

Create additional titles from the ellipsis menu (`...`) on **My Studios and Titles → New title**.

## Open Game Manager

Select a title from **My Studios and Titles** to enter Game Manager. Take a moment to explore — you'll spend most of your PlayFab time here.
