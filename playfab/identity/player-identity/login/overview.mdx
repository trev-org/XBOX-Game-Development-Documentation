Signing the player in is the first thing your game does. The login response returns a security token that every other PlayFab API call uses.

PlayFab supports both anonymous and recoverable sign-in.

## Anonymous sign-in

Zero player input. A device ID or custom ID creates a unique PlayFab account with no friction.

- Best first-run experience for mobile games.
- Uniquely identifies a device, but the account is tied to that device.
- If the player loses the device, the account is usually lost too.

## Recoverable sign-in

Requires identity information — a username/email \+ password, or authentication with a provider like Facebook, Apple, Google, Xbox, Steam, PlayStation™, or Nintendo.

- Player can sign in on a new device.
- Recommended before shipping.

"PlayStation" is a registered trademark or trademark of Sony Interactive Entertainment Inc.

## Recommended pattern

1. Start players with an anonymous login — low friction, fast start.
2. After a tutorial or paid purchase, prompt them to link a recoverable provider.
3. Keep the anonymous login as the default; use the recoverable login only for cross-device play or recovery.
