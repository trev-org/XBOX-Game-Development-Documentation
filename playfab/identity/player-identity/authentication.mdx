PlayFab authenticates players using many providers. Give players multiple ways to sign in and let them link their favorite accounts for account recovery and cross-device play.

PlayFab also supports Microsoft-backed sign-in for Game Manager — covered in [Developer identity](/playfab/identity/developer-identity/create-account).

## Sign-in categories

- **Anonymous logins** — zero player friction. Device IDs or custom IDs create an account without any input.
- **Recoverable logins** — the player provides identity data (email, Facebook, Google, Steam, Xbox, and so on). The account can be recovered on a new device.
- **Encrypted logins** — optional extra layer that protects login payloads with a title public key and shared secret.
